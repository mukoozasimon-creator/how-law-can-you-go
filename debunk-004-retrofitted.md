<div class="quip">This one is actually true, and the sentence is better than the myth.</div>

<div class="settled">
<b>What the statute says</b>
<p>The Metropolitan Police Act 1839, section 54, makes it an offence to carry a plank (or ladder, or pole, or other bulky article) along a pavement "in such manner as to inconvenience or obstruct the passengers."</p>
<p>The UK Law Commission confirmed this in <em>Legal Curiosities: Fact or Fable?</em> (2013). It is one of the rare weird laws that is both real and still technically in force, though enforced about as often as you would expect.</p>
