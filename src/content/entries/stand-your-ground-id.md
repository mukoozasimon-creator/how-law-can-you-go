## The Short Answer

Idaho is a **stand-your-ground state**. You have **no duty to retreat** before using force in self-defense anywhere you have a right to be (Idaho Code 19-202A(3)). Idaho's **Castle Doctrine** (Idaho Code 18-4009(2)) justifies the use of deadly force in defense of your habitation against someone who manifestly intends to commit a felony or enter violently. You may use deadly force to prevent imminent death, serious bodily injury, or the commission of a forcible felony.

## Key Statutes

### Idaho Code 19-202A - Defense of Self, Others and Certain Places

> "(3) In the exercise of the right of self-defense or defense of another, a person need not retreat from any place that person has a right to be. A person may stand his ground and defend himself or another person by the use of all force and means which would appear to be necessary to a reasonable person in a similar situation and with similar knowledge without the benefit of hindsight."

**Live link:** https://legislature.idaho.gov/statutesrules/idstat/title19/t19ch2/sect19-202a/
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** Explicit statutory stand-your-ground. No duty to retreat from any place you have a right to be.

### Idaho Code 18-4009 - Justifiable Homicide by Officer or Party Acting in Aid

> "(2) The killing of a human being is justifiable when committed in the lawful defense of habitation, property or person, against one who manifestly intends or endeavors, by violence or surprise, to commit a felony, or against one who manifestly intends and endeavors, in a violent, riotous or tumultuous manner, to enter the habitation of another for the purpose of offering violence to any person therein."

**Live link:** https://legislature.idaho.gov/statutesrules/idstat/title18/t18ch40/sect18-4009/
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** Castle Doctrine applies to habitation. Deadly force justified against violent/riotous entry with intent to assault.

### Idaho Code 19-202A(1) - No Legal Jeopardy for Self-Defense

> "No person in this state shall be placed in legal jeopardy of any kind whatsoever for protecting himself or his family by reasonable means necessary, or when coming to the aid of another whom he reasonably believes to be in imminent danger of or the victim of aggravated assault, robbery, rape, murder or other heinous crime."

**Live link:** https://legislature.idaho.gov/statutesrules/idstat/title19/t19ch2/sect19-202a/
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** Strong statutory protection against legal jeopardy for justified self-defense.

## What This Means for You

| Situation | Duty to Retreat? | Deadly Force Allowed? |
|-----------|---------------|---------------------|
| In your habitation (violent intruder) | No | Yes, under Castle Doctrine |
| In public (lawfully present) | No | Yes, if imminent deadly threat |
| Defending another | No | Yes, same standard |
| Pursuit of attacker | Yes | Allowed until danger no longer exists |

## Important Notes

- **No duty to retreat:** Idaho explicitly states that a person need not retreat from any place they have a right to be.
- **Pursuit allowed:** Idaho's stand-your-ground law uniquely allows pursuit of the attacker until there is no longer any danger, if pursuit is reasonably necessary.
- **Castle Doctrine limited to habitation:** The statutory Castle Doctrine applies to your habitation. Deadly force is justified against violent/riotous entry.
- **No legal jeopardy:** Idaho Code 19-202A(1) provides strong protection against any legal jeopardy for justified self-defense.
- **Initial aggressor exception:** You cannot claim self-defense if you were the initial aggressor.
- **Proportionality:** Force must be proportional to the threat.
- **Recording:** Idaho is a one-party consent state for recordings (Idaho Code 18-6702). You may record conversations you are a party to without the other person's consent.

## Cross-State Notes

- **vs. Montana:** Both are stand-your-ground states in the Mountain West.
