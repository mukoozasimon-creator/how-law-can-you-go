## The Short Answer

In Michigan, your landlord must give you a **7-day written notice** to pay rent or quit for nonpayment, and a **30-day written notice** for lease violations or to terminate a month-to-month tenancy. Michigan has no just-cause requirement, no rent control (statewide preemption), and security deposits are capped at **1.5 months' rent** with required move-in/move-out inventory checklists. Self-help eviction is illegal, and tenants have repair rights under the implied warranty of habitability.

### Michigan Compiled Laws Section 554.602 - Security Deposit Cap

**Key rules:**
- Maximum security deposit: **1.5 months' rent**
- Must be held in **regulated financial institution** (bank or credit union)
- Required **move-in and move-out inventory checklists** with both parties signing
- Return deadline: **30 days** after move-out with itemized statement
- Wrongful withholding: **2x the deposit** plus attorney's fees

**Live link:** https://codes.findlaw.com/mi/chapter-554-landlord-and-tenant/mi-st-sect-554-602/
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 1.5-month cap. Inventory checklists required. 30-day return. Double damages for wrongful withholding.

### Michigan Compiled Laws Section 554.607 - Truth in Renting Act

> "A landlord shall not include a provision in a rental agreement that would do any of the following: (a) Authorize the landlord to terminate the tenancy without cause. (b) Authorize the landlord to recover possession of the premises without resorting to legal process. (c) Authorize the landlord to confess judgment against the tenant." cite🛠web_search:3#1:~:text=Self-help evictions - lockouts, utility shutoffs, seizing belongings - are illegal; only a court can remove a tenant - § 554.607.

**Live link:** https://codes.findlaw.com/mi/chapter-554-landlord-and-tenant/mi-st-sect-554-607/
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** Self-help eviction is illegal. Lease clauses authorizing self-help are void. Only court order can remove tenant.

### Michigan Compiled Laws Section 554.139 - Warranty of Habitability

> "In every lease or license of residential premises, the landlord covenants that the premises and all common areas are fit for the use intended by the parties and will be kept in reasonable repair during the term of the lease or license." cite🛠web_search:3#1:~:text=In every lease or license of residential premises, the landlord covenants that the premises and all common areas are fit for the use intended by the parties and will be kept in reasonable repair during the term of the lease or license.

## Important Notes

- **No just-cause requirement:** Michigan landlords can terminate month-to-month tenancies for any reason with proper notice.
- **No rent control:** Michigan preempts local rent control ordinances.
- **Inventory checklists:** The move-in/move-out inventory checklist requirement is strict. Failure to provide and complete checklists can result in forfeiture of deposit deductions.
- **Security deposit interest:** Not required by state law, but some local ordinances may require it.
- **Eviction timeline:** Uncontested evictions typically take 4-6 weeks from filing to lockout. Tenant has 10 days to answer after service.
- **Recording:** Michigan is a one-party consent state for recordings (MCL 750.539c). You may record conversations you are a party to without the other person's consent.

## Cross-State Notes

- **vs. Ohio:** Both have similar 3-day/7-day notice frameworks, but Michigan has a stricter security deposit cap (1.5 months vs. no cap in Ohio) and required inventory checklists.
- **vs. Pennsylvania:** Michigan has shorter eviction notice (7 days vs. 10 days for nonpayment) but stricter deposit rules with required checklists.
- **vs. California:** Michigan is significantly more landlord-friendly with no just-cause requirement and no rent control.
