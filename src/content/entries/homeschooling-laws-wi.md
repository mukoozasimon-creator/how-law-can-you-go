## Summary

Wisconsin homeschools operate as "home-based private educational programs" under Wis. Stat. § 118.165. Parents must file an annual PI-1206 Homeschool Enrollment Report online by October 15, provide at least 875 hours of instruction per year (July 1 - June 30), and teach a sequentially progressive curriculum in 6 required subjects. Wisconsin has no parent qualification requirements, no standardized testing, and no curriculum approval. The PI-1206 form is essentially a census - parents affirm compliance without submitting proof. Wisconsin law also guarantees homeschoolers the right to participate in public school athletics.

## Legal Framework

### Primary Statute

**Wis. Stat. § 118.165** - Private schools defined

> "An institution is a private school if its educational program meets all of the following criteria: (a) The primary purpose of the program is to provide private or religious-based education. (b) The program is privately controlled. (c) The program provides at least 875 hours of instruction each school year. (d) The program provides a sequentially progressive curriculum of fundamental instruction in reading, language arts, mathematics, social studies, science and health."

- **Instrument**: Wis. Stat. § 118.165
- **Live Link**: https://docs.legis.wisconsin.gov/statutes/statutes/118/165
- **Repeal Check**: Active as of 2026; no repeal legislation pending
- **Verdict**: Home-based private educational programs are recognized as private schools with specific hour and subject requirements.

### Enrollment Reporting Statute

**Wis. Stat. § 115.30(3)** - Reports to the department

> "On or before each October 15, each administrator of a public or private school system or a home-based private educational program shall submit, on forms provided by the department, a statement of the enrollment on the 3rd Friday in September... The administrator of each private school system and home-based private educational program shall indicate in his or her report whether the system or program meets all of the criteria under s. 118.165 (1)."

- **Instrument**: Wis. Stat. § 115.30(3)
- **Live Link**: https://docs.legis.wisconsin.gov/statutes/statutes/115/30
- **Repeal Check**: Active as of 2026
- **Verdict**: Annual PI-1206 report is required by October 15.

## Notification

**Annual PI-1206 Homeschool Enrollment Report** must be filed online at the Wisconsin Department of Public Instruction website by **October 15** each year. The form:

- Reports enrollment as of the 3rd Friday in September
- Includes a legal affirmation that the parent is in compliance with Wisconsin homeschooling law
- Is essentially a census - no proof of compliance is submitted

**Paper submissions are no longer accepted** - online filing only.

**Mid-Year Start**: If beginning to homeschool after October 15, file the PI-1206 immediately before beginning.

## Required Subjects (6)

Under Wis. Stat. § 118.165(d), the curriculum must provide fundamental instruction in:

1. Reading
2. Language Arts
3. Mathematics
4. Social Studies
5. Science
6. Health

The curriculum must be "sequentially progressive" (moving from simpler to more complex concepts). Wisconsin does not approve or review curriculum selections.

**Note**: Art, music, and foreign language are optional.

## Assessment & Testing

No standardized testing or assessments are required.

## Record-Keeping

No records are required by law. However, keeping documentation of hours and subjects is recommended in case questions arise.

## Special Provisions

- **Sports Access**: Wisconsin law guarantees homeschoolers the right to participate in public school interscholastic athletics and extracurricular activities
- **Single Family Unit**: An instructional program provided to more than one family unit does not constitute a home-based private educational program (Wis. Stat. § 115.001(3g))
- **Truancy**: A child receiving instruction in a homeschool has an excused absence from public school (Wis. Stat. § 118.15(4))
- **Transfer Students**: When transferring to public school, the superintendent may administer a standardized achievement test and determine appropriate grade/course placement
- **No Penalty for Non-Filing**: While failure to file the PI-1206 means the child is not legally excused from compulsory attendance, the truancy process has substantial procedural requirements before penalties apply

## Distinctive Features

- Annual PI-1206 form is a simple online census (no proof submitted)
- 875-hour annual requirement (reasonable for most families)
- 6 required subjects with "sequentially progressive" standard
- **Statutory guarantee of sports access** - homeschoolers can participate in public school athletics
- No parent qualifications, no testing, no curriculum approval
- Single-family-unit restriction (cannot co-op with other families as the "homeschool")
- Strong truancy procedural protections
