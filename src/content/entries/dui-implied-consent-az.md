<div class="quip">Arizona: where the desert is hot and your license is suspended for a year if you refuse the chemical test.</div>

<div class="settled">
<b>The stuff that is actually true in Arizona</b>
<ul>
<li><strong>You already consented to chemical testing.</strong> A.R.S. 28-1321. By driving in Arizona, you are deemed to have consented to blood, breath, urine, or other bodily substance testing if lawfully arrested for DUI. The law enforcement agency chooses which test.</li>
<li><strong>First refusal = 12-month suspension. Second = 2 years.</strong> A.R.S. 28-1321(B). First refusal = 12 months. Second refusal within 84 months = 2 years. Administrative suspension by MVD.</li>
<li><strong>Unconscious = no right to refuse.</strong> A.R.S. 28-1321(D). If you are unconscious or incapable of refusal, you are deemed not to have withdrawn consent. Blood can be drawn without a warrant.</li>
<li><strong>Officer can get a warrant after refusal.</strong> A.R.S. 28-1321. Even if you refuse, the officer can obtain a search warrant for a blood draw. The refusal suspension still applies even if blood is obtained by warrant.</li>
<li><strong>You can record the stop.</strong> Arizona is a one-party consent state. You may record your own interactions with police without informing them.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="varies">
<b>The grey area where people get themselves arrested</b>
<ul>
<li><strong>"I want to speak to an attorney first."</strong> You have no right to an attorney before deciding whether to submit to chemical testing. The officer does not have to wait.</li>
<li><strong>"I'll take a blood test instead of breath."</strong> The agency chooses the test, not you. You can request blood, but the agency is not required to honor it.</li>
<li><strong>"The officer didn't inform me of the consequences."</strong> The officer must inform you that refusal will result in suspension. Failure to give proper warning can invalidate the refusal.</li>
<li><strong>"I wasn't driving, I was sleeping in the car."</strong> Arizona uses 'actual physical control' standard. If you have the keys and the ability to operate the vehicle, you can be arrested for DUI.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="wrongbox">
<b>Things TikTok told you that are wrong</b>
<ul>
<li>"If you refuse, they have no case." Wrong. Refusal triggers automatic suspension AND can be used as evidence of guilt. Plus the officer can get a warrant for your blood.</li>
<li>"They can't take blood without a warrant." Wrong. If you are unconscious or incapable of refusal, blood can be drawn without a warrant.</li>
<li>"The suspension only applies if you're convicted." Wrong. The suspension is administrative and happens regardless of the criminal case outcome.</li>
<li>"You have 30 days to request a hearing." Wrong. You have 30 days from the notice date. The suspension is effective 30 days after the notice is served.</li>
</ul>
</div>
