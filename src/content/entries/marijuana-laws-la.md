# Marijuana Laws - Louisiana

## Verdict

Louisiana permits medical cannabis only. Recreational marijuana remains prohibited. The state decriminalized possession of up to 14 grams in 2021 (reduced to $100 fine), but the legislature reversed course in 2026 with a recriminalization bill signed by the governor. Medical patients may purchase from licensed therapeutic marijuana pharmacies (retailers). Home cultivation is prohibited. Out-of-state patients with qualifying conditions are recognized under Act 438 (HB 135).

## Medical Program

- **Status:** Active since 2015
- **Regulator:** Louisiana Department of Health (LDH) since January 1, 2025
- **Qualifying conditions:** Cancer, HIV/AIDS, cachexia, seizure disorders, epilepsy, spasticity, Crohn's disease, muscular dystrophy, multiple sclerosis, PTSD, autism spectrum disorder, traumatic brain injury, chronic pain, and others
- **Physician requirements:** Must be licensed by Louisiana State Board of Medical Examiners with marijuana registration permit
- **Dispensaries:** 10 retail permits with up to 2 satellite locations each
- **Product forms:** Flower, edibles, topicals, metered-dose inhalers, tinctures
- **Tax:** 7% gross sales tax on therapeutic cannabis
- **Out-of-state patients:** Recognized under Act 438 (HB 135) with qualifying conditions

## Home Cultivation

- **Status:** Illegal for all purposes.
- Qualifying patients cannot cultivate their own marijuana under Louisiana law.

## DUI / Impairment

- Louisiana prohibits operating a motor vehicle while under the influence of cannabis.
- Act 478 (HB 234) specifically prohibits passengers and operators from smoking or vaping marijuana in vehicles on public highways.
- Penalties follow standard DUI statutes (La. R.S. 14:98).

## Employment Protections

- No comprehensive employment protections for medical cannabis patients.
- HB 351 (pending as of 2026) seeks to prohibit disqualification of medical patients from certain employee benefits.
- Employers may maintain drug-free workplace policies.

## Federal Conflict

- Cannabis remains Schedule I under federal law.
- Interstate transport is prohibited even for medical patients.
- Federal property in Louisiana prohibits cannabis possession.
