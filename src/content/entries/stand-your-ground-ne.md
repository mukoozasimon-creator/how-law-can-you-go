## The Short Answer

Nebraska is **NOT a stand-your-ground state**. You have a **duty to retreat** before using deadly force in public if you know you can do so with complete safety (Neb. Rev. Stat. 28-1409(4)(b)). Nebraska's **Castle Doctrine** (28-1409(4)(b)(i)) removes the duty to retreat inside your **dwelling** or **place of work** if you were not the initial aggressor. Deadly force is only justified when you reasonably believe it is necessary to prevent death, serious bodily harm, kidnapping, or sexual intercourse compelled by force or threat.

## Key Statutes

### Neb. Rev. Stat. 28-1409 - Use of Force in Self-Protection

> "(4) The use of deadly force shall not be justifiable under this section unless the actor believes that such force is necessary to protect himself against death, serious bodily harm, kidnapping or sexual intercourse compelled by force or threat, nor is it justifiable if: (b) The actor knows that he can avoid the necessity of using such force with complete safety by retreating or by surrendering possession of a thing to a person asserting a claim of right thereto or by complying with a demand that he abstain from any action which he has no duty to take, except that: (i) The actor shall not be obliged to retreat from his dwelling or place of work, unless he was the initial aggressor or is assailed in his place of work by another person whose place of work the actor knows it to be..."

**Live link:** https://law.justia.com/codes/nebraska/chapter-28/statute-28-1409/
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** Duty to retreat before deadly force in public if safe retreat is known to be available. No duty to retreat in dwelling or place of work.

### Neb. Rev. Stat. 28-1410 - Use of Force for Protection of Other Persons

> "(2) The use of deadly force is not justifiable under this section unless the actor believes that such force is necessary to protect the other person against death, serious bodily harm, kidnapping or sexual intercourse compelled by force or threat, nor is it justifiable if: (a) The actor, with the purpose of causing death or serious bodily harm, provoked the use of force against himself in the same encounter; or (b) The actor knows that he can avoid the necessity of using such force with complete safety by retreating..."

**Live link:** https://law.justia.com/codes/nebraska/chapter-28/statute-28-1410/
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** Same duty to retreat applies when defending others.

## What This Means for You

| Situation | Duty to Retreat? | Deadly Force Allowed? |
|-----------|---------------|---------------------|
| In your dwelling (not initial aggressor) | No | Yes, if reasonable belief of imminent death/serious bodily harm |
| In your place of work (not initial aggressor) | No | Yes, same standard |
| In public | Yes, if safe retreat possible | Only if cannot retreat safely |
| Defending another | Yes, if safe retreat possible | Same standard |

## Important Notes

- **Duty to retreat in public:** Nebraska explicitly requires retreat before deadly force in public if safe retreat is available.
- **Castle Doctrine extends to workplace:** The no-duty-to-retreat exception applies to both your dwelling and your place of work.
- **Subjective knowledge standard:** The duty to retreat requires that you "know" you can retreat with complete safety. This is a subjective standard.
- **Initial aggressor exception:** You cannot claim self-defense if you were the initial aggressor.
- **Proportionality:** Force must be proportional to the threat.
- **No stand-your-ground legislation:** Nebraska has not enacted stand-your-ground legislation.
- **Recording:** Nebraska is a one-party consent state for recordings (Neb. Rev. Stat. 86-290). You may record conversations you are a party to without the other person's consent.

## Cross-State Notes

- **vs. Iowa:** IA is a stand-your-ground state; NE is not.
- **vs. Kansas:** KS is a stand-your-ground state; NE is not.
- **vs. South Dakota:** SD repealed pre-suit notice requirement but does not have SYG; NE has duty to retreat.
