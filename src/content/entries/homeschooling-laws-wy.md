# Homeschooling Laws - Wyoming

## Statutory Framework

Wyoming homeschools operate as **"home-based educational programs"** under **W.S. 21-4-102** and **W.S. 21-4-101(a)(v)-(vi)**. A homeschool is explicitly **not** considered a private school under Wyoming law.

### Primary Instrument: W.S. 21-4-102

> "Every parent, guardian or other person having control or charge of any child who is a resident of this state and whose seventh birthday falls on or before August 1 ... and who has not yet attained his sixteenth birthday or completed the tenth grade shall be required to ensure that the child is enrolled in a private school, public school, or home-based educational program."
> - Source: [Wyoming Department of Education - Homeschooling](https://edu.wyoming.gov/parents/homeschooling/) (2026-07-24)
> - Repeal check: Active as of 2026; no repeal pending.

### Definition: W.S. 21-4-101(a)(v)

> "A home-based education program means a program of educational instruction provided to a child by the child's parent or legal guardian or by a person designated by the parent or legal guardian. Instruction provided to more than one family unit does not constitute a home-based educational program."
> - Source: [U.S. Department of Education - Wyoming State Regulation](http://www.ed.gov/birth-grade-12-education/education-choice/state-regulation-of-private-and-home-schools/wyoming-state-regulation-of-private-and-home-schools) (Authority: S)

**Key limitation:** Instruction provided to more than one family unit does **not** constitute a home-based educational program. This restricts co-op arrangements.

### Curriculum Submission: W.S. 21-4-102(b)

> "It shall be the responsibility of every person administering a home-based educational program to submit a curriculum to the local board of trustees each year showing that the program complies with the requirements of the law. Failure to submit a curriculum showing compliance evidences that the home-based program does not comply with the requirements of the law."
> - Source: [U.S. Department of Education - Wyoming State Regulation](http://www.ed.gov/birth-grade-12-education/education-choice/state-regulation-of-private-and-home-schools/wyoming-state-regulation-of-private-and-home-schools) (Authority: S)

**Key details:**
- Annual curriculum submission to the **local board of trustees**
- The curriculum must show compliance with the "basic academic educational program" standard
- Failure to submit is evidence of noncompliance

### Basic Academic Educational Program: W.S. 21-4-101(a)(vi)

> "A basic academic educational program is one that provides a sequentially progressive curriculum of fundamental instruction in reading, writing, mathematics, civics, history, literature, and science."
> - Source: [SVHS - Homeschooling in Wyoming](https://svhs.co/homeschooling-wyoming-laws/) (2025-01-08)

**Religious protection:** "There is not a requirement for the inclusion of any concept, topic, or practice in conflict with the program's religious doctrines nor any prohibition against excluding from its curriculum any concept, topic, or practice consistent with its religious doctrines." W.S. 21-4-101(a)(vi).

### Notification Form

While the statute requires curriculum submission, the Wyoming Department of Education also provides a **Homeschool Notification Form** that parents may submit to the local school district. This is a practical step to prevent truancy confusion.

> "To start homeschooling in Wyoming, parents simply need to follow this legal step: Provide written notice of intent to homeschool by submitting a Homeschool Notification Form to the local school district."
> - Source: [SVHS - Homeschooling in Wyoming](https://svhs.co/homeschooling-wyoming-laws/)

### Teacher Qualifications

**None.** Wyoming statute does not require any degree, diploma, or teaching credential.

### Testing Requirements

**None.** Wyoming does not mandate standardized testing for homeschool students.

> "The state statute also does not require homeschoolers to participate in any standardized testing regimes beyond what parents choose for their own students. The only required assessment is the one-time notice indicating a family's intent to homeschool."
> - Source: [SVHS - Homeschooling in Wyoming](https://svhs.co/homeschooling-wyoming-laws/)

### Instructional Time

No minimum hours or days are specified in statute for homeschools.

### Compulsory Attendance Ages

Ages 7 (by August 1) through 16, or completion of 10th grade.

### Withdrawal Requirement: W.S. 21-4-102(c)

Parents must meet in person with a school district counselor or administrator to provide written consent to withdraw a child from school. The written consent must include a separate provision authorizing release of the student's identity and address to the Wyoming National Guard Youth Challenge Program for recruitment purposes.

> "The parent, guardian or other person having control or charge of any child under the age of eighteen (18), who has not otherwise notified the district of enrolling that child in a different school district or in a private school or home-based educational program, shall meet in person with a school district counselor or administrator to provide the school district with written consent to the withdrawal of that child from school attendance."
> - Source: [FindLaw - Wyoming Statutes 21-4-102](https://codes.findlaw.com/wy/title-21-education/wy-st-sect-21-4-102/) (Authority: A)

### Public School Access

Wyoming law guarantees homeschoolers access to extracurricular activities, including those sanctioned by the Wyoming High School Activities Association (WHSAA).

> "W.S. 21-4-506(a) states that any school age child who is a resident of a school district and who is not enrolled as a full-time student in the district in which they reside, shall be permitted by the district to participate in any activities which are sanctioned by the Wyoming High School Activities Association (WHSAA)."
> - Source: [School Choice Matters - Wyoming Policy Report](https://schoolchoicematters.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/SchoolChoice_StateReport_Wyoming.pdf)

### Distinctive Features

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Notification** | Annual curriculum submission to local board of trustees |
| **Testing** | None |
| **Qualifications** | None |
| **Required Subjects** | 7 subjects (reading, writing, math, civics, history, literature, science) |
| **Instructional Time** | None specified |
| **Single-Family Unit** | Instruction to more than one family unit does not qualify |
| **Religious Protection** | No requirement to include concepts conflicting with religious doctrines |
| **Sports Access** | Statutory guarantee for WHSAA-sanctioned activities |
| **Withdrawal** | In-person meeting + written consent required |

### Verdict

Wyoming is a **low-regulation** state with a unique structural feature: the annual curriculum submission to the local board of trustees. While this creates a compliance step absent in the most deregulated states, the absence of testing, qualifications, and hour requirements keeps the burden minimal. The single-family-unit restriction is notable for limiting co-op arrangements, and the religious protection clause is unusually explicit.
