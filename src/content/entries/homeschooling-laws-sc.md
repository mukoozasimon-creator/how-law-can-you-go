## Summary

South Carolina offers three distinct legal pathways for homeschooling, each created by a separate statute: Option 1 (School District Approval under § 59-65-40), Option 2 (SCAIHS Membership under § 59-65-45), and Option 3 (Homeschool Association under § 59-65-47). Option 3 is the most popular. All three options require parent notification, but the oversight body, testing requirements, and subject mandates differ significantly. South Carolina also guarantees homeschoolers the right to participate in public school interscholastic activities under the Equal Access Act.

## Legal Framework

### Option 1 - School District Approval

**S.C. Code § 59-65-40** - Home schooling programs

> "Parents or guardians may teach their children at home if the instruction is approved by the district board of trustees of the district in which the children reside. A district board of trustees shall approve home schooling programs which meet the following standards..."

- **Instrument**: S.C. Code § 59-65-40
- **Live Link**: https://www.scstatehouse.gov/code/t59c065.php
- **Repeal Check**: Active as of 2026; no repeal legislation pending
- **Verdict**: District approval required with specific standards including testing, subjects, and attendance.

### Option 2 - SCAIHS

**S.C. Code § 59-65-45** - Alternative home schooling requirements

> "In lieu of the requirements of Section 59-65-40, parents or guardians may teach their children at home if the instruction is conducted under the auspices of the South Carolina Association of Independent Home Schools."

- **Instrument**: S.C. Code § 59-65-45
- **Live Link**: https://www.scstatehouse.gov/code/t59c065.php
- **Repeal Check**: Active as of 2026
- **Verdict**: SCAIHS membership exempts families from § 59-65-40 requirements.

### Option 3 - Homeschool Association

**S.C. Code § 59-65-47** - Third option

> "In lieu of the requirements of Section 59-65-40 or Section 59-65-45, parents or guardians may teach their children at home if the instruction is conducted under the auspices of an association for homeschools which has no fewer than fifty members..."

- **Instrument**: S.C. Code § 59-65-47
- **Live Link**: https://www.scstatehouse.gov/code/t59c065.php
- **Repeal Check**: Active as of 2026
- **Verdict**: Association membership exempts families from § 59-65-40/45 requirements.

## Requirements by Option

| Feature | Option 1 (District) | Option 2 (SCAIHS) | Option 3 (Association) |
|---------|---------------------|-------------------|------------------------|
| **Notification** | File with district | SCAIHS notifies district | Association notifies district |
| **Parent Qualifications** | HS diploma or GED | HS diploma or GED | HS diploma or GED (or none if association allows) |
| **Required Subjects** | 6 subjects | 5 subjects | 5 subjects |
| **Testing** | Annual statewide testing | None | None |
| **Attendance** | 4.5 hrs/day, 180 days | 180 days | 180 days |
| **Records** | Submit to district | Maintain at home | Maintain at home |
| **Fees** | None | ~$50/year | Varies ($25-$200) |

### Required Subjects

All options require at minimum:
- Reading
- Writing
- Mathematics
- Science
- Social Studies
- Grades 7-12: Composition and Literature

Option 1 additionally requires access to library facilities.

### Parent Qualifications

Options 1 and 2 require the teaching parent to hold at least a high school diploma or GED. Option 3 associations set their own qualification standards; some associations do not require a diploma.

## Notification

- **Option 1**: Parent files directly with the school district. The district cannot deny the right to homeschool; notification is informational only.
- **Option 2**: SCAIHS handles district notification on the family's behalf.
- **Option 3**: The association handles district notification on the family's behalf.

## Assessment & Testing

- **Option 1**: Students must participate in the annual statewide testing program (BSAP or equivalent), administered by a certified school district employee. Parent pays test administrator if testing at home.
- **Option 2**: No testing required.
- **Option 3**: No testing required.

## Record-Keeping

- **Option 1**: Semiannual progress reports submitted to the district; attendance records; curriculum records
- **Option 2**: Records maintained at home per SCAIHS standards
- **Option 3**: Records maintained at home per association standards

## Special Provisions

- **Equal Access Act (S.C. Code § 59-65-47(E))**: Homeschool students are eligible for interscholastic activities (sports, clubs, academic competitions) at their resident district school. Must meet same academic and eligibility requirements as public school students.
- **LIFE Scholarship**: Available to homeschoolers who complete 14-unit college prep curriculum, score 1100+ SAT or 24+ ACT, and attend a SC public university
- **Special Education**: Homeschoolers can request evaluation through local school district; limited services may be available
- **First Grade Readiness**: Option 1 requires first-grade-eligible students to be tested for readiness within first 15 instructional days

## Distinctive Features

- **Three-path system** with dramatically different compliance burdens
- Option 3 (Association) is the most popular (~70% of homeschoolers)
- **Statutory right to public school sports** (Equal Access Act)
- Option 1 requires 4.5-hour daily minimum (specific hourly minimum)
- Option 1 requires annual statewide testing; Options 2/3 have none
- SCAIHS is a state-created organization with statutory authority
- Districts cannot deny the right to homeschool under any option
