# Marijuana Laws - Nebraska

## Verdict

Nebraska legalized medical cannabis via voter-approved ballot measures in November 2024. Measure 437 (71% approval) permits qualified patients to possess and use up to 5 ounces of medical cannabis with a healthcare practitioner's recommendation. Measure 438 (67% approval) creates the regulatory framework for distribution. As of 2026, the program is being implemented by the new Nebraska Medical Cannabis Commission, though legal challenges remain pending before the state Supreme Court. Recreational marijuana remains illegal. First-time possession of less than 1 ounce is decriminalized to a civil infraction ($300 fine). Home cultivation is not permitted.

## Named Instruments

- **Measure 437 (Nov 2024 ballot)** - Medical cannabis possession and use
- **Measure 438 (Nov 2024 ballot)** - Medical cannabis regulation and distribution
- **Neb. Rev. Stat. Section 28-416** - Possession penalties
- **Neb. Rev. Stat. Section 28-419** - Decriminalization (first offense <1 oz)

## Possession Limits

| Product Type | Legal Status | Limit |
|--------------|-------------|-------|
| Medical cannabis (patient) | Legal (Measure 437) | Up to 5 ounces (excluding weight of non-cannabis ingredients) |
| Recreational cannabis | Illegal | 0 |
| First offense <1 oz | Decriminalized (civil infraction) | Up to $300 fine |

## Penalty Tiers (Non-Medical Possession)

| Amount | Classification | Penalty |
|--------|---------------|---------|
| <1 oz (first offense) | Civil infraction | Up to $300 fine; no jail |
| <1 oz (subsequent) | Class IV misdemeanor | Up to 7 days jail; up to $500 fine |
| 1 oz - 1 lb | Class III misdemeanor | Up to 3 months jail; up to $500 fine |
| >1 lb | Class IV felony | Up to 2 years prison; up to $10,000 fine |
| Distribution | Felony | Enhanced penalties based on amount |

## Medical Program

- **Status:** Voter-approved November 2024; implementation underway as of 2026
- **Enabling law:** Measure 437 (possession/use) and Measure 438 (regulation/distribution)
- **Regulator:** Nebraska Medical Cannabis Commission (newly created)
- **Qualifying conditions:** Not fully specified in ballot text; practitioner discretion expected
- **Patient requirements:** Age 18+ (minors with parent/guardian consent), written recommendation from healthcare practitioner
- **Possession:** Up to 5 ounces (excluding weight of other ingredients in preparations)
- **Home grow:** Not allowed
- **Caregivers:** Permitted to assist patients with possession and acquisition
- **Business licensing:** Commission will license cultivation, manufacturing, distribution, and dispensing
- **Legal challenges:** Opponents filed suit; Lancaster County District Court upheld measures December 2024; case pending before Nebraska Supreme Court

## Home Cultivation

- **Status:** Illegal for all purposes.
- Neither patients nor caregivers may cultivate cannabis at home.

## DUI / Impairment

- Nebraska prohibits operating a motor vehicle under the influence of cannabis.
- No per se THC limit is established.
- Medical authorization is not a defense to DUI.

## Employment Protections

- No statutory employment protections for medical cannabis patients as of 2026.
- Employers may maintain drug-free workplace policies.
- Program rules may address employment protections during implementation.

## Federal Conflict

- Cannabis remains Schedule I under federal law.
- Interstate transport is prohibited.
- Federal property in Nebraska prohibits cannabis possession.
