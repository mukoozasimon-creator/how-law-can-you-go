## The Short Answer

In South Dakota, as of July 1, 2024, your landlord is **no longer required to give a separate pre-suit notice** before filing an eviction action (SDCL 21-16-1, as amended by SB 90). The landlord may proceed directly to filing a forcible entry and detainer (FED) action. However, landlords may still choose to give a **3-day notice** as a courtesy. To end a month-to-month tenancy, the landlord must give **30 days' written notice** (SDCL 43-32-13). South Dakota has no just-cause requirement and no rent control. Self-help eviction is illegal.

## Key Statutes

### SDCL 21-16-1 - Forcible Entry and Detainer (No Pre-Suit Notice Required)

> "An FED action is maintainable for: forcible entry; wrongful expulsion; unlawful holding by force or menace; a lessee holding over after lease end; failing to pay rent for three days after due; post-sale or post-judgment holdover; and lease-triggered waste or breach."

**Live link:** https://sdlegislature.gov/statutes/21-16-1
**Repeal check:** Active. Former SDCL 21-16-2 (notice to quit requirement) was repealed effective July 1, 2024.
**Verdict:** No separate statutory notice to quit is required before filing. Landlord may file FED action directly. This is a significant change from pre-2024 law.

### SDCL 21-16-6 - Verified Complaint and Summons

> "The complaint must be in writing, verified by the plaintiff or agent, and served together with a summons. The defendant has 5 days after service to appear and plead."

**Live link:** https://sdlegislature.gov/statutes/21-16-6
**Repeal check:** Active. Amended July 1, 2024 to extend response time from 4 days to 5 days.
**Verdict:** 5-day response window after service of summons and complaint.

### SDCL 43-32-13 - 30-Day Month-to-Month Termination

> "A month-to-month tenancy may be terminated by either party upon 30 days' written notice."

**Live link:** https://sdlegislature.gov/statutes/43-32-13
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 30 days written notice for month-to-month termination by either party.

### SDCL 43-32-6 - Security Deposits

**Key rules:**
- Maximum: **1 month's rent**.
- Return deadline: **2 weeks** (14 days) after lease termination with itemized statement.
- Must provide written notice of deposit terms.

**Live link:** https://sdlegislature.gov/statutes/43-32-6
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 14-day return. 1-month cap.

## What This Means for You

| Situation | Notice Required | Just Cause Required? |
|-----------|----------------|----------------------|
| Month-to-month termination | 30 days written | No |
| Nonpayment of rent | No pre-suit notice required | N/A |
| Lease violation | No pre-suit notice required | N/A |
| Holdover | No pre-suit notice required | N/A |

## Important Notes

- **No just-cause requirement:** South Dakota landlords can terminate month-to-month tenancies for any reason with proper notice.
- **No rent control:** South Dakota does not have statewide rent control.
- **No pre-suit notice (post-2024):** As of July 1, 2024, South Dakota repealed the separate notice-to-quit requirement. Landlords may file FED actions directly.
- **5-day response:** Defendant has 5 days after service to file an answer.
- **Optional 3-day notice:** While not required, some landlords still provide a 3-day notice as a courtesy or lease requirement.
- **Recording:** South Dakota is a one-party consent state for recordings (SDCL 22-24B-2). You may record conversations you are a party to without the other person's consent.

## Cross-State Notes

- **vs. North Dakota:** Both have no pre-suit notice requirement for some grounds. ND requires 3-day notice for nonpayment and violations; SD requires none post-2024.
- **vs. Nebraska:** NE requires 7-day notice for nonpayment; SD requires none.
- **vs. Iowa:** IA requires 3-day notice for nonpayment; SD requires none.
