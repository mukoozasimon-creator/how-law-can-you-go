## The Short Answer

In Maryland, eviction notice requirements vary by jurisdiction. For nonpayment, Baltimore City requires a **30-day written notice** (Baltimore City Code Art. 13, § 8-402); elsewhere in Maryland, a **10-day written notice** applies (Real Property § 8-401). For lease violations, the landlord must give a **30-day notice** to cure or vacate. To end a month-to-month tenancy, the landlord must give **30 days' written notice** (§ 8-402). Maryland has no statewide just-cause requirement, but Montgomery County and Prince George's County have **local just-cause protections**. Self-help eviction is illegal.

### Real Property § 8-402 - Termination of Month-to-Month Tenancy

> "A tenancy at will may be terminated by either party by giving written notice to the other at least thirty (30) days before the date specified in the notice." cite🛠web_search:27#12:~:text=A tenancy at will may be terminated by either party by giving written notice to the other at least thirty (30) days before the date specified in the notice.

**Live link:** https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=grp&section=8-402
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.

### Real Property § 8-203 - Security Deposits

**Key rules:**
- No statewide cap on security deposit amount.
- Return deadline: **45 days** after move-out with itemized statement.
- Must provide **move-in inspection checklist** if deposit exceeds one month's rent.
- Wrongful withholding: Tenant can recover **actual damages** plus attorney's fees.

**Live link:** https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=grp&section=8-203
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 45-day return. Move-in checklist required for deposits over one month's rent.

## Important Notes

- **Local just-cause protections:** Montgomery County and Prince George's County have local just-cause protections. Check local ordinances.
- **No statewide just-cause requirement:** Maryland landlords can terminate month-to-month tenancies for any reason with proper notice (except in localities with just-cause rules).
- **No rent control:** Maryland preempts local rent control, but some counties have rent stabilization.
- **Eviction timeline:** Hearings typically within 2-3 weeks of filing. Writ of possession issued after judgment.
- **Service methods:** Personal delivery or certified/registered mail. Posting on door is NOT authorized for pre-suit notice.
- **Recording:** Maryland is a two-party consent state for recordings (Md. Courts and Judicial Proceedings Code § 10-402). All parties must consent to recording conversations.

## Cross-State Notes

- **vs. Virginia:** Both have no statewide just-cause requirement, but MD has longer notice in Baltimore City (30 days vs. VA's 5 days).
- **vs. Pennsylvania:** Both have moderate protections, but MD has no lease waiver provision (PA allows lease modification of notice rules).
- **vs. Washington:** MD is far more landlord-friendly with no statewide just-cause requirement.
