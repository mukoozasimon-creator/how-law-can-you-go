<div class="quip">Pennsylvania: where the cheesesteaks are worth the calories and the DUI refusal costs you a year of driving.</div>

<div class="settled">
<b>The stuff that is actually true in Pennsylvania</b>
<ul>
<li><strong>You already consented to chemical testing.</strong> 75 Pa.C.S. 1547. By driving in Pennsylvania, you are deemed to have consented to breath or blood testing if lawfully arrested for DUI. The officer chooses which test. Urine testing is no longer standard for DUI cases.</li>
<li><strong>First refusal = 12-month suspension. Second = 18 months.</strong> 75 Pa.C.S. 1547(b). Administrative suspension by PennDOT, separate from criminal case.</li>
<li><strong>Refusal is admissible in court.</strong> <em>Commonwealth v. Myers</em> (2017). The prosecutor can use your refusal as evidence of consciousness of guilt.</li>
<li><strong>Pennsylvania has three DUI tiers.</strong> 75 Pa.C.S. 3802. General impairment (0.08-0.099%), High rate (0.10-0.159%), and Highest rate (0.16%+). Each tier carries different penalties. Refusal is treated as the highest rate tier.</li>
<li><strong>You can record the stop.</strong> Pennsylvania is a two-party consent state, but recording police in public is generally protected by the First Amendment. Do not interfere.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="varies">
<b>The grey area where people get themselves arrested</b>
<ul>
<li><strong>"I want to speak to an attorney first."</strong> You have no right to an attorney before deciding whether to submit to chemical testing. The officer does not have to wait.</li>
<li><strong>"I'll take a blood test instead of breath."</strong> The officer chooses the test, not you. You can request blood, but the officer is not required to honor it.</li>
<li><strong>"The officer didn't read me the implied consent warning."</strong> The officer must inform you that refusal will result in suspension. Failure to give proper warning may invalidate the refusal.</li>
<li><strong>"I wasn't driving, I was sleeping in the car."</strong> Pennsylvania uses 'actual physical control' standard. If you have the keys and the ability to operate the vehicle, you can be arrested for DUI.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="wrongbox">
<b>Things TikTok told you that are wrong</b>
<ul>
<li>"If you refuse, they have no case." Wrong. Refusal triggers automatic suspension AND can be used as evidence of guilt. The prosecutor can still convict on officer observations and field sobriety tests.</li>
<li>"You have a right to an independent test before the state's test." Wrong. You are entitled to an independent test AFTER submitting to the state's test, not before.</li>
<li>"The suspension only applies if you're convicted." Wrong. The suspension is administrative and happens regardless of the criminal case outcome.</li>
<li>"You can get an OLL (Occupational Limited License) right away." Wrong. You must serve a portion of the suspension before becoming eligible for an OLL or PLD.</li>
</ul>
</div>
