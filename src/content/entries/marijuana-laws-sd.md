# Marijuana Laws - South Dakota

## At a Glance

| Feature | Status |
|---------|--------|
| Recreational | No - fully illegal (Constitutional Amendment A passed 54% in 2020 but struck down by Supreme Court Nov. 2021; Measure 27 defeated 47% in 2022) |
| Medical | Yes (Initiated Measure 26, Nov. 2020; effective July 1, 2021) |
| Home Grow | Yes - minimum 3 plants for registered medical patients (or amount prescribed by physician) |
| Age | 21+ for any possession; any age for medical with parental consent if under 18 |
| Possession Limit (Rec) | Any amount: criminal offense |
| Possession Limit (Med) | 3 oz; additional amounts of products set by Department of Health |
| Tax | N/A (no legal market) |
| Distinctive Feature | Only state where voters approved both medical and recreational simultaneously (2020), but recreational was struck down by courts; unique "internal possession" law (positive drug test = misdemeanor); employer must treat medical patients same as prescription drug users |

### 1. Medical Marijuana Program

**Instrument:** South Dakota Codified Laws Title 34, Chapter 20G (Initiated Measure 26, 2020)

**Live Link:** [https://sdlegislature.gov/Statutes/34-20G](https://sdlegislature.gov/Statutes/34-20G)

**Verbatim Quote (offset ~char 100-400):**
> "Measure No. 26, approved Nov. 3, 2020... Cardholders may possess 3 ounces of marijuana and additional amounts of marijuana products. Additionally, if a resident cardholder is allowed to grow marijuana plants the cardholder may possess a minimum of 3 plants, as well as marijuana and products made from those plants."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending. The medical program is operational with registered dispensaries.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Registered patients may possess up to 3 oz and cultivate a minimum of 3 plants (or physician-prescribed amount).

**Repeal Check:** N/A - the amendment was already struck down.

**Verdict:** VOID. Amendment A was struck down as unconstitutional under the single-subject rule. Recreational cannabis remains fully illegal.

### 4. Employment Protections for Medical Patients

**Instrument:** South Dakota Codified Laws Title 34, Chapter 20G (Initiated Measure 26)

**Live Link:** [https://sdlegislature.gov/Statutes/34-20G](https://sdlegislature.gov/Statutes/34-20G)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "Under IM 26, South Dakota employers are required to afford a qualifying patient the same rights as afforded to a person who is prescribed a pharmaceutical medication... a qualifying patient cannot be conclusively determined to be presently under the influence of marijuana based solely on the presence of metabolites or components of cannabis in their drug test."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Employers must treat medical cannabis patients equivalently to patients using prescription medications. A positive drug test alone cannot establish impairment.

### 5. "Internal Possession" Law

**Instrument:** South Dakota Codified Laws (unique state law)

**Live Link:** N/A

**Verbatim Quote:**

## Distinctive State Features

1. **Courts Overturned Voter-Approved Recreational:** South Dakota is the only state where voters approved recreational cannabis (54%, 2020) and the courts struck it down. A subsequent 2022 initiative also failed.

2. **"Internal Possession" Law:** South Dakota is the only U.S. state where a positive cannabis drug test alone constitutes a misdemeanor offense.

3. **Medical Employment Protections:** Measure 26 requires employers to treat medical cannabis patients the same as prescription medication users and prohibits conclusive impairment determinations based solely on metabolite presence.

4. **Minimum 3 Plant Home Grow:** Medical patients are guaranteed a minimum of 3 plants, with physicians able to prescribe higher amounts.

5. **No Decriminalization:** Unlike neighboring North Dakota, South Dakota has not decriminalized any amount of recreational possession.
