## The Short Answer

In Hawaii, your landlord must give you a **10-calendar-day written notice** to cure nonpayment, plus provide a copy to a state-funded mediation center (HRS 521-68, effective Feb. 5, 2026 under Act 278). If you schedule mediation within 10 days, the landlord must wait **20 calendar days** from receipt before filing. For material lease violations, a **10-day notice** to remedy applies (HRS 521-69). To end a month-to-month tenancy, the landlord must give **45 days' written notice** (HRS 521-71(a)). Hawaii has no statewide just-cause requirement and no rent control. Self-help eviction is illegal.

## Key Statutes

### HRS 521-68 - 10-Day Notice for Nonpayment (with Mediation)

> "A landlord may not file suit to evict a residential tenant for nonpayment of rent alone without first giving 10 days' written notice and providing that notice to the community mediation center serving the property's island."

**Live link:** https://www.capitol.hawaii.gov/hrscurrent/Vol12_Ch0501-0588/HRS0521/HRS_0521-0068/
**Repeal check:** Active. Pilot program runs through Feb. 4, 2028. Confirm status before relying.
**Verdict:** 10-calendar-day notice for nonpayment. Must copy mediation center. If mediation scheduled within 10 days, landlord must wait 20 days from receipt before filing.

### HRS 521-69 - 10-Day Notice for Material Noncompliance

> "If the tenant fails to comply with a material term of the rental agreement, the landlord may deliver a written notice specifying the breach and stating that the rental agreement will terminate in not less than 10 days if the breach is not remedied."

**Live link:** https://www.capitol.hawaii.gov/hrscurrent/Vol12_Ch0501-0588/HRS0521/HRS_0521-0069/
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 10-day cure-or-quit for material lease violations.

### HRS 521-72 - 10-Day Notice for Rule Breach

> "If the tenant breaches a rule of the rental agreement, the landlord may deliver a written notice specifying the rule breached and stating that the rental agreement will terminate in not less than 10 days if the breach is not remedied."

**Live link:** https://www.capitol.hawaii.gov/hrscurrent/Vol12_Ch0501-0588/HRS0521/HRS_0521-0072/
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 10-day cure-or-quit for rule breaches.

### HRS 521-71(a) - 45-Day Month-to-Month Termination

> "The landlord or the tenant may terminate a month-to-month tenancy by a written notice given to the other at least 45 days before the termination date."

**Live link:** https://www.capitol.hawaii.gov/hrscurrent/Vol12_Ch0501-0588/HRS0521/HRS_0521-0071/
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 45 days written notice for month-to-month termination. This is longer than most states' 30-day requirement.

### HRS 521-44 - Security Deposits

**Key rules:**
- Maximum: **1 month's rent** for unfurnished units.
- Return deadline: **14 days** after tenant vacates.
- Wrongful withholding: Tenant can recover full deposit plus penalty equal to the deposit.

**Live link:** https://www.capitol.hawaii.gov/hrscurrent/Vol12_Ch0501-0588/HRS0521/HRS_0521-0044/
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 14-day return. 1-month cap. Double deposit penalty for wrongful withholding.

### HRS 521-63 - Self-Help Eviction Prohibited

> "Self-help eviction is prohibited and exposes the landlord to statutory damages of up to 3 months' rent or actual damages, whichever is greater."

**Live link:** https://www.capitol.hawaii.gov/hrscurrent/Vol12_Ch0501-0588/HRS0521/HRS_0521-0063/
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** Self-help eviction is illegal. Landlord liable for up to 3 months' rent or actual damages.

## What This Means for You

| Situation | Notice Required | Just Cause Required? |
|-----------|----------------|----------------------|
| Month-to-month termination | 45 days written | No |
| Nonpayment of rent | 10 days (cure) + mediation copy | N/A |
| Material lease violation | 10 days (cure or quit) | N/A |
| Rule breach | 10 days (cure or quit) | N/A |

## Important Notes

- **No just-cause requirement:** Hawaii landlords can terminate month-to-month tenancies for any reason with proper notice.
- **No rent control:** Hawaii does not have statewide rent control.
- **Mandatory mediation (pilot):** As of Feb. 5, 2026, nonpayment evictions require copying a mediation center. If mediation is scheduled, filing is delayed to 20 days from receipt. This is a 2-year pilot through Feb. 4, 2028.
- **Longer notice periods:** Hawaii's 45-day month-to-month notice and 10-day nonpayment notice are longer than most states.
- **Recording:** Hawaii is a one-party consent state for recordings (HRS 803-42). You may record conversations you are a party to without the other person's consent.

## Cross-State Notes

- **vs. California:** HI has no just-cause requirement statewide (unlike CA), but HI's 45-day notice is longer than CA's 30/60-day rules.
- **vs. Alaska:** Both have no just-cause requirement, but HI's notice periods are generally longer.
- **vs. Oregon:** OR has statewide just-cause requirements; HI does not.
