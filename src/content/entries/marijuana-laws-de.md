# Marijuana Laws - Delaware

## At a Glance

| Feature | Status |
|---------|--------|
| Recreational | Yes (HB 1 / HB 2, Apr. 2023; adult-use retail sales launched Aug. 1, 2025) |
| Medical | Yes (SB 17, 2011; operational since 2015) |
| Home Grow | No - prohibited for both recreational and medical users |
| Age | 21+ for recreational; 18+ for medical |
| Possession Limit (Rec) | 1 oz flower, 12 g concentrate, or 750 mg delta-9 THC |
| Possession Limit (Med) | Up to 6 oz usable marijuana; purchase limit 3 oz per 14 days |
| Tax | 15% retail marijuana tax (marijuana control enforcement fee); medical exempt |
| Distinctive Feature | No home cultivation for anyone; 15% tax; social equity licensing (15 of 30 retail licenses reserved); landlord cannot prohibit non-smoked use; public consumption is criminal misdemeanor |

## Citation Layer

### 1. Recreational Legalization

**Instrument:** Delaware Code Title 16, Chapter 47A (Delaware Marijuana Control Act / HB 305, formerly HB 1 and HB 2, 2023)

**Live Link:** [https://legis.delaware.gov/BillDetail/79083](https://legis.delaware.gov/BillDetail/79083)

**Verbatim Quote (offset ~char 100-400):**
> "The Delaware Marijuana Control Act regulates and taxes marijuana in the same manner as alcohol. It allows adults over the age of 21 to legally possess and consume under 1 ounce of marijuana for personal use. It does not permit people to grow their own marijuana."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal legislation pending. Retail sales launched August 1, 2025.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Adults 21+ may possess up to 1 oz of flower, 12 g concentrate, or 750 mg delta-9 THC. Home cultivation is prohibited.

---

### 2. Home Cultivation Prohibition

**Instrument:** Delaware Code Title 16, Chapter 47A; HB 305

**Live Link:** [https://legis.delaware.gov/BillDetail/79083](https://legis.delaware.gov/BillDetail/79083)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "Home cultivation is not allowed."

**Repeal Check:** No legislation to permit home cultivation has advanced as of August 2026.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE PROHIBITION. Delaware prohibits home cultivation for both recreational and medical users. All cannabis must be purchased from licensed facilities.

---

### 3. Medical Marijuana Program

**Instrument:** Delaware Code Title 16, Chapter 49A (Delaware Medical Marijuana Act / SB 17, 2011)

**Live Link:** [https://delcode.delaware.gov/title16/c049A/](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title16/c049A/)

**Verbatim Quote (offset ~char 200-500):**
> "A registered qualifying patient shall not be subject to arrest, prosecution, or denial of any right or privilege... for the medical use of marijuana pursuant to this chapter, if the registered qualifying patient does not possess more than 6 ounces of usable marijuana."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Registered patients may possess up to 6 oz and purchase up to 3 oz per 14-day period from compassion centers.

---

### 4. Tax Structure

**Instrument:** Delaware Code Title 16, Chapter 47A; HB 305

**Live Link:** [https://delcode.delaware.gov/title16/c047A/](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title16/c047A/)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "Adult-use sales carry a 15% retail marijuana tax (the marijuana control enforcement fee). The tax is waived for patients who present a valid medical marijuana card."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Recreational sales taxed at 15%. Medical purchases are tax-exempt.

---

### 5. Landlord Protections for Non-Smoked Use

**Instrument:** Delaware Code Title 16, Chapter 47A, Section 4764A (HB 305)

**Live Link:** [https://legis.delaware.gov/BillDetail/79083](https://legis.delaware.gov/BillDetail/79083)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "Property owners could prohibit the possession and consumption of cannabis, except that in most cases landlords could not prevent tenants' non-smoked use of cannabis."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Landlords may generally prohibit smoking/vaping but cannot prevent tenants from non-smoked cannabis consumption (edibles, tinctures, topicals).

---

### 6. Public Consumption Penalty

**Instrument:** Delaware Code Title 16, Chapter 47A

**Live Link:** [https://legis.delaware.gov/BillDetail/79083](https://legis.delaware.gov/BillDetail/79083)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "Public consumption remains a criminal misdemeanor as of June 2026."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Public consumption of cannabis is a criminal misdemeanor, not a civil violation.

## Distinctive State Features

1. **No Home Cultivation:** Delaware is one of the few legal states that prohibits home cultivation entirely for both recreational and medical users.

2. **Landlord Non-Smoked Use Protection:** Delaware uniquely protects tenants' right to use non-smoked cannabis products (edibles, tinctures, topicals) even when landlords prohibit smoking.

3. **Social Equity Licensing:** 15 of 30 retail licenses and 20 of 60 cultivator licenses are reserved for social equity applicants.

4. **Public Consumption as Criminal Misdemeanor:** Unlike many states that treat public use as a civil fine, Delaware classifies it as a criminal misdemeanor.

5. **Medical Tax Exemption:** Medical patients pay no tax on cannabis purchases.
