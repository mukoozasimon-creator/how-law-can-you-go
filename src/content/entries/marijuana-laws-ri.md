# Marijuana Laws - Rhode Island

## At a Glance

| Feature | Status |
|---------|--------|
| Recreational | Yes (Rhode Island Cannabis Act / H 7593 / S 2430, May 2022; sales began Dec. 1, 2022) |
| Medical | Yes (Edward O. Hawkins and Thomas C. Slater Medical Marijuana Act, 2006) |
| Home Grow | Yes - 3 mature + 3 immature per dwelling unit (recreational); 12 mature + 12 immature (medical, with state-issued plant tags) |
| Age | 21+ for recreational; 18+ for medical (minors with caregiver) |
| Possession Limit (Rec) | 1 oz in public; up to 10 oz at primary residence |
| Possession Limit (Med) | 2.5 oz per 15-day period |
| Tax | 10% state excise + 7% state sales + 3% local = ~20% total on recreational; medical untaxed |
| Distinctive Feature | Automatic expungement for pre-2024 possession convictions; social equity + workers' cooperative license set-asides; 10 oz home possession cap; medical patients must use state-issued plant tags |

## Citation Layer

### 1. Recreational Possession and Home Cultivation

**Instrument:** Rhode Island General Laws Title 21, Chapter 28.11, Section 21-28.11-22 (Rhode Island Cannabis Act)

**Live Link:** [https://codes.findlaw.com/ri/title-21-food-and-drugs/ri-gen-laws-sect-21-28-11-22/](https://codes.findlaw.com/ri/title-21-food-and-drugs/ri-gen-laws-sect-21-28-11-22/)

**Verbatim Quote (offset ~char 100-450):**
> "A person twenty-one (21) years of age or older shall not be arrested, prosecuted, penalized, sanctioned or disqualified under the laws of the state in any manner... (2) Within any residence, possessing, cultivating or processing not more than a total of three (3) mature cannabis plants and up to a total of three (3) immature cannabis plants per dwelling unit for personal use... (3) Within the person's primary residence, possessing up to ten ounces (10 oz.) total of cannabis per resident."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal legislation pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Adults 21+ may possess 1 oz in public, up to 10 oz at home, and cultivate 3 mature + 3 immature plants per dwelling unit.

### 2. Medical Marijuana Home Cultivation

**Instrument:** Rhode Island General Laws Title 21, Chapter 28.6 (Edward O. Hawkins and Thomas C. Slater Medical Marijuana Act)

**Live Link:** [https://law.justia.com/codes/rhode-island/title-21/chapter-21-28-6/](https://law.justia.com/codes/rhode-island/title-21/chapter-21-28-6/)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "Medical marijuana cardholders are permitted to cultivate 12 immature marijuana plants and 12 mature ones in their homes or other indoor facilities... Each plant must have a state-issued plant tag certificate obtained through the Office of Cannabis Regulation."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Registered medical patients may cultivate 12 mature + 12 immature plants indoors, with state-issued plant tags required for each plant.

### 3. Automatic Expungement

**Instrument:** Rhode Island General Laws Section 21-28.11-5 (Rhode Island Cannabis Act)

**Live Link:** [https://webserver.rilegislature.gov/Statutes/TITLE21/21-28.11/INDEX.htm](https://webserver.rilegislature.gov/Statutes/TITLE21/21-28.11/INDEX.htm)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "Section 12-1.3-5 of the Rhode Island Cannabis Act provides for the expungement of all misdemeanor, felony, or civil violation convictions relating to the possession of marijuana before July 1, 2024. Convictions for the delivery or manufacturing of marijuana are not eligible for expungement."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Automatic expungement applies to possession convictions predating July 1, 2024. Delivery/manufacturing convictions remain ineligible.

### 4. Social Equity and Workers' Cooperative Licenses

**Instrument:** Rhode Island General Laws Title 21, Chapter 28.11, Section 21-28.11-4 (Rhode Island Cannabis Act)

**Live Link:** [https://webserver.rilegislature.gov/Statutes/TITLE21/21-28.11/INDEX.htm](https://webserver.rilegislature.gov/Statutes/TITLE21/21-28.11/INDEX.htm)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "The Cannabis Act describes social equity applicants as individuals who have been adversely and disproportionately affected by the enforcement of now-abrogated marijuana-related laws in Rhode Island... One of these four licenses will be reserved for social equity applicants. Another license will be specially reserved for workers' cooperative applicants."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Rhode Island reserves retail licenses for social equity applicants and workers' cooperatives in each of six geographic zones.

### 5. Tax Structure

**Instrument:** Rhode Island General Laws; Rhode Island Cannabis Act

**Live Link:** [https://tax.ri.gov/](https://tax.ri.gov/)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "Recreational cannabis is subject to: 7% State Sales and Use Tax; 10% State Cannabis Excise Tax; 3% Local Cannabis Excise Tax."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Combined effective tax rate of approximately 20% (10% excise + 7% sales + 3% local). Medical cannabis is not taxed.

## Distinctive State Features

1. **10 oz Home Possession Cap:** Rhode Island allows adults to possess up to 10 ounces of cannabis at their primary residence - one of the highest home possession limits in the nation.

2. **Automatic Expungement:** Rhode Island provides automatic expungement for pre-2024 possession convictions, a relatively rare provision.

3. **Workers' Cooperative License Set-Asides:** Rhode Island is one of the few states to reserve licenses specifically for workers' cooperatives.

4. **Medical Plant Tagging:** Medical patients must obtain state-issued plant tags for each cultivated plant - a requirement not imposed on recreational home growers.

5. **Greenhouse Exclusion:** Greenhouses do not qualify as "indoor" spaces for medical cultivation under Rhode Island regulations.
