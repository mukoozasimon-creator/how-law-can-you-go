# Homeschooling Laws - California

## At a Glance

| Feature | Status |
|---------|--------|
| Legal Status | Yes - 3 pathways (home-based private school, private satellite program, certified tutor) |
| Notification Required | Yes - Private School Affidavit (PSA) filed Oct 1-15 annually |
| Teacher Qualifications | No teaching credential required for parents |
| Required Subjects | Same as public schools (English, math, social sciences, science, visual/performing arts, health, PE) |
| Testing Required | No |
| Instructional Hours | None specified (3 hrs/day minimum for certified tutor option) |
| Compulsory Ages | 6-18 |
| Distinctive Feature | PSA is a tax form, not an approval process; no state oversight of curriculum; largest homeschool population in US |

## Citation Layer

### 1. Legal Framework - Three Pathways

**Instrument:** California Education Code Section 48222 (private school exemption); Section 33190 (private school affidavit)

**Live Link:** [https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=EDC&division=2.&title=2.&part=27.&chapter=1.&article=5.](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=EDC&division=2.&title=2.&part=27.&chapter=1.&article=5.)

**Verbatim Quote (offset ~char 200-500):**
> "The exemption from the provisions of this chapter applicable to a private school applies to a parent, guardian, or other person having control or charge of a child who is being instructed in a private full-time day school by a person capable of teaching."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal legislation pending. AB 2756 (2018) sought to require fire inspections for homeschools and was defeated. No significant threats pending as of August 2026.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. California recognizes homeschools as private schools. Three legal pathways exist.

---

### 2. Private School Affidavit (PSA)

**Instrument:** California Education Code Section 33190; California Department of Education PSA filing system

**Live Link:** [https://www.cde.ca.gov/sp/ps/psaffidavit.asp](https://www.cde.ca.gov/sp/ps/psaffidavit.asp)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "Persons, firms, associations, partnerships, or corporations offering or conducting private school instruction on the elementary or high school level shall, between the first and fifteenth day of October of each year, file with the Superintendent of Public Instruction an affidavit."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. The PSA must be filed between October 1-15 annually. It is a statistical filing, not an approval process. The state does not review or approve curriculum.

---

### 3. Required Subjects

**Instrument:** California Education Code Section 51210 (courses of study for elementary); Section 51220 (courses of study for secondary)

**Live Link:** [https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=EDC&division=4.&title=2.&part=28.&chapter=2.&article=1.](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=EDC&division=4.&title=2.&part=28.&chapter=2.&article=1.)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "English, mathematics, social sciences, science, visual and performing arts, health, and physical education."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Homeschools must offer the same branches of study taught in California public schools.

---

### 4. Certified Tutor Option

**Instrument:** California Education Code Section 48224

**Live Link:** [https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=EDC&division=2.&title=2.&part=27.&chapter=1.&article=5.](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=EDC&division=2.&title=2.&part=27.&chapter=1.&article=5.)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "A child who is being instructed by a person who holds a valid California teaching credential for the grade level taught is exempt from compulsory attendance."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Parents with valid CA teaching credentials may register as certified tutors, exempting their children from compulsory attendance. Instruction must be at least 3 hours/day between 8am-4pm for 175 days/year.

---

### 5. Record Keeping

**Instrument:** California Education Code; California Department of Education guidelines

**Live Link:** [https://www.cde.ca.gov/sp/ps/psaffidavit.asp](https://www.cde.ca.gov/sp/ps/psaffidavit.asp)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "Attendance records, courses of study, instructor qualifications, and immunization records or waivers must be maintained."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Private schools (including homeschools) must maintain: attendance records, courses of study, instructor qualifications, and immunization records/waivers.

## Distinctive State Features

1. **PSA is Not Approval:** The Private School Affidavit is a statistical filing only. The state does not review, approve, or evaluate homeschool programs.

2. **Largest Homeschool Population:** California has the highest number of homeschool students in the United States, estimated at 200,000+.

3. **Three Distinct Pathways:** Unlike most states, California offers three legally distinct pathways (home-based private school, PSP, certified tutor), each with different requirements.

4. **No Testing Mandate:** California does not require standardized testing or annual evaluations for homeschool students.

5. **AB 2756 Defeated:** A 2018 bill that would have required fire marshal inspections for homeschools was defeated after significant homeschool community opposition.
