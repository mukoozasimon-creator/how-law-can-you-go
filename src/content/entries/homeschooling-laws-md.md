## Summary

Maryland requires moderate regulation for homeschooling. Under COMAR 13A.10.01, parents must submit a Home Instruction Notification form to the local school superintendent at least 15 days before beginning home instruction. Parents must provide instruction in 8 required subjects and maintain a portfolio for review. Annual verification of continuation is required. Maryland does not require parent qualifications, standardized testing, or minimum instructional hours. Families may also homeschool under the supervision of an approved nonpublic school or church organization.

## Legal Framework

### Primary Statute

**Md. Code, Education § 7-301** - Compulsory attendance

> Establishes that all children ages 5-16 must attend school or receive regular, thorough instruction.

- **Instrument**: Md. Code, Education § 7-301
- **Live Link**: https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/laws/statute?ggo=7-301
- **Repeal Check**: Active as of 2026; no repeal legislation pending
- **Verdict**: Homeschooling is permitted as an alternative to public school attendance.

### Primary Regulation

**COMAR 13A.10.01** - Home Instruction

> "A parent or guardian who chooses to provide a home instruction program for his or her child must sign a statement on a form prescribed by the Maryland State Department of Education, indicating consent to home instruction requirements. The home instruction regulations specify that the form must be submitted to the local superintendent or designee at least 15 calendar days prior to beginning home instruction."

- **Instrument**: COMAR 13A.10.01
- **Live Link**: https://www.marylandpublicschools.org/about/pages/dee/npsa/home-instruction.aspx
- **Repeal Check**: Active as of 2026; no repeal legislation pending
- **Verdict**: Home instruction is permitted with notification, subject, and portfolio requirements.

| **Required Subjects** | 8 subjects (see below) |
| **Testing** | None required by state |

## Notification

**Home Instruction Notification** must be submitted to the local school superintendent at least **15 calendar days** before beginning home instruction. The form is prescribed by the Maryland State Department of Education.

**Annual Verification**: Once home instruction has begun, the parent must annually verify continuation with the local school system or supervising nonpublic school/institution.

**Status Changes**: Parents must notify the supervising entity of any change in status during the school year.

**Important**: A parent can be held responsible for unexcused absences if a child is withdrawn from public school but the notice of consent has not been filed and instruction has not been initiated.

## Required Subjects (8)

Under COMAR 13A.10.01, home instruction must include:

1. English/Language Arts
2. Mathematics
3. Science
4. Social Studies
5. Art
6. Music
7. Health
8. Physical Education

There are no restrictions on which curriculum is used, as long as all 8 subjects are covered.

## Assessment & Testing

No standardized testing is required by state regulation. However:

- If the reviewing school determines the child is not receiving proper instruction, the parent has **30 days** to show the problem has been resolved
- If not resolved, the child must be enrolled in public school or an accredited nonpublic school
- Some districts conduct portfolio reviews to assess progress

## Record-Keeping

Parents must maintain a **portfolio** for each child, which may be reviewed by the supervising entity. The portfolio should demonstrate regular, thorough instruction in the required subjects.

**Umbrella School Exception**: Parents do not need to maintain a portfolio if the child is supervised by:
- A nonpublic school with a certificate of approval from the State Board of Education, OR
- A school or institution offering an educational program operated by a bona fide church organization

## Special Provisions

- **Umbrella Schools**: Families may homeschool under the supervision of an approved nonpublic school or church organization, which may reduce oversight
- **Portfolio Review**: Under district supervision, portfolios may be reviewed near the conclusion of each semester
- **No Additional Requirements**: Local school systems may not impose requirements beyond those in COMAR 13A.10.01
- **Sports/Activities**: No statutory right to public school extracurricular participation
- **Funding**: No state-level financial programs for homeschoolers

## Distinctive Features

- 15-day advance notification requirement
- 8 required subjects (above average)
- Portfolio review system (not standardized testing)
- No parent qualifications required
- No minimum instructional hours specified
- Umbrella school option reduces oversight
- Local districts cannot add requirements beyond COMAR 13A.10.01
