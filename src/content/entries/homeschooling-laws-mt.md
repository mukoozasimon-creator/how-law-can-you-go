# Homeschooling Laws - Montana

## Statutory Framework

Montana homeschools operate under **MCA 20-5-102** (compulsory attendance) and **MCA 20-5-109** (nonpublic and homeschool requirements for exemption).

### Primary Instrument: MCA 20-5-109

> "To qualify its students for exemption from compulsory enrollment under 20-5-102, a home school: (a) shall notify the county superintendent of schools, of the county in which the home school is located in each school fiscal year of the student's attendance at the school; (b) shall maintain records on pupil attendance and make the records available to the county superintendent of schools on request; (c) shall provide at least the minimum aggregate hours of pupil instruction in accordance with 20-1-301 and 20-1-302; (d) shall provide an organized course of study that includes instruction in the subjects required of public schools as a basic instructional program pursuant to 20-7-111."
> - Source: [Montana Coalition of Home Educators - Laws](https://www.mtche.org/laws) (Authority: NA)
> - Repeal check: Active as of 2026; no repeal pending.

### Notification

Parents must notify the **county superintendent of schools** in each school fiscal year of the student's attendance.

> "Home educators must provide basic notification of their intent to homeschool in Montana to the county superintendent of the local school board."
> - Source: [SVHS - Homeschooling in Montana](https://svhs.co/homeschooling-montana-laws/) (2026-01-16)

The notification is the main legal requirement. Once submitted, parents can enroll their child without further state approval.

### Required Subjects

The curriculum must include instruction in subjects required of public schools as a basic instructional program under MCA 20-7-111. The basic instructional program includes:
- English language arts
- Mathematics
- Social studies
- Science
- Health
- Arts
- Career education

> "Provide an organized course of study that includes instruction in the subjects required of public schools as a basic instructional program pursuant to 20-7-111."
> - Source: [Montana Coalition of Home Educators - Laws](https://www.mtche.org/laws)

### Instructional Time

Minimum aggregate hours per MCA 20-1-301 and 20-1-302:
- **360 hours** for half-time kindergarten
- **720 hours** for full-time kindergarten through grade 3
- **1,080 hours** for grades 4 through 12

> "Provide at least the minimum aggregate hours of pupil instruction in accordance with 20-1-301 and 20-1-302."
> - Source: [Montana Coalition of Home Educators - Laws](https://www.mtche.org/laws)

### Teacher Qualifications

**None.** Montana statute does not require any degree, diploma, or teaching credential for homeschool parents.

> "There are no specific teacher certification or qualification requirements for homeschool instructors in Montana."
> - Source: [SVHS - Homeschooling in Montana](https://svhs.co/homeschooling-montana-laws/)

### Testing Requirements

**None.** Montana does not require any form of standardized testing or assessment for homeschool students.

> "Assessment: Montana does not require any form of standardized testing or assessment."
> - Source: [SVHS - Homeschooling in Montana](https://svhs.co/homeschooling-montana-laws/)

### Record-Keeping

Parents must maintain records on pupil attendance and make them available to the county superintendent on request.

> "Maintain records on pupil attendance and make the records available to the county superintendent of schools on request."
> - Source: [Montana Coalition of Home Educates - Laws](https://www.mtche.org/laws)

### Health and Safety

The homeschool must be housed in a building that complies with applicable local health and safety regulations.

> "Must be housed in a building that complies with applicable local health and safety regulations in accordance with state law."
> - Source: [Heritage Academy Kalispell - Homeschooling Laws in Montana](https://www.heritageacademykalispell.org/homeschooling-laws-in-montana)

### Compulsory Attendance Ages

Ages 7 through 16, or completion of 8th grade (whichever is later).

> "Children between the ages of 7 and 16 must be enrolled in school (MCA 20-5-102)."
> - Source: [Blue Folder - Montana Homeschool Requirements](https://bluefolder.app/states/montana) (2026)

### Immunization

Montana requires immunization records or exemption documentation. Homeschools must maintain disease immunization records and make them available on request.

### Public School Access

Montana does not guarantee homeschoolers access to public school classes, sports, or extracurricular activities. District policies vary.

### Distinctive Features

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Notification** | Annual notice to county superintendent |
| **Testing** | None |
| **Qualifications** | None |
| **Required Subjects** | Basic instructional program (English, math, social studies, science, health, arts, career ed) |
| **Instructional Time** | 360 (half K) / 720 (K-3) / 1,080 (4-12) hours |
| **Record-Keeping** | Attendance records (available on request) |
| **Health/Safety** | Building must comply with local health/safety regs |
| **Compulsory Ages** | 7-16 or completion of 8th grade |

### Verdict

Montana is a **low-regulation** state with modest structural requirements. The annual notification, minimum hour standards, and basic instructional program requirements create a light compliance burden, but the absence of testing, qualifications, and curriculum approval keeps Montana among the more permissive states. The health/safety building requirement is unusual among homeschool states.
