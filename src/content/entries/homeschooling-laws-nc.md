# Homeschooling Laws - North Carolina

## At a Glance

| Feature | Status |
|---------|--------|
| Legal Status | Yes - single compliance pathway through DNPE |
| Notification Required | Yes - one-time Notice of Intent to DNPE |
| Teacher Qualifications | High school diploma or GED required |

## Citation Layer

### 1. Legal Framework - DNPE

**Instrument:** North Carolina General Statutes Chapter 115C, Article 39 (Nonpublic Schools); N.C.G.S. 115C-547 et seq.

**Live Link:** [https://www.ncleg.gov/Laws/GeneralStatuteSections/Chapter115C](https://www.ncleg.gov/Laws/GeneralStatuteSections/Chapter115C)

**Verbatim Quote (offset ~char 100-400):**
> "A home school shall be defined as a nonpublic school... wherein the student or students receive academic instruction from their parent, legal guardian, or a member of the household."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending. North Carolina has maintained stable homeschool regulations with gradual expansion of funding options.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. North Carolina defines homeschools as nonpublic schools under the Division of Non-Public Education (DNPE).

---

### 2. Notice of Intent

**Instrument:** N.C.G.S. 115C-563(a)

**Live Link:** [https://www.ncleg.gov/Laws/GeneralStatuteSections/Chapter115C](https://www.ncleg.gov/Laws/GeneralStatuteSections/Chapter115C)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "A home school shall file a notice of intent to operate with the Division of Non-Public Education."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. One-time Notice of Intent filed with DNPE. Must include: school name, address, chief administrator name, and type of school (religious or independent).

---

### 3. Teacher Qualifications

**Instrument:** N.C.G.S. 115C-563(b)

**Live Link:** [https://www.ncleg.gov/Laws/GeneralStatuteSections/Chapter115C](https://www.ncleg.gov/Laws/GeneralStatuteSections/Chapter115C)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "The person or persons providing the instruction shall hold at least a high school diploma or its equivalent."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Teaching parent must have a high school diploma or GED.

---

### 4. Annual Testing

**Instrument:** N.C.G.S. 115C-564; N.C.G.S. 115C-549

**Live Link:** [https://www.ncleg.gov/Laws/GeneralStatuteSections/Chapter115C](https://www.ncleg.gov/Laws/GeneralStatuteSections/Chapter115C)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "A nationally standardized test... shall be administered annually to each student."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Annual nationally normed standardized test required. Test must measure achievement in English grammar, reading, spelling, and mathematics. Results kept on file for 1 year.

---

### 5. Operational Requirements

**Instrument:** N.C.G.S. 115C-548; N.C.G.S. 115C-556

**Live Link:** [https://www.ncleg.gov/Laws/GeneralStatuteSections/Chapter115C](https://www.ncleg.gov/Laws/GeneralStatuteSections/Chapter115C)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "The school shall operate on a regular schedule... during at least nine calendar months of the year."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Homeschool must operate at least 9 calendar months per year. Must maintain immunization records (or religious exemption) and attendance/disease records on file.

## Distinctive State Features

1. **DNPE Administration:** North Carolina uniquely administers all homeschools through a dedicated state division (Division of Non-Public Education), creating a centralized but streamlined process.

2. **Annual Testing:** Unlike Georgia's triennial testing, North Carolina requires annual standardized testing - one of the more frequent testing mandates.

3. **No Required Subject List:** North Carolina does not mandate specific subjects, giving parents more curriculum flexibility than Georgia or Pennsylvania.

4. **One-Time Notification:** Unlike states requiring annual notification, NC requires only a one-time Notice of Intent to DNPE.

5. **ESA+ for Disabilities:** Students with disabilities may access ESA+ funding for educational expenses.
