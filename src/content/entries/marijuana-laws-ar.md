# Marijuana Laws - Arkansas

## Verdict

Arkansas permits medical cannabis only under Amendment 98 (2016). Registered patients may purchase up to 2.5 ounces of usable marijuana per rolling 14-day period across all dispensaries combined. Home cultivation is prohibited. Out-of-state patients may apply for a Visiting Patient card ($50, 90-day validity) to purchase from Arkansas dispensaries. Recreational marijuana remains illegal. Employment protections exist but with a significant safety-sensitive exception under Act 593 (2017).

## Possession Limits (Medical)

| Product Type | Limit |
|--------------|-------|
| Usable marijuana | 2.5 oz per rolling 14-day period (across all dispensaries) |
| Edibles | 10 mg THC per portion maximum |
| Home possession | Same as purchase limits (no home grow) |

## Medical Program

- **Status:** Active since 2019
- **Enabling law:** Arkansas Medical Marijuana Amendment (Amendment 98, 2016)
- **Regulator:** Arkansas Department of Health (ADH)
- **Qualifying conditions:** Cancer, glaucoma, HIV/AIDS, hepatitis C, ALS, Tourette's, Crohn's disease, ulcerative colitis, PTSD, severe arthritis, fibromyalgia, Alzheimer's, cachexia, peripheral neuropathy, intractable pain, severe nausea, seizures, severe muscle spasms, and others
- **Patient requirements:** Arkansas resident, age 18+ (minors with caregiver), physician certification, ADH registry card
- **Product restrictions:** Edibles limited to 10 mg THC per portion; no animal/vehicle/cartoon shapes; no transparent packaging; dispensaries cannot sell smoking paraphernalia
- **Dispensaries:** Up to 40 licensed dispensaries
- **Cultivators:** Up to 8 licensed cultivation facilities
- **Out-of-state reciprocity:** Visiting Patient card available ($50, 90 days, 14-day processing)

## Home Cultivation

- **Status:** Illegal for all purposes.
- Qualifying patients and designated caregivers are not permitted to cultivate medical marijuana under Amendment 98.

## DUI / Impairment

- Arkansas prohibits operating a motor vehicle under the influence of cannabis.
- Amendment 98, Section 6(a)(3) does not allow anyone to operate a vehicle, aircraft, or motorboat under the influence of marijuana.
- Medical card is not a defense to DUI.

## Employment Protections

- **Basic protection:** Amendment 98 prohibits employers from refusing to hire, firing, or punishing someone solely for being a registered patient or caregiver.
- **Safety-sensitive exception (Act 593):** Employers may exclude medical marijuana patients from safety-sensitive positions involving heavy equipment, driving, aviation, hazardous materials, and other roles where impairment could cause serious harm.
- **Impairment standard:** A positive drug test alone does not prove impairment; employers must have additional evidence (behavior, appearance, or other reliable information).
- **Drug-free workplace:** Employers may enforce drug-free workplace policies and prohibit on-duty use.

## Federal Conflict

- Cannabis remains Schedule I under federal law.
- Interstate transport is prohibited even with medical authorization.
- Federal property in Arkansas prohibits cannabis possession.
