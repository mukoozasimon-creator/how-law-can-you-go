<div class="quip">Georgia: where the peaches are sweet and the DUI refusal costs you a year of driving.</div>

<div class="settled">
<b>The stuff that is actually true in Georgia</b>
<ul>
<li><strong>You already consented to chemical testing.</strong> O.C.G.A. 40-5-55. By driving in Georgia, you are deemed to have consented to blood, breath, urine, or other bodily substance testing if lawfully arrested for DUI or involved in a serious injury/fatal crash. The officer chooses which test.</li>
<li><strong>Refusal = 1-year minimum suspension.</strong> O.C.G.A. 40-5-67.1. First refusal = 1-year suspension. The officer must read the appropriate implied consent notice at the time the test is requested.</li>
<li><strong>You are entitled to an independent test.</strong> O.C.G.A. 40-5-67.1. After submitting to the state's test, you are entitled to an additional independent test at your own expense from qualified personnel of your choosing. The officer must reasonably accommodate this request.</li>
<li><strong>Refusal is admissible in court.</strong> Your refusal can be offered into evidence against you at trial as evidence of consciousness of guilt.</li>
<li><strong>You can record the stop.</strong> Georgia is a one-party consent state. You may record your own interactions with police without informing them.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="varies">
<b>The grey area where people get themselves arrested</b>
<ul>
<li><strong>"I want to speak to an attorney first."</strong> You have no right to an attorney before deciding whether to submit to chemical testing. The officer does not have to wait.</li>
<li><strong>"I'll take a blood test instead of breath."</strong> The officer chooses the test, not you. You can request blood, but the officer is not required to honor it.</li>
<li><strong>"The officer didn't read me the implied consent warning."</strong> The officer must read the appropriate notice at the time the test is requested. Failure to do so can invalidate the refusal.</li>
<li><strong>"I want an independent test before the state's test."</strong> Wrong. You are entitled to an independent test AFTER submitting to the state's test, not before.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="wrongbox">
<b>Things TikTok told you that are wrong</b>
<ul>
<li>"If you refuse, they have no case." Wrong. Refusal triggers automatic suspension AND can be used as evidence of guilt. The prosecutor can still convict on officer observations and field sobriety tests.</li>
<li>"They can't suspend your license if you beat the DUI." Wrong. The implied consent suspension is administrative and independent of the criminal case.</li>
<li>"You have a right to an attorney before deciding." Wrong. You have no right to counsel before the implied consent decision.</li>
<li>"The officer must give you the notice in writing." Wrong. The officer must read the notice to you orally. Written notice is not required.</li>
</ul>
</div>
