# Marijuana Laws - Mississippi

## Verdict

Mississippi permits medical cannabis only under the Mississippi Medical Cannabis Act (SB 2095, signed February 2022). The program uses a Medical Cannabis Equivalency Unit (MCEU) system: 1 MCEU equals 3.5g of flower, 100mg of THC in infused products, or 1g of concentrate. Patients may purchase up to 6 MCEUs per week (21g), 24 MCEUs per month (84g), and possess up to 28 MCEUs (98g) at once. Flower is capped at 30% THC; concentrates at 60% THC. Home cultivation is prohibited. First-time possession of up to 30g is decriminalized. Recreational marijuana remains illegal.

## Possession Limits (Medical)

| Product Type | Limit |
|--------------|-------|
| Flower | 3.5g = 1 MCEU; max 28 MCEU possession (98g) |
| Concentrates | 1g = 1 MCEU; max 28 MCEU possession |
| Infused products | 100mg THC = 1 MCEU; max 28 MCEU possession |
| Weekly purchase | Max 6 MCEU (21g flower equivalent) |
| Monthly purchase | Max 24 MCEU (84g flower equivalent) |
| Home possession | Max 28 MCEU (98g flower equivalent) |

## Penalty Tiers (Non-Medical Possession)

| Amount | Classification | Penalty |
|--------|---------------|---------|
| <=30g (first offense) | Civil infraction (decriminalized) | $100-$250 fine |
| <=30g (subsequent) | Misdemeanor | Up to 1 year jail; up to $1,000 fine |
| >30g - 250g | Felony | Up to 3 years prison; up to $3,000 fine |
| >250g - 500g | Felony | 2-8 years prison; up to $50,000 fine |
| >500g | Felony | 4-30 years prison; up to $1,000,000 fine |

## Medical Program

- **Status:** Active since 2022
- **Enabling law:** Mississippi Medical Cannabis Act (SB 2095, 2022)
- **Regulator:** Mississippi State Department of Health (MSDH) and Department of Revenue (MDOR)
- **Qualifying conditions:** ~28 debilitating conditions including cancer, epilepsy, Parkinson's, Huntington's, muscular dystrophy, MS, spasticity, ALS, glaucoma, HIV/AIDS, hepatitis, Alzheimer's, Crohn's, ulcerative colitis, sickle cell anemia, autism, chronic pain, PTSD, and others
- **Patient requirements:** Mississippi resident, age 18+ (minors with parent/guardian), physician certification from MSDH-registered practitioner
- **Product forms:** Flower, concentrates, edibles, topicals, tinctures, oils, beverages, capsules
- **Prohibited:** Smoking/vaporizing in motor vehicles or public; home cultivation
- **Dispensary restrictions:** No drive-through, curbside, or delivery; sales only inside dispensary; no advertising on broadcast/electronic/print media or billboards (HB 1195, 2026)
- **Tax:** 7% excise tax + 4% sales tax on medical cannabis

## Home Cultivation

- **Status:** Illegal for all purposes.
- Patients and caregivers are not allowed to grow their own cannabis.

## DUI / Impairment

- Mississippi prohibits operating a motor vehicle, boat, train, or aircraft while under the influence of cannabis.
- Any person convicted of DUI forfeits their right to drive for at least six months.
- Medical card is not a defense to DUI.

## Employment Protections

- The Mississippi Medical Cannabis Act does not require any employer to allow patients to use medical cannabis or prevent them from requiring drug testing.
- Employers may maintain drug-free workplace policies.
- No statutory employment protections for medical cannabis patients.
- Landlords may ban tenants from using medical cannabis at home.

## Federal Conflict

- Cannabis remains Schedule I under federal law.
- Interstate transport is prohibited.
- Federal property in Mississippi prohibits cannabis possession.
