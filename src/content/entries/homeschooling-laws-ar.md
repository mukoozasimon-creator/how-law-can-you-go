# Homeschooling Laws - Arkansas

## Statutory Framework

Arkansas recognizes homeschooling as a form of private education. There is no standalone homeschool statute; compliance flows through the private school exemption to compulsory attendance.

### Primary Instrument: Arkansas Code Annotated (Compulsory Attendance Exemption)

Arkansas compulsory attendance law (ages 5-17) provides exemptions for children enrolled in private schools. Homeschools qualify under this framework.

> "Arkansas homeschool laws require children ages 5-17 to attend school, with homeschooling recognized as private education. Parents must provide annual notice to their local school district but face minimal other requirements."
> - Source: [Sounded Therapy - Arkansas Homeschool Requirements](https://soundedtherapy.com/arkansas-homeschool-requirements-legal-guide/) (2025-12-03)

### Notice of Intent

Parents must submit an **annual Notice of Intent** to the local superintendent.

**Key requirements:**
- Filed by **August 15** each year (or within a reasonable time for mid-year starts)
- Must include: student name, date of birth, grade level, and homeschool address
- Must affirm that the curriculum includes reading, writing, mathematics, science, and social studies

> "The parent must submit a written notice of intent to begin homeschooling to their local superintendent by August 15th each year. This notice must include the name, date of birth, and grade level of each homeschooled child, as well as the name and address of the homeschool."
> - Source: [SVHS - Homeschooling in Arkansas & Homeschool Laws](https://svhs.co/homeschooling-arkansas-laws/) (2025-01-10)

### Teacher Qualifications

**None specified.** Arkansas statute does not require a high school diploma, college degree, or teaching certification for homeschool parents.

### Testing Requirements

**None.** Arkansas removed its previously required standardized testing mandate.

> "Standardized testing for homeschoolers is no longer required."
> - Source: [Homeschool Grades - Arkansas](https://homeschoolgrades.com/homeschool-laws/arkansas) (2026)

> "In Arkansas, homeschooled students are not required to participate in standardized testing. The state leaves the decision to administer tests and monitor the progress of homeschooled children up to the parent."
> - Source: [SVHS - Homeschooling in Arkansas](https://svhs.co/homeschooling-arkansas-laws/)

### Instructional Time

No minimum hours or days are specified in statute.

### Required Subjects

The notice of intent must affirm that the curriculum includes:
- Reading
- Writing
- Mathematics
- Science
- Social studies

However, there is no curriculum approval process and no enforcement mechanism beyond the parent's affirmation.

### Record-Keeping

Not statutorily mandated, but maintaining records of coursework, grades, and progress is strongly recommended for transcript purposes and potential re-enrollment in public school.

### Compulsory Attendance Ages

Ages 5 through 17.

### LEARNS Act ESA Funding

The **Arkansas LEARNS Act** (2023) provides Education Savings Account (ESA) funding of up to **$6,672 annually** for eligible homeschool families to use for curriculum, tutoring, educational technology, and other approved expenses. This is one of the most generous homeschool funding programs in the nation.

> "The LEARNS Act, passed in 2023, transformed Arkansas's educational landscape. The Education Savings Account program provides up to $6,672 per eligible student annually for homeschool expenses."
> - Source: [Numa School - Homeschooling in Arkansas](https://numaschool.com/states/arkansas/homeschool-laws) (2026-07-30)

### Restrictions

Homeschooling is **prohibited** if a registered sex offender resides in the household.

### Public School Access

Arkansas law does not guarantee homeschoolers access to public school sports or classes. District policies vary.

### Distinctive Features

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Notification** | Annual Notice of Intent by Aug 15 |
| **Testing** | None |
| **Qualifications** | None |
| **Required Subjects** | 5 subjects affirmed in notice (no approval) |
| **Instructional Time** | None specified |
| **Funding** | LEARNS Act ESA up to $6,672/year |
| **Restriction** | Prohibited if sex offender in household |

### Verdict

Arkansas is a **low-regulation** homeschool state with minimal bureaucratic burden. The annual notice is straightforward, and the absence of testing, qualifications, and hour requirements gives parents broad autonomy. The LEARNS Act ESA program is a significant distinctive feature, making Arkansas financially attractive for homeschooling families.
