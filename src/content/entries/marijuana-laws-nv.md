# Marijuana Laws - Nevada

## Verdict

Nevada permits adult-use (recreational) cannabis for adults 21+ under Question 2 (2016), with retail sales operational since July 2017. Adults may possess up to 2 oz of flower, 1 oz of concentrate, 16 oz of solid edibles, or 72 oz of liquid products in public. Home cultivation of up to 6 plants per person (max 12 per household) is permitted only if the grower lives more than 25 miles from the nearest licensed dispensary. Medical patients may possess up to 2.5 oz of usable cannabis and cultivate up to 12 plants regardless of dispensary proximity.

## Named Instruments

- **NRS Chapter 453D** - Regulation and Taxation of Marijuana (Question 2 implementation)
- **NRS 453D.110** - Personal use and possession limits
- **NRS 453D.400** - Home cultivation restrictions (25-mile rule)
- **NRS 453D.110(2)** - Concentrate and edible possession limits
- **NRS 484C.110** - DUI (impairment-based; no per se THC limit)

## Possession Limits

| Product Type | Public Limit |
|--------------|-------------|
| Flower | 2 oz |
| Concentrates | 1 oz |
| Solid edibles | 16 oz |
| Liquid products | 72 oz |
| Seeds | 10 |
| Plants (home grow) | 6 per person / 12 per household (25-mile rule applies) |

*Medical patients: up to 2.5 oz usable cannabis; up to 12 plants regardless of dispensary distance.*

## Penalty Tiers (Over-Limit Possession)

| Amount | Classification | Penalty |
|--------|---------------|---------|
| >2 oz - 1 lb | Misdemeanor | Up to $600 fine |
| >1 lb | Felony | 1-4 years prison; up to $5,000 fine |
| >12 plants (rec, first offense) | Felony | Probation typical; up to 4 years prison |
| >12 plants (subsequent) | Felony | Enhanced penalties |

## Medical Program

- **Status:** Active since 2000 (NRS 453A); overseen by Nevada Department of Health and Human Services
- **Qualifying conditions:** Cancer, glaucoma, HIV/AIDS, PTSD, cachexia, severe pain, severe nausea, seizures, persistent muscle spasms, and others
- **Patient registry:** Required
- **Home grow:** Up to 12 plants regardless of dispensary proximity (unlike recreational growers)
- **Possession:** Up to 2.5 oz usable cannabis
- **Tax:** Medical patients exempt from 10% recreational excise tax

## Home Cultivation

- **Recreational:** Up to 6 plants per person, max 12 per household; ONLY if >25 miles from nearest licensed dispensary
- **Medical:** Up to 12 plants regardless of dispensary distance
- **Requirements:** Must be in enclosed, locked space not visible from public view
- **Important:** Growing >12 plants is a felony under NRS 453D.400

## DUI / Impairment

- **Standard:** Impairment-based; no per se nanogram THC blood limit
- **Testing:** Blood draws and field sobriety testing
- **Penalty:** First offense - 2 days to 6 months jail, $400-$1,000 fine, license suspension 90 days
- **Open container:** Must be stored in trunk or area not readily accessible to driver/passengers

## Employment Protections

- Nevada provides employment protections for off-duty cannabis use under NRS 613.333.
- Employers may not refuse to hire or terminate an employee solely for lawful off-duty cannabis use, with exceptions for safety-sensitive positions and federally regulated employers.
- Employers may still prohibit on-duty use and impairment.

## Federal Conflict

- Cannabis remains Schedule I under federal law.
- Interstate transport is prohibited even to neighboring legal states (CA, OR, AZ).
- Federal property (Hoover Dam, Area 51, national parks) prohibits cannabis.
