# Marijuana Laws - Iowa

## Verdict

Iowa permits medical cannabidiol only under the Iowa Medical Cannabidiol Act. The program is extremely restrictive: no smokable flower, no THC-infused edibles, and products are limited to oral forms, topical forms, nebulizable inhaled forms, suppositories, and vapes. Patients may possess no more than 4.5 grams of THC over a 90-day period, though certifying practitioners may waive this limit for terminally ill patients or established patients for whom 4.5g is insufficient. Recreational cannabis is illegal. Home cultivation is prohibited. Out-of-state patients with valid cards may possess products in permitted forms but cannot purchase from Iowa dispensaries.

## Named Instruments

- **Iowa Code Chapter 124E** - Medical Cannabidiol Act
- **Iowa Code Section 124.401** - Possession penalties
- **Iowa Admin. Code 641 IAC 154.14** - Permitted product forms
- **HF 2589 (2020)** - Expanded qualifying conditions and THC limits
- **SF 73 (2023)** - Adult-use legalization bill (did not advance)

## Possession Limits (Medical)

| Product Type | Limit |
|--------------|-------|
| THC (all forms) | 4.5 grams per 90-day period |
| Waiver-eligible THC | Greater amount permitted for terminal illness or established patients with practitioner approval |
| Product forms | Oral, topical, nebulized inhalable, suppositories, vapes ONLY |
| Prohibited forms | Flower, THC-infused edibles |

| Amount | Classification | Penalty |
|--------|---------------|---------|

| Third offense | Aggravated misdemeanor | Up to 2 years jail; up to $6,250 fine |

## Medical Program

- **Status:** Active since 2014 (SF 2360); expanded 2017 (HF 524), 2019, 2020 (HF 2589)
- **Enabling law:** Iowa Medical Cannabidiol Act (Iowa Code Chapter 124E)
- **Regulator:** Iowa Department of Health and Human Services - Office of Medical Cannabidiol
- **Qualifying conditions:** Cancer, chronic pain, Crohn's disease, MS, HIV/AIDS, ALS, Parkinson's, seizures, intractable pain, PTSD, autism, ulcerative colitis, terminal illness (life expectancy <1 year), and others
- **Patient requirements:** Iowa resident, physician certification from approved healthcare practitioner, state registry card
- **Product restrictions:** NO flower; NO THC-infused edibles; ONLY oral, topical, nebulized inhalable, suppositories, vapes
- **THC limit:** 4.5g per 90 days (waivable for terminal patients or established patients)
- **Dispensaries:** Limited licenses; Governor signed bill doubling dispensary count in June 2026
- **Out-of-state:** Out-of-state cardholders may possess permitted forms but cannot purchase from Iowa dispensaries

## Home Cultivation

- **Status:** Illegal for all purposes.
- Only licensed growers may cultivate cannabis within Iowa.

## DUI / Impairment

- Iowa prohibits operating a motor vehicle under the influence of cannabis.
- It is illegal to drive with medical cannabis unless the product is sealed and in a safe compartment of the vehicle.
- No per se THC limit is established.

## Employment Protections

- No statutory employment protections for medical cannabidiol patients.
- Employers and landlords are allowed to prohibit cannabis use on their property.
- Health insurance does not cover medical cannabis in Iowa.

## Federal Conflict

- Cannabis remains Schedule I under federal law.
- Interstate transport is prohibited.
- Federal property in Iowa prohibits cannabis possession.
