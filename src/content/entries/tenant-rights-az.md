## The Short Answer

In Arizona, your landlord must give you a **5-day written notice** to pay rent or quit for nonpayment (ARS § 33-1368). For material lease violations, the landlord must give a **10-day notice** to comply or vacate. For irreparable breaches (serious criminal activity), the landlord may give **immediate notice** to vacate. To end a month-to-month tenancy, either party must give **30 days' written notice** (§ 33-1375). Arizona has no just-cause requirement, no rent control, and no statewide security deposit cap. Self-help eviction is illegal.

### ARS § 33-1375 - Termination of Month-to-Month Tenancy

> "A tenancy at will may be terminated by the landlord by giving notice to the tenant at least thirty days prior to the periodic rental date specified in the notice." cite🛠web_search:27#10:~:text=Month-to-month tenancy termination: 30 days' notice (ARS §33-1375)

**Live link:** https://www.azleg.gov/ars/33/01375.htm
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 30 days written notice for month-to-month termination by either party.

### ARS § 33-1321 - Security Deposits

**Key rules:**
- No statewide cap on security deposit amount.
- Return deadline: **14 days** after move-out with itemized statement.
- Must provide **move-in form** with inventory of conditions.
- Wrongful withholding: Tenant can recover **2x the amount wrongfully withheld** plus damages.

**Live link:** https://www.azleg.gov/ars/33/01321.htm
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 14-day return. Move-in form required. Double damages for wrongful withholding.

### ARS § 33-1313(B) - Service of Notice

> "A.R.S. § 33-1313(B) authorizes service of a notice under the Residential Landlord and Tenant Act by delivering it in hand to the tenant or mailing it by registered or certified mail to the place the tenant holds out for receipt of communications or, absent that, to the tenant's last known place of residence." cite🛠web_search:27#10:~:text=A.R.S. § 33-1313(B) authorizes service of a notice under the Residential Landlord and Tenant Act by delivering it in hand to the tenant or mailing it by registered or certified mail

### ARS § 33-1367 - Self-Help Eviction Prohibited

> "A landlord may not recover possession of the dwelling unit by willfully interrupting electric, gas, water or other essential service to the tenant, except as the result of an emergency, or by willfully preventing the tenant from gaining reasonable access to the dwelling unit by any means, including changing the locks." cite🛠web_search:27#1:~:text=Self-help eviction is prohibited under ARS § 33-1367.

**Live link:** https://www.azleg.gov/ars/33/01367.htm
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** Self-help eviction is illegal. Only court order can remove tenant. Tenant can recover actual damages or 2 months' rent, whichever is greater.

## Important Notes

- **No just-cause requirement:** Arizona landlords can terminate month-to-month tenancies for any reason with proper notice.
- **No rent control:** Arizona preempts local rent control.
- **Fast eviction timeline:** Arizona has one of the faster eviction processes. Hearings typically within 2-3 weeks of filing.
- **Special detainer action:** Arizona evictions are filed as "special detainer" actions in Justice Court.
- **Recording:** Arizona is a one-party consent state for recordings (ARS § 13-3005). You may record conversations you are a party to without the other person's consent.

## Cross-State Notes

- **vs. Texas:** Both are landlord-friendly with no just-cause or rent control, but AZ has a faster 5-day notice vs. TX's 3-day notice.
- **vs. California:** AZ is far more landlord-friendly with no just-cause requirement and no rent control.
- **vs. Nevada:** Both have similar 5-day/30-day notice frameworks and no just-cause requirements.
