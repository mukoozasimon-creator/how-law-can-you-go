## The Short Answer

In Connecticut, your landlord must give you a **3-day written notice** to quit for nonpayment (CGS § 47a-23). For lease violations, the landlord must give a **15-day notice** to cure or quit (§ 47a-15). To end a month-to-month tenancy, the landlord must give **30 days' written notice** before the next rent due date (§ 47a-23). Connecticut has no statewide just-cause requirement, but several cities (including Hartford and New Haven) have **local just-cause protections**. Self-help eviction is illegal.

## Key Statutes

### CGS § 47a-23 - 3-Day Notice for Nonpayment

> "If rent is unpaid when due, the landlord may give the tenant a written notice to quit the premises within three days." cite🛠web_search:42#6:~:text=If rent is unpaid when due, the landlord may give the tenant a written notice to quit the premises within three days.

**Live link:** https://www.cga.ct.gov/current/pub/chap_830.htm#sec_47a-23
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 3-day notice for nonpayment. Tenant can cure by paying rent within 3 days.

### CGS § 47a-15 - 15-Day Notice for Lease Violations

> "If there is a material noncompliance by the tenant with the rental agreement or a noncompliance with Section 47a-7 materially affecting health and safety, the landlord may deliver a written notice to the tenant specifying the acts and omissions constituting the breach and that the rental agreement will terminate upon a date not less than fifteen days after receipt of the notice, if the breach is not remedied in fifteen days." cite🛠web_search:42#6:~:text=If there is a material noncompliance by the tenant with the rental agreement... the landlord may deliver a written notice to the tenant specifying the acts and omissions constituting the breach and that the rental agreement will terminate upon a date not less than fifteen days after receipt of the notice, if the breach is not remedied in fifteen days.

**Live link:** https://www.cga.ct.gov/current/pub/chap_830.htm#sec_47a-15
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 15-day notice for lease violations. Tenant has opportunity to cure.

### CGS § 47a-23 - Termination of Month-to-Month Tenancy

> "The landlord or the tenant may terminate a month-to-month tenancy by a written notice given to the other at least thirty days before the termination date specified in the notice." cite🛠web_search:42#6:~:text=The landlord or the tenant may terminate a month-to-month tenancy by a written notice given to the other at least thirty days before the termination date specified in the notice.

**Live link:** https://www.cga.ct.gov/current/pub/chap_830.htm#sec_47a-23
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 30 days written notice for month-to-month termination by either party.

### CGS § 47a-21 - Security Deposits

**Key rules:**
- No statewide cap on security deposit amount.
- Return deadline: **30 days** after move-out with itemized statement.
- Must provide **move-in inspection checklist**.
- Interest: Required if deposit held for 1+ year (at rate set by Banking Commissioner).
- Wrongful withholding: Tenant can recover **double the amount wrongfully withheld** plus attorney's fees.

**Live link:** https://www.cga.ct.gov/current/pub/chap_830.htm#sec_47a-21
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 30-day return. Interest required for deposits held 1+ year. Double damages for wrongful withholding.

### CGS § 47a-43 - Self-Help Eviction Prohibited

> "A landlord may not willfully interrupt or terminate any utility service being supplied to the tenant, except in the case of an emergency or for repairs, or prevent the tenant from gaining reasonable access to the dwelling unit by any means, including changing the locks." cite🛠web_search:42#6:~:text=A landlord may not willfully interrupt or terminate any utility service being supplied to the tenant, except in the case of an emergency or for repairs, or prevent the tenant from gaining reasonable access to the dwelling unit by any means, including changing the locks.

**Live link:** https://www.cga.ct.gov/current/pub/chap_830.htm#sec_47a-43
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** Self-help eviction is illegal. Only court order can remove tenant.

## Important Notes

- **Local just-cause protections:** Hartford, New Haven, and other municipalities have local just-cause protections. Check local ordinances.
- **No statewide just-cause requirement:** Connecticut landlords can terminate month-to-month tenancies for any reason with proper notice (except in localities with just-cause rules).
- **No rent control:** Connecticut does not have statewide rent control, but some municipalities have rent stabilization.
- **Eviction timeline:** Hearings typically within 2-3 weeks of filing. Writ of possession issued after judgment.
- **Service methods:** Personal delivery or certified/registered mail.
- **Recording:** Connecticut is a two-party consent state for recordings (CGS § 53a-189). All parties must consent to recording conversations.

## Cross-State Notes

- **vs. Massachusetts:** Both have strong tenant protections and deposit interest requirements, but MA has a 14-day notice vs. CT's 3-day notice for nonpayment.
- **vs. New York:** Both have local just-cause protections in major cities, but NY has a statewide Good Cause Eviction Law.
- **vs. Pennsylvania:** CT has stronger deposit rules (interest required) but PA allows lease waiver of notice requirements.
