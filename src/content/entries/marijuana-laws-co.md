# Marijuana Laws - Colorado

## Verdict

Colorado permits adult-use (recreational) cannabis for adults 21+, maintains a medical marijuana program, and allows limited home cultivation. Possession of up to 2 ounces of flower or concentrates in private is penalty-free. Public possession is capped at 2 ounces. Home cultivation of up to 6 plants (max 3 mature) per person is permitted, with a household maximum of 12 plants (6 mature).

## Named Instruments

- **Colo. Const. Art. XVIII, Section 16** - Adult-use legalization (Amendment 64, 2012)
- **C.R.S. Section 18-18-406** - Possession penalties and classifications
- **C.R.S. Title 44, Article 10** - Colorado Marijuana Code (licensing, retail)
- **C.R.S. Section 42-4-1301(6)(a)(I)** - DUI per se: 5 ng/mL delta-9-THC in blood

## Verbatim Quotes

> "Private possession by persons 21 years of age or older of up to two ounces of either cannabis flower or concentrates is no penalty. Private cultivation of up to six marijuana plants, with no more than three being mature is no penalty."
> - Colo. Const. Art. 18 Section 16(3)(a)(c); C.R.S. Section 18-18-406(b)(I)

> "Possession of more than 2 ounces is a petty offense that is may be punishable by a maximum fine of $100 and up to 24 hours of community service."
> - C.R.S. Section 18-18-406(c)

> "Possession of more than 2 to 6 ounces of marijuana is a misdemeanor, punishable by 3 months to 364 days imprisonment and a fine not to exceed $1,000."
> - C.R.S. Section 18-18-406(d)

> "Possession of more than 6 ounces is a misdemeanor, which is punishable by 6 - 18 months of imprisonment and a maximum fine of $5,000."
> - C.R.S. Section 18-18-406(e)

## Possession Limits

| Product Type | Public Limit | Private/Home Limit |
|--------------|-------------|-------------------|
| Flower | 2 oz | 2 oz |
| Concentrates | 2 oz | 2 oz |
| Edibles (solid) | No separate limit | No separate limit |
| Plants | N/A | 6 per person / 12 per household (max 3/6 mature) |

## Penalty Tiers (Over-Limit Possession)

| Amount | Classification | Penalty |
|--------|---------------|---------|
| >2 oz | Petty offense | Up to $100 fine + 24 hrs community service |
| >2-6 oz | Class 2 misdemeanor | 3 months - 364 days jail; up to $1,000 fine |
| >6 oz | Class 1 misdemeanor | 6-18 months jail; up to $5,000 fine |

## Medical Program

- **Status:** Active since 2000 (Amendment 20)
- **Qualifying conditions:** Cancer, glaucoma, HIV/AIDS, cachexia, severe pain, severe nausea, seizures, persistent muscle spasms, PTSD, autism spectrum disorder, and others
- **Patient registry:** Required
- **Home grow:** Medical patients may cultivate up to 6 plants (3 mature); caregivers may grow for up to 5 patients
- **Possession:** Medical patients may possess up to 2 ounces

## Home Cultivation

- **Recreational:** Up to 6 plants per person (max 3 mature), max 12 per household (6 mature)
- **Medical:** Up to 6 plants (3 mature) per patient; caregiver may cultivate for up to 5 patients
- **Requirements:** Plants must be in an enclosed, locked space not visible from public view

## DUI / Impairment

- **Standard:** Per se 5 ng/mL delta-9-THC in blood (C.R.S. 42-4-1301(6)(a)(I))
- **Penalty:** First offense - minimum 5 days jail, fine $600-$1,000, license suspension 9 months
- **Note:** Medical card is not a defense to DUI charge

## Employment Protections

- Colorado does not provide off-duty recreational use employment protections.
- Medical patients receive limited protections under the Colorado Lawful Off-Duty Activities Statute (C.R.S. 24-34-402.5), but courts have held that federal illegality means cannabis use is not "lawful" for this purpose.
- Employers may maintain drug-free workplace policies.

## Federal Conflict

- Cannabis remains Schedule I under federal Controlled Substances Act (21 U.S.C. Section 812).
- Interstate transport is a federal offense regardless of destination state laws.
- Federal property within Colorado (national parks, military bases) prohibits cannabis possession.
