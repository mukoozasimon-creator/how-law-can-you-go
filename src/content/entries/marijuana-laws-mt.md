# Marijuana Laws - Montana

## At a Glance

| Feature | Status |
|---------|--------|
| Recreational | Yes (Initiative 190, Nov. 2020; effective Jan. 1, 2021; sales began Jan. 1, 2022) |
| Medical | Yes (Initiative 148, 2004; expanded by SB 423, 2011) |
| Home Grow | Yes - 2 mature + 2 seedlings (recreational); 4 mature + 4 seedlings (medical patients without a provider) |
| Age | 21+ for recreational; 18+ for medical (minors with parental consent) |
| Possession Limit (Rec) | 1 oz flower, 8 g concentrate, or 800 mg THC in edibles |
| Possession Limit (Med) | 1 oz per day, 5 oz per month |
| Tax | 20% excise on recreational; 4% on medical; up to 3% local option tax |
| Distinctive Feature | County opt-in/opt-out system for recreational sales; 5 ng/mL THC DUI per se; strong employer rights to prohibit use |

## Citation Layer

### 1. Recreational Legalization

**Instrument:** Montana Code Annotated Title 50, Chapter 46, Part 3 (Medical Marijuana); Ballot Initiative 190 (Montana Marijuana Regulation and Taxation Act, 2020); Constitutional Initiative 118 (setting legal age at 21)

**Live Link:** [https://leg.mt.gov/bills/mca/title_0500/chapter_0460/part_0030/sections_index.html](https://leg.mt.gov/bills/mca/title_0500/chapter_0460/part_0030/sections_index.html)

**Verbatim Quote (offset ~char 200-500):**
> "Adults 21 and older can legally buy and possess up to one ounce of cannabis, grow a small number of plants at home, and consume in a private residence - but not in public, not on federal land, and never behind the wheel."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal legislation pending. HB 701 (2021) established the county opt-out framework and refined the regulatory structure. The legislature continues to refine rules but the core legalization remains intact.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Adults 21+ may possess up to 1 oz of flower (or equivalent) and cultivate 2 mature plants + 2 seedlings.

---

### 2. County Opt-In/Opt-Out System

**Instrument:** Montana Code Annotated; HB 701 (2021) - county option framework

**Live Link:** [https://revenue.mt.gov/card/cannabis/2025-legislative-updates](https://revenue.mt.gov/card/cannabis/2025-legislative-updates)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "By current law, local governments can hold elections to: Opt in/out of allowing adult-use marijuana businesses; Allow/disallow specific types of businesses."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Counties that voted against Initiative 190 in 2020 were automatically opted out of recreational sales. Counties may hold local elections to opt in or out. As of 2026, approximately half of Montana counties permit recreational sales.

---

### 3. Home Cultivation Limits

**Instrument:** Montana Code Annotated Title 50, Chapter 32, Section 50-32-101 et seq.; Initiative 190

**Live Link:** [https://leg.mt.gov/bills/mca/title_0500/chapter_0320/part_0010/sections_index.html](https://leg.mt.gov/bills/mca/title_0500/chapter_0320/part_0010/sections_index.html)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "Recreational users 21+ can grow 2 mature plants and 2 seedlings, while registered medical patients can grow 4 mature plants and 4 seedlings."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Recreational: 2 mature + 2 seedlings. Medical (without provider): 4 mature + 4 seedlings. Plants must be in a locked, enclosed area out of public view.

---

### 4. DUI - Per Se THC Limit

**Instrument:** Montana Code Annotated Title 61, Chapter 8, Section 61-8-410 (DUI)

**Live Link:** [https://leg.mt.gov/bills/mca/title_0610/chapter_0080/part_0040/sections_index.html](https://leg.mt.gov/bills/mca/title_0610/chapter_0080/part_0040/sections_index.html)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> Montana maintains a 5 nanograms per milliliter (ng/mL) THC blood concentration per se limit for DUI.

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Operating a vehicle with 5 ng/mL or more of THC in blood constitutes DUI per se.

### 5. Employment Protections

**Instrument:** No statutory off-duty employment protections for recreational use. Employers retain broad rights to prohibit marijuana use and maintain drug-testing policies.

**Live Link:** N/A

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "Employers can prohibit marijuana use, maintain drug-testing policies, and forbid impairment on the job."

**Repeal Check:** N/A

**Verdict:** NO OFF-DUTY RECREATIONAL EMPLOYMENT PROTECTIONS. Montana employers may enforce zero-tolerance policies.

### 6. Tax Structure

**Instrument:** Montana Code Annotated; Initiative 190; HB 701

**Live Link:** [https://revenue.mt.gov/card/cannabis](https://revenue.mt.gov/card/cannabis)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "Recreational marijuana: 20%. Medical marijuana: 4%. Optional local tax: Up to 3% additional."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Recreational sales taxed at 20%; medical at 4%; counties may add up to 3% local option tax.

## Distinctive State Features

1. **County Opt-Out System:** Montana uniquely allows counties to opt out of recreational cannabis sales entirely. This creates a patchwork where legal purchase may require travel to an adjacent county.

2. **5 ng/mL DUI Per Se:** Montana employs a strict per se THC blood limit for DUI.

3. **No Employment Protections:** Employers retain full authority to prohibit off-duty use.

4. **Strong Medical Program:** Montana's medical program predates recreational by 16 years and offers lower taxes (4% vs. 20%) and higher cultivation limits.
