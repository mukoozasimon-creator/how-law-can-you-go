# Homeschooling Laws - New York

## At a Glance

| Feature | Status |
|---------|--------|
| Legal Status | Yes - single compliance pathway (no alternatives, no umbrella schools) |
| Notification Required | Yes - Letter of Intent by July 1 + IHIP by August 15 |
| Teacher Qualifications | None required (parent competency assumed) |
| Required Subjects | 12+ subjects including patriotism, citizenship, highway safety, alcohol/drug/tobacco education |
| Testing Required | Yes - grades 4, 6, 8; annually grades 9-12 |
| Instructional Hours | 900 hours (grades 1-6); 990 hours (grades 7-12) |
| Compulsory Ages | 6-16 (6-17 in NYC) |
| Distinctive Feature | Highest regulation in the US; 4 quarterly reports; 7-year record retention; NYC has centralized DOE process |

## Citation Layer

### 1. Legal Framework - Section 100.10

**Instrument:** New York Education Law Section 100.10 of the Commissioner's Regulations; 8 NYCRR 100.10

**Live Link:** [https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/programs/nonpublic-schools/home-instruction/section100.10.pdf](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/programs/nonpublic-schools/home-instruction/section100.10.pdf)

**Verbatim Quote (offset ~char 100-400):**
> "A parent or guardian may instruct a minor child at home provided that the substantial equivalent of instruction is provided to the student."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending. Multiple bills to reduce reporting requirements have been introduced but not passed.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. New York requires homeschool instruction to be "substantially equivalent" to public school instruction, with extensive documentation requirements.

---

### 2. Letter of Intent

**Instrument:** 8 NYCRR 100.10(a)

**Live Link:** [https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/programs/nonpublic-schools/home-instruction/section100.10.pdf](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/programs/nonpublic-schools/home-instruction/section100.10.pdf)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "The parent or guardian shall submit a written notice of intention to instruct at home to the superintendent of schools of the school district in which the student resides."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Letter of Intent due by July 1 annually (or within 14 days of starting mid-year). Must include child's name, age, grade level, and parent's name/address.

---

### 3. Individualized Home Instruction Plan (IHIP)

**Instrument:** 8 NYCRR 100.10(b)

**Live Link:** [https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/programs/nonpublic-schools/home-instruction/section100.10.pdf](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/programs/nonpublic-schools/home-instruction/section100.10.pdf)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "The parent or guardian shall submit an individualized home instruction plan... within four weeks of the receipt of the notice of intention or by August 15, whichever is later."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. IHIP must include syllabi/curriculum descriptions for each required subject, textbook titles, four quarterly report dates, and names of all instructors. District has 10 business days to respond.

---

### 4. Quarterly Reports

**Instrument:** 8 NYCRR 100.10(c)

**Live Link:** [https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/programs/nonpublic-schools/home-instruction/section100.10.pdf](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/programs/nonpublic-schools/home-instruction/section100.10.pdf)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "The parent or guardian shall submit a quarterly report to the school district on dates specified in the IHIP."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Four quarterly reports per year showing hours completed, material covered, and grades/narrative evaluation. Typical dates: November 15, January 31, April 15, June 30.

---

### 5. Annual Assessment

**Instrument:** 8 NYCRR 100.10(d)

**Live Link:** [https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/programs/nonpublic-schools/home-instruction/section100.10.pdf](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/programs/nonpublic-schools/home-instruction/section100.10.pdf)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "The parent or guardian shall submit an annual assessment... in grades 4, 6, 8, and at the end of each school year for students in grades 9 through 12."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Standardized testing required in grades 4, 6, 8, and annually in high school. Must show adequate progress. Alternative: written narrative evaluation by certified teacher or psychologist.

---

### 6. NYC Special Rules

**Instrument:** NYC Department of Education Office of Homeschooling

**Live Link:** [https://www.schools.nyc.gov/enrollment/enrollment-help/home-schooling](https://www.schools.nyc.gov/enrollment/enrollment-help/home-schooling)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "New York City has its own centralized system for managing homeschool compliance. The requirements are identical - LOI, IHIP, quarterly reports, and annual assessment - but the submission method is different."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. NYC residents submit Letter of Intent to LetterofIntent@schools.nyc.gov. All other documents submitted via separate NYC DOE email addresses. Compulsory age extends to 17 in NYC.

## Distinctive State Features

1. **Highest Regulation in US:** New York has the most extensive homeschool requirements of any state - LOI, IHIP, 4 quarterly reports, annual assessments, and 7-year record retention.

2. **12+ Required Subjects:** Includes unique requirements like patriotism/citizenship, highway safety, and alcohol/drug/tobacco education not found in most states.

3. **NYC Centralized Process:** New York City operates a centralized homeschool office with email-based submission, different from the rest of the state.

4. **No Funding:** Unlike Florida or Texas, New York provides no financial support or tax credits for homeschoolers.

5. **7-Year Record Retention:** Records must be kept for 7 years, longer than most states.
