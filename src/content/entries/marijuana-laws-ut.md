# Marijuana Laws - Utah

## Verdict

Utah permits medical cannabis only. Proposition 2 (2018) was followed by legislative refinement into the Utah Medical Cannabis Act. Patients with qualifying conditions may purchase from licensed medical cannabis pharmacies after obtaining a state-issued medical cannabis card. Smoking cannabis is prohibited; vaporization of raw flower is permitted. Possession is limited to the lesser of a 30-day supply or 4 ounces of flower / 20 grams THC. Home cultivation is not allowed. Recreational marijuana remains illegal.

## Named Instruments

- **Utah Code Section 26B-4-101 et seq.** - Utah Medical Cannabis Act
- **Utah Code Section 26B-4-216** - Medical cannabis card requirements (effective 5/6/2026)
- **Utah Code Section 58-37-8** - Possession penalties (controlled substances)
- **Proposition 2 (2018)** - Voter initiative (subsequently modified by legislature)

## Verbatim Quotes

> "Recreational cannabis is not legal in Utah in 2026. Medical cannabis is legal for eligible patients who complete Utah's application process."
> - Utah Department of Health and Human Services

> "Patients are allowed to possess the lesser of: a) a one-month supply based on the dosage amount specified by their doctor or state-licensed pharmacist at a medical cannabis pharmacy; or b) four ounces of flower or 20 grams THC."
> - Utah Code Section 26B-4-201

> "Marijuana may be dispensed in the following forms: flower or bud, tablets, capsules, concentrated oil, liquid suspension, topicals, transdermal patches, sublingual preparations, and lozenges. Patients are not permitted to cultivate their own medical cannabis."
> - Utah Code Section 26B-4-201

> "Smoking cannabis flower is not permitted, though patients can purchase unprocessed flower for vaporization devices."
> - Utah Department of Health and Human Services

## Possession Limits (Medical)

| Product Type | Limit |
|--------------|-------|
| Flower | Lesser of 30-day supply OR 4 ounces |
| THC (all forms) | Lesser of 30-day supply OR 20 grams |
| Other products | 30-day supply as specified by QMP/pharmacist |
| Home possession | Same as above (no home grow) |

## Penalty Tiers (Non-Medical Possession)

| Amount | Classification | Penalty |
|--------|---------------|---------|
| <1 oz | Class B misdemeanor | Up to 6 months jail; up to $1,000 fine |
| 1 oz - 1 lb | Class A misdemeanor | Up to 1 year jail; up to $2,500 fine |
| >1 lb - 100 lbs | Third-degree felony | Up to 5 years prison; up to $5,000 fine |
| >100 lbs | Second-degree felony | 1-15 years prison; up to $10,000 fine |
| Distribution | Enhanced felony | Enhanced penalties based on amount and location |

## Medical Program

- **Status:** Active since 2018
- **Enabling law:** Utah Medical Cannabis Act (Proposition 2, modified by legislature)
- **Regulator:** Utah Department of Health and Human Services
- **Qualifying conditions:** HIV/AIDS, Alzheimer's, ALS, cancer, cachexia, Crohn's disease, epilepsy, multiple sclerosis, PTSD, autism, chronic pain, and others
- **Patient requirements:** Utah resident, evaluation by Qualified Medical Provider (QMP), state-issued medical cannabis card
- **Pharmacy requirements:** 14 state-approved pharmacies; each must employ at least one state-licensed pharmacist
- **Purchase limits:** No more than 14-day supply per transaction based on allotted dosage
- **Product forms:** Flower/bud (vaporization only), tablets, capsules, concentrated oil, liquid suspension, topicals, transdermal patches, sublingual preparations, lozenges
- **Prohibited:** Smoking; home cultivation
- **Caregivers:** Up to 2 caregivers per patient; caregivers may not cultivate

## Home Cultivation

- **Status:** Illegal for all purposes.
- Neither patients nor caregivers may cultivate cannabis at home.

## DUI / Impairment

- Utah prohibits operating a motor vehicle under the influence of cannabis.
- No per se THC limit is established; impairment determined by officer observation.
- Medical cannabis card is not a defense to DUI.
- Cannabis use in public is prohibited.

## Employment Protections

- No comprehensive statutory employment protections for medical cannabis patients.
- Employers retain discretion regarding drug policies.
- Medical card does not guarantee workplace protections, especially in safety-sensitive roles.
- State employees are protected from discipline based on registered patient status (if use complies with law).

## Federal Conflict

- Cannabis remains Schedule I under federal law.
- Interstate transport is prohibited.
- Federal property in Utah prohibits cannabis possession.
