## The Short Answer

In Montana, your landlord must give you a **3-day written notice** to pay rent or vacate for nonpayment (MCA 70-24-422(2)). For material lease violations, a **14-day notice** to remedy applies, with lease termination at the end of the 14-day period if uncured (70-24-422(1)(d)). For repeated violations within 6 months, a **5-day unconditional notice** applies (70-24-422(1)(e)). For criminal activity or violence, a **3-day unconditional notice** applies (70-24-422(1)(e)). To end a month-to-month tenancy, the landlord must give **30 days' written notice** (70-24-441). Montana has no just-cause requirement and no rent control. Self-help eviction is illegal.

## Key Statutes

### MCA 70-24-422(2) - 3-Day Notice for Nonpayment

> "If rent is unpaid when due and the tenant fails to pay rent within 3 days after written notice by the landlord of nonpayment and the landlord's intention to terminate the rental agreement if the rent is not paid within that period, the landlord may terminate the rental agreement."

**Live link:** https://leg.mt.gov/bills/mca/title_0700/chapter_0240/part_0040/section_0220/0700-0240-0040-0220.html
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 3-day notice for nonpayment. Tenant can cure by paying within 3 days.

### MCA 70-24-422(1)(d) - 14-Day Notice for Lease Violations

> "If the noncompliance is not listed in subsection (1)(b), (1)(c), or (1)(f), the notice period is 14 days."

**Live link:** https://leg.mt.gov/bills/mca/title_0700/chapter_0240/part_0040/section_0220/0700-0240-0040-0220.html
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 14-day cure-or-vacate for most lease violations. Tenant can cure by complying within 14 days.

### MCA 70-24-422(1)(e) - 5-Day Notice for Repeated Violations

> "If substantially the same act or omission that constituted a prior noncompliance of which notice was given recurs within 6 months, the landlord may terminate the rental agreement upon at least 5 days' written notice."

**Live link:** https://leg.mt.gov/bills/mca/title_0700/chapter_0240/part_0040/section_0220/0700-0240-0040-0220.html
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 5-day unconditional notice for repeated violations within 6 months. No cure right.

### MCA 70-24-422(1)(e) - 3-Day Notice for Criminal Activity

> "For criminal activity or violence threatening health or safety, the landlord may terminate upon 3 days' written notice."

**Live link:** https://leg.mt.gov/bills/mca/title_0700/chapter_0240/part_0040/section_0220/0700-0240-0040-0220.html
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 3-day no-cure notice for criminal activity or violence.

### MCA 70-24-441 - 30-Day Month-to-Month Termination

> "The landlord or the tenant may terminate a month-to-month tenancy by a written notice given to the other at least 30 days before the periodic rental date."

**Live link:** https://leg.mt.gov/bills/mca/title_0700/chapter_0240/part_0040/section_0441/0700-0240-0040-0441.html
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 30 days written notice for month-to-month termination by either party.

### MCA 70-25-202 - Security Deposits

**Key rules:**
- No statutory cap on security deposit amount.
- Return deadline: **30 days** with deductions (itemized statement); **10 days** if no deductions.

**Live link:** https://leg.mt.gov/bills/mca/title_0700/chapter_0250/part_0020/section_0202/0700-0250-0020-0202.html
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 10-30 day return depending on deductions. No statutory cap.

## What This Means for You

| Situation | Notice Required | Just Cause Required? |
|-----------|----------------|----------------------|
| Month-to-month termination | 30 days written | No |
| Nonpayment of rent | 3 days (pay or vacate) | N/A |
| Lease violation | 14 days (cure or vacate) | N/A |
| Repeated violation (within 6 months) | 5 days (no cure) | N/A |
| Criminal activity | 3 days (no cure) | N/A |

## Important Notes

- **No just-cause requirement:** Montana landlords can terminate month-to-month tenancies for any reason with proper notice.
- **No rent control:** Montana does not have statewide rent control.
- **No deposit cap:** Montana does not impose a statutory cap on security deposits.
- **Retaliation protection:** MCA 70-24-431 protects tenants from retaliation within 6 months of protected activity.
- **Recording:** Montana is a one-party consent state for recordings (MCA 45-8-213). You may record conversations you are a party to without the other person's consent.

## Cross-State Notes

- **vs. Idaho:** Both have 3-day notices for nonpayment and no just-cause requirements. MT has 14-day cure for violations vs. ID's 3-day.
- **vs. Wyoming:** Both have 3-day notices for nonpayment and no just-cause requirements.
- **vs. North Dakota:** Both have 3-day notices for nonpayment and no just-cause requirements.
