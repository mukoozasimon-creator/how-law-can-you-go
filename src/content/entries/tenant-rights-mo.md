## The Short Answer

In Missouri, your landlord must make a **demand for rent** before filing eviction for nonpayment (RSMo § 441.030). There is no fixed statutory waiting period between the demand and filing - the landlord can file immediately after demanding rent. For lease violations, the landlord must give a **10-day notice** to cure or vacate. To end a month-to-month tenancy, the landlord must give **30 days' written notice** (§ 441.060). Missouri has no just-cause requirement, no rent control, and no statewide security deposit cap. Self-help eviction is illegal.

### RSMo § 441.060 - Termination of Month-to-Month Tenancy

> "A tenancy at will may be terminated by either party by giving written notice to the other at least thirty (30) days before the date specified in the notice." cite🛠web_search:27#5:~:text=A tenancy at will may be terminated by either party by giving written notice to the other at least thirty (30) days before the date specified in the notice.

### RSMo § 535.300 - Security Deposits

**Key rules:**
- No statewide cap on security deposit amount.
- Return deadline: **30 days** after move-out with itemized statement.
- Must provide **move-in inspection checklist** if deposit exceeds one month's rent.
- Wrongful withholding: Tenant can recover **actual damages** plus attorney's fees.

**Live link:** https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=535.300
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 30-day return. Move-in checklist required for deposits over one month's rent.

## Important Notes

- **No just-cause requirement:** Missouri landlords can terminate month-to-month tenancies for any reason with proper notice.
- **No rent control:** Missouri preempts local rent control.
- **Eviction timeline:** Hearings typically within 2-3 weeks of filing. Writ of possession issued after judgment.
- **Service methods:** Personal delivery or certified/registered mail. Posting on door is NOT authorized for pre-suit notice.
- **Recording:** Missouri is a one-party consent state for recordings (RSMo § 542.402). You may record conversations you are a party to without the other person's consent.

## Cross-State Notes

- **vs. Illinois:** MO is more landlord-friendly with no just-cause requirement and no Chicago-style RLTO.
- **vs. Kansas:** Both have similar landlord-friendly frameworks with no just-cause or rent control.
- **vs. Iowa:** Both have no just-cause requirement, but MO has a demand-for-rent system vs. IA's 3-day notice.
