<div class="quip">Maryland: where the crabs are blue and your license is suspended for 270 days if you refuse the test (thanks, Noah's Law).</div>

<div class="settled">
<b>The stuff that is actually true in Maryland</b>
<ul>
<li><strong>You already consented to chemical testing.</strong> Md. Transp. Code 16-205.1. By driving in Maryland, you are deemed to have consented to breath and blood testing if lawfully arrested for DUI. The officer chooses which test.</li>
<li><strong>First refusal = 270-day suspension. Second = 2 years.</strong> Md. Transp. Code 16-205.1. You can elect the 1-year ignition interlock program instead of serving the suspension.</li>
<li><strong>The officer must fully advise you on the DR-15 form.</strong> Md. Transp. Code 16-205.1. The officer must explain administrative penalties for failing or refusing, including that refusal = ineligibility for any restricted license other than ignition interlock.</li>
<li><strong>Refusal carries enhanced criminal penalties.</strong> Md. Transp. Code 21-902(g). Knowingly refusing the test can add up to 2 months incarceration and $500 fine on top of any DUI penalties.</li>
<li><strong>You can record the stop.</strong> Maryland is a two-party consent state, but recording police in public is generally protected. Do not interfere.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="varies">
<b>The grey area where people get themselves arrested</b>
<ul>
<li><strong>"I want to speak to an attorney first."</strong> You can consult with a lawyer if you ask, so long as the consultation does not interfere with the testing process. The breath test technician must administer the test within 2 hours of apprehension.</li>
<li><strong>"I'll take a blood test instead of breath."</strong> The officer chooses the test, not you. You can request blood, but the officer is not required to honor it unless breath is unavailable or you are taken to a medical facility.</li>
<li><strong>"The officer didn't read me the DR-15."</strong> If the officer fails to fully advise you on the DR-15 form, the refusal may be invalid.</li>
<li><strong>"I wasn't driving, I was sleeping in the car."</strong> Maryland uses 'driving or attempting to drive' standard. If you have the keys and the ability to operate the vehicle, you can be arrested for DUI.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="wrongbox">
<b>Things TikTok told you that are wrong</b>
<ul>
<li>"If you refuse, they have no case." Wrong. Refusal triggers automatic suspension, can be used as evidence of guilt, AND carries enhanced criminal penalties under 21-902(g).</li>
<li>"You can get a restricted license right away." Wrong. After a refusal, the only restricted license available is the ignition interlock program.</li>
<li>"The suspension only applies if you're convicted." Wrong. The suspension is administrative and happens regardless of the criminal case outcome.</li>
<li>"You have 30 days to request a hearing." Wrong. You have 30 days from the notice date to request an MVA hearing.</li>
</ul>
</div>
