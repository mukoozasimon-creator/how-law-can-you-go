## The Short Answer

In Mississippi, your landlord must give you a **3-day written notice** to pay rent or the rental agreement terminates (Miss. Code Ann. 89-8-13(5)). For lease violations, a **14-day notice** to cure or quit applies (89-8-13(3)). For substantial violations affecting health/safety, no notice may be required (89-8-19(4)). To end a month-to-month tenancy, the landlord must give **30 days' written notice** (89-8-19). Mississippi has no just-cause requirement and no rent control. Self-help eviction is illegal.

## Key Statutes

### Miss. Code Ann. 89-8-13(5) - 3-Day Notice for Nonpayment

> "If the material noncompliance is nonpayment of rent, the landlord may give notice stating that the rental agreement will terminate if payment is not made within three days."

**Live link:** https://law.justia.com/codes/mississippi/title-89/chapter-8/section-89-8-13/
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 3-day notice for nonpayment. Tenant can cure by paying within 3 days. Even after judgment, tenant can avoid removal by paying all unpaid rent and costs before the court-ordered move-out date.

### Miss. Code Ann. 89-8-13(3) - 14-Day Notice for Lease Violations

> "If there is a material noncompliance by the tenant with the rental agreement, the landlord may deliver a written notice specifying the acts and omissions constituting the breach and that the rental agreement will terminate upon a date not less than 14 days after receipt of the notice if the breach is not remedied."

**Live link:** https://law.justia.com/codes/mississippi/title-89/chapter-8/section-89-8-13/
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 14-day cure-or-quit for lease violations. Second violation within 6 months = 14-day unconditional notice.

### Miss. Code Ann. 89-8-19(4) - Immediate Termination for Serious Violations

> "A landlord is not required to send notice before terminating a tenancy when the tenant has committed a substantial violation of the rental agreement or the law that materially affects health or safety."

**Live link:** https://law.justia.com/codes/mississippi/title-89/chapter-8/section-89-8-19/
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** No notice required for serious health/safety violations. Landlord can proceed immediately.

### Miss. Code Ann. 89-8-19 - 30-Day Month-to-Month Termination

> "The landlord or the tenant may terminate a month-to-month tenancy by a written notice given to the other at least 30 days before the termination date."

**Live link:** https://law.justia.com/codes/mississippi/title-89/chapter-8/section-89-8-19/
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 30 days written notice for month-to-month termination by either party.

### Miss. Code Ann. 89-8-39 - Post-Judgment Rent Payment

> "If the judgment is based solely on nonpayment of rent, the judge may not issue a warrant for removal if the tenant pays in full all unpaid rent and other sums awarded in the judgment by the court-ordered move-out date."

**Live link:** https://law.justia.com/codes/mississippi/title-89/chapter-8/section-89-8-39/
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** Tenant can stop eviction even after judgment by paying all amounts due before the move-out date.

## What This Means for You

| Situation | Notice Required | Just Cause Required? |
|-----------|----------------|----------------------|
| Month-to-month termination | 30 days written | No |
| Nonpayment of rent | 3 days (pay or quit) | N/A |
| Lease violation | 14 days (cure or quit) | N/A |
| Serious health/safety violation | No notice required | N/A |

## Important Notes

- **No just-cause requirement:** Mississippi landlords can terminate month-to-month tenancies for any reason with proper notice.
- **No rent control:** Mississippi does not have statewide rent control.
- **Post-judgment cure:** Even after an eviction judgment for nonpayment, the tenant can avoid removal by paying all amounts due before the court-ordered move-out date.
- **Self-help prohibited:** Residential landlords cannot lock out tenants, shut off utilities, or remove belongings without a court order.
- **Recording:** Mississippi is a one-party consent state for recordings (Miss. Code Ann. 41-29-531). You may record conversations you are a party to without the other person's consent.

## Cross-State Notes

- **vs. Alabama:** Both have no just-cause requirement and similar 3-day/14-day frameworks.
- **vs. Louisiana:** LA has a 5-day notice for nonpayment; MS has 3 days.
- **vs. Tennessee:** TN has a 14-day notice for violations similar to MS, but TN has a 14-day pay-or-quit for nonpayment vs. MS's 3 days.
