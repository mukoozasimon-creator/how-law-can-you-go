# Homeschooling Laws - Florida

## At a Glance

| Feature | Status |
|---------|--------|
| Legal Status | Yes - 3 pathways (home education program, umbrella school, private tutor) |
| Notification Required | Yes - one-time Notice of Intent to county superintendent |
| Teacher Qualifications | None required for home education program |
| Required Subjects | None specified for home education program |
| Testing Required | No - annual evaluation with 5 options (not mandated testing) |
| Instructional Hours | None specified |
| Compulsory Ages | 6-16 |
| Distinctive Feature | Personalized Education Program (PEP) provides ~$8,000/year; 5 evaluation options including portfolio review; no required subjects list |

## Citation Layer

### 1. Legal Framework - Home Education Program

**Instrument:** Florida Statutes Title XLVIII, Chapter 1002, Section 1002.41 (Home Education Program)

**Live Link:** [https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2023/1002.41](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2023/1002.41)

**Verbatim Quote (offset ~char 100-400):**
> "A 'home education program' means the sequentially progressive instruction of a student directed by his or her parent... in order to satisfy the attendance requirements of ss. 1002.41 and 1003.01(13)."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending. Florida has consistently expanded homeschool rights, including PEP funding and UIL access.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Florida's home education program is the most common pathway and requires only a one-time Notice of Intent and annual evaluation.

### 2. Notice of Intent

**Instrument:** Florida Statutes Section 1002.41(1)

**Live Link:** [https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2023/1002.41](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2023/1002.41)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "The parent shall notify the district school superintendent of the county in which the parent resides of her or his intent to establish and maintain a home education program."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. One-time written notice to the county superintendent required within 30 days of starting. Must include student names, addresses, birthdates, and parent signature.

---

### 3. Annual Evaluation - Five Options

**Instrument:** Florida Statutes Section 1002.41(1)(c)

**Live Link:** [https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2023/1002.41](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2023/1002.41)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "The parent shall provide for an annual educational evaluation in which is documented the student's demonstration of educational progress at a level commensurate with her or his ability."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Five evaluation options: (1) portfolio review by certified teacher, (2) nationally normed achievement test, (3) state assessment, (4) licensed psychologist evaluation, (5) mutually agreed method with superintendent.

---

### 4. Personalized Education Program (PEP)

**Instrument:** Florida Statutes Chapter 1002; Florida Department of Education

**Live Link:** [https://www.fldoe.org/schools/school-choice/k-12-scholarship-programs/personalized-education-program/](https://www.fldoe.org/schools/school-choice/k-12-scholarship-programs/personalized-education-program/)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "The Personalized Education Program provides approximately $8,000 per student annually for educational expenses."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Florida's PEP provides ~$8,000/year per student for homeschool educational expenses, one of the most generous homeschool funding programs in the US.

---

### 5. Portfolio and Record Keeping

**Instrument:** Florida Statutes Section 1002.41(1)(b)

**Live Link:** [https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2023/1002.41](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2023/1002.41)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "The parent shall maintain a portfolio of records and materials. The portfolio shall consist of the following: a log of educational activities... samples of any writings, worksheets, workbooks, or creative materials used or developed by the student."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Parents must maintain a portfolio including a log of educational activities and work samples. The portfolio is kept at home and reviewed only if requested by the superintendent.

## Distinctive State Features

1. **PEP Funding (~$8,000/year):** Florida provides one of the most generous homeschool funding programs in the nation through Personalized Education Program scholarships.

2. **Five Evaluation Options:** Unlike states that mandate standardized testing, Florida allows parents to choose from five evaluation methods, including a simple portfolio review by a certified teacher.

3. **No Required Subjects List:** Florida's home education program does not specify required subjects, giving parents maximum curriculum flexibility.

4. **No Minimum Hours:** No instructional day or hour requirements exist.

5. **Dexter Mosely Act:** Homeschool students in grades 6-12 may participate in public school extracurricular activities, including sports, with 30-day advance notice.
