# Marijuana Laws - Vermont

## At a Glance

| Feature | Status |
|---------|--------|
| Recreational | Yes (H.511 / Act 86, 2018; possession/cultivation legal; retail sales began Oct. 2022 via S.54 / Act 164) |
| Medical | Yes (S.76, 2004; expanded by S.16, 2017) |
| Home Grow | Yes - 2 mature + 4 immature per dwelling unit (recreational); 6 mature + 12 immature (medical) |
| Age | 21+ for recreational; 18+ for medical |
| Possession Limit (Rec) | 2 oz (raised from 1 oz effective July 1, 2026 per S.278) |
| Possession Limit (Med) | 2 oz |
| Tax | 14% cannabis excise tax + 6% state sales tax = 20% total on recreational; medical untaxed |
| Distinctive Feature | Harvest from home grow does NOT count toward possession limit; flavored vape ban; 30% flower / 60% concentrate THC caps; municipalities must opt in to retail |

## Citation Layer

### 1. Recreational Possession and Home Cultivation

**Instrument:** Vermont Statutes Annotated Title 18, Chapter 84, Section 4230e (H.511 / Act 86, 2018)

**Live Link:** [https://legislature.vermont.gov/statutes/section/18/084/04230e](https://legislature.vermont.gov/statutes/section/18/084/04230e)

**Verbatim Quote (offset ~char 100-400):**
> "A person 21 years of age or older who cultivates not more than two mature cannabis plants and four immature cannabis plants shall not be penalized or sanctioned in any manner by the State or any of its political subdivisions or denied any right or privilege under State law."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending. S.278 (2026) raised the possession limit from 1 oz to 2 oz effective July 1, 2026.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Adults 21+ may possess up to 2 oz and cultivate 2 mature + 4 immature plants per dwelling unit.

---

### 2. Harvest Exclusion from Possession Limit

**Instrument:** 18 V.S.A. Section 4230e(a)(3)

**Live Link:** [https://legislature.vermont.gov/statutes/section/18/084/04230e](https://legislature.vermont.gov/statutes/section/18/084/04230e)

**Verbatim Quote (offset ~char 400-600):**
> "Any cannabis harvested from the plants allowed pursuant to this subsection shall not count toward the one-ounce possession limit in section 4230a of this title, provided it is stored in an indoor facility on the property where the cannabis was cultivated and reasonable precautions are taken to prevent unauthorized access to the cannabis."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Cannabis harvested from lawful home grow does not count toward the possession limit, provided it is stored properly on the cultivation property.

---

### 3. Retail Sales and Taxation

**Instrument:** Vermont Statutes Annotated; S.54 / Act 164 (2020)

**Live Link:** [https://tax.vermont.gov/business/cannabis-excise-tax](https://tax.vermont.gov/business/cannabis-excise-tax)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "Vermont imposes a fourteen percent (14%) excise tax on the retail sale of cannabis and cannabis products."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Retail sales subject to 14% excise tax plus 6% state sales tax (20% total). Medical cannabis is untaxed.

---

### 4. Potency Caps

**Instrument:** S.54 / Act 164 (2020); Cannabis Control Board rules

**Live Link:** [https://www.mpp.org/states/vermont/summary-of-s-54-the-bill-to-regulate-and-tax-cannabis-in-vermont/](https://www.mpp.org/states/vermont/summary-of-s-54-the-bill-to-regulate-and-tax-cannabis-in-vermont/)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "Flower could not exceed 30% THC. Solid concentrates could not exceed 60%... A single package could not exceed 50 milligrams of THC... Servings are limited to five milligrams of THC."

**Repeal Check:** S.72 (2026 legislative session) proposed lifting the 60% concentrate cap; bill status pending as of August 2026.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Adult-use flower capped at 30% THC; solid concentrates at 60% THC; single package at 50 mg THC; servings at 5 mg THC. These restrictions do not apply to medical cannabis or home cultivation.

---

### 5. Flavored Vape Ban

**Instrument:** S.54 / Act 164 (2020)

**Live Link:** [https://www.mpp.org/states/vermont/summary-of-s-54-the-bill-to-regulate-and-tax-cannabis-in-vermont/](https://www.mpp.org/states/vermont/summary-of-s-54-the-bill-to-regulate-and-tax-cannabis-in-vermont/)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "Vape cartridges and cannabis flower that contain flavors that are not naturally occurring in cannabis could not be sold."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Flavored vape cartridges and artificially flavored flower are prohibited.

---

### 6. Municipal Opt-In

**Instrument:** S.54 / Act 164 (2020)

**Live Link:** [https://legislature.vermont.gov/statutes/section/18/084/04230e](https://legislature.vermont.gov/statutes/section/18/084/04230e)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "Vermont municipalities retain authority to regulate or prohibit cannabis establishments within their borders. Towns must opt in to allow retail cannabis sales."

**Repeal Check:** The Vermont Growers Association 2026 policy agenda includes replacing retail opt-in with opt-out, but no legislation has passed.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Municipalities must affirmatively opt in to allow retail cannabis sales.

## Distinctive State Features

1. **Harvest Exclusion:** Vermont is one of the few states where home-grown harvest does not count toward the statutory possession limit.

2. **Flavored Vape Ban:** Vermont prohibits the sale of cannabis vape products with non-naturally-occurring flavors.

3. **Strict Potency Caps:** Adult-use flower (30% THC) and concentrate (60% THC) caps are among the most restrictive in legal states.

4. **Municipal Opt-In:** Towns must actively choose to allow retail sales, creating significant geographic gaps in access.

5. **No Per Se DUI Limit:** Vermont does not have a per se THC blood limit for DUI; impairment must be proven by observable evidence.
