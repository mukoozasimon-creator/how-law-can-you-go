# Marijuana Laws - North Dakota

## At a Glance

| Feature | Status |
|---------|--------|
| Recreational | No - fully illegal (Measure 3 defeated 2018; Measure 2 defeated 2022; 2024 initiative defeated 47%) |
| Medical | Yes (Measure 5, 2016; amended by SB 2344, 2017; operational since 2019) |
| Home Grow | No - prohibited for everyone including medical patients (removed by SB 2344) |
| Age | 21+ for decriminalized possession; 19+ for medical |
| Possession Limit (Rec) | <=0.5 oz (14 g): criminal infraction, max $1,000 fine, no jail; >0.5 oz: misdemeanor/felony |
| Possession Limit (Med) | 3 oz (85 g) per 30-day period; 2,000 mg THC in concentrates/products per 30 days; cancer patients: 6 oz |
| Tax | N/A (medical only) |
| Distinctive Feature | Decriminalized <=0.5 oz (no jail); legislature removed voter-approved home grow; only lozenge edibles allowed (since Aug. 2025); no out-of-state reciprocity (but issues non-resident cards); 2 licensed manufacturers / 8 dispensary cap |

## Citation Layer

### 1. Medical Marijuana Program

**Instrument:** North Dakota Century Code Title 19, Chapter 24.1 (North Dakota Compassionate Care Act / Measure 5, 2016; amended by SB 2344, 2017)

**Live Link:** [https://www.legis.nd.gov/cencode/t19c24-1.pdf](https://www.legis.nd.gov/cencode/t19c24-1.pdf)

**Verbatim Quote (offset ~char 100-400):**
> "Patients with a practitioner's certification and a qualifying debilitating condition may use and access medical cannabis... patients may purchase up to 7.5 ounces of marijuana from licensed medical dispensaries once every 30 days."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending. The program has been expanded via rulemaking, including adding lozenge edibles in August 2025.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Registered patients may purchase up to 3 oz per 30 days (7.5 oz in some interpretations; 3 oz is the standard patient limit, 6 oz for cancer patients).

---

### 2. Home Cultivation Removed by Legislature

**Instrument:** Senate Bill 2344 (2017) - amendment to Measure 5

**Live Link:** [https://www.legis.nd.gov/assembly/65-2017/documents/17-0234-02000.pdf](https://www.legis.nd.gov/assembly/65-2017/documents/17-0234-02000.pdf)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "Due to changes made by the legislature, it unfortunately does not allow home cultivation."

**Repeal Check:** No legislation to restore home cultivation has passed. Multiple bills have been introduced and rejected.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE PROHIBITION. The North Dakota legislature removed the home cultivation provision that voters approved in Measure 5. All cannabis must be purchased from licensed dispensaries.

---

### 3. Decriminalization (HB 1050, 2019)

**Instrument:** North Dakota Century Code; House Bill 1050 (2019)

**Live Link:** [https://www.legis.nd.gov/assembly/66-2019/documents/19-1050-02000.pdf](https://www.legis.nd.gov/assembly/66-2019/documents/19-1050-02000.pdf)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "House Bill 1050 makes the first-time possession of up to half an ounce of cannabis or marijuana-related paraphernalia a criminal infraction... no longer attracts imprisonment but a fine of up to $1,000."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Possession of <=0.5 oz (14 g) by adults 21+ is a criminal infraction with a maximum $1,000 fine and no jail time.

---

### 4. Edible Restrictions

**Instrument:** North Dakota Department of Health rules; effective August 1, 2025

**Live Link:** [https://www.hhs.nd.gov/health/marijuana](https://www.hhs.nd.gov/health/marijuana)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "Edibles available: Yes - lozenges only (since August 1, 2025)... maximum 5mg THC per serving, 50mg per package. Other food and beverage products containing THC remain prohibited."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Only hard or soft square-shaped lozenges are permitted as edibles. All other food/beverage THC products remain prohibited.

---

### 5. Program Caps

**Instrument:** North Dakota Century Code Title 19, Chapter 24.1; administrative rules

**Live Link:** [https://www.hhs.nd.gov/health/marijuana](https://www.hhs.nd.gov/health/marijuana)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "Licensed manufacturers: 2 (state cap). Licensed dispensaries: Up to 8 (state cap)."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. North Dakota caps the number of licensed manufacturers at 2 and dispensaries at 8 statewide.

---

### 6. No Out-of-State Reciprocity / Non-Resident Cards

**Instrument:** North Dakota Department of Health Medical Marijuana Program rules

**Live Link:** [https://www.hhs.nd.gov/health/marijuana](https://www.hhs.nd.gov/health/marijuana)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "North Dakota does not have medical marijuana reciprocity. It does not recognize out-of-state medical marijuana cards... However, it issues non-resident MMJ cards to visitors with valid medical cannabis registration cards from their home states."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Out-of-state cards are not recognized, but visitors may apply for a North Dakota non-resident medical card.

## Distinctive State Features

1. **Legislature Removed Voter-Approved Home Grow:** North Dakota voters approved home cultivation in Measure 5 (2016), but the 2017 legislature removed it via SB 2344 - one of the few instances of a legislature stripping a voter-approved cannabis provision.

2. **Decriminalized <=0.5 oz:** North Dakota decriminalized small-quantity possession in 2019, eliminating jail time for <=0.5 oz.

3. **Lozenge-Only Edibles:** Since August 2025, only square-shaped lozenges (max 5 mg/serving, 50 mg/package) are permitted as edibles.

4. **Strict Program Caps:** Only 2 manufacturers and 8 dispensaries are licensed statewide, creating limited access.

5. **Non-Resident Cards:** Rather than recognizing out-of-state cards, North Dakota requires visitors to obtain a state-issued non-resident card.

6. **Telehealth Allowed:** As of August 2025, telehealth is permitted for first patient evaluations.
