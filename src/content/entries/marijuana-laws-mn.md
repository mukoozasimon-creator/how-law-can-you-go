# Marijuana Laws - Minnesota

## Verdict

Minnesota legalized adult-use cannabis on August 1, 2023, under HF 100 (Minnesota Statute Chapter 342, the Cannabis Regulatory Act). Adults 21+ may possess up to 2 oz of flower, 8 g of concentrate, or 800 mg of edible THC in public; up to 2 lbs of flower at a private residence. Home cultivation of up to 8 plants per residence (max 4 flowering) is permitted. A 15% gross receipts tax applies to retail sales.

## Named Instruments

- **MN Statute Chapter 342** - Cannabis Regulatory Act (HF 100, 2023)
- **MN Statute 342.09** - Possession and personal use limits
- **MN Statute 342.27** - Home cultivation
- **MN Statute 295.81** - Cannabis gross receipts tax (15% as of July 1, 2025)

## Possession Limits

| Product Type | Public Limit | Private/Home Limit |
|--------------|-------------|-------------------|
| Flower | 2 oz | 2 lbs |
| Concentrate | 8 g | No separate cap beyond home limit |
| Edible THC | 800 mg | No separate cap beyond home limit |
| Plants | N/A | 8 per residence (max 4 flowering) |

## Penalty Tiers (Over-Limit Possession)

Possession over statutory limits may result in civil penalties or criminal charges depending on quantity and circumstances. Specific penalty tiers are codified in MN Statute 342.09 et seq.

## Medical Program

- **Status:** Active; integrated into the adult-use framework
- **Qualifying conditions:** Cancer, glaucoma, HIV/AIDS, Tourette syndrome, ALS, seizures, severe muscle spasms, Crohn's disease, terminal illness, intractable pain, PTSD, autism, sickle cell disease, chronic pain, and others
- **Patient registry:** Required for medical tax exemptions and higher limits
- **Home grow:** Same as recreational (8 plants, 4 flowering)

## Home Cultivation

- **Limit:** Up to 8 cannabis plants per residence for personal use, with a maximum of 4 flowering plants at one time
- **Requirements:** Must occur in a secure space not visible to the public and out of reach of minors
- **No registration required** for personal home grow

## DUI / Impairment

- Minnesota prohibits driving under the influence of cannabis.
- No specific per se THC blood limit is established; impairment is determined by officer observation and chemical testing.
- Refusal to submit to chemical testing carries license revocation penalties under implied consent laws.

## Employment Protections

- Minnesota provides employment protections for off-duty cannabis use.
- Employers may not discriminate against applicants or employees based on lawful cannabis use outside of work, with exceptions for safety-sensitive positions and federal contractors.
- Employers retain the right to prohibit on-duty use and impairment.

## Federal Conflict

- Cannabis remains a Schedule I controlled substance under federal law (21 U.S.C. Section 812).
- Interstate transport of cannabis is prohibited regardless of state legality.
- Cannabis purchased in Minnesota must be consumed in Minnesota.
