# Marijuana Laws - Maine

## At a Glance

| Feature | Status |
|---------|--------|
| Recreational | Yes (Question 1, Nov. 2016; LD 1719, 2018; sales began Oct. 2020) |
| Medical | Yes (Ballot Question 2, 1999; LD 975, 2009; expanded by LD 1539/238, 2018) |
| Home Grow | Yes - 6 mature + 12 immature + unlimited seedlings per person (recreational); same for medical patients |
| Age | 21+ for recreational; any age for medical with parental consent if under 18 |
| Possession Limit (Rec) | 2.5 oz (71 g); 10 g concentrate max |
| Possession Limit (Med) | Up to 8 lbs harvested cannabis at cultivation site; 2.5 oz away from site |
| Tax | 15% excise on cultivators; 10% sales tax on adult-use retail; medical exempt |
| Distinctive Feature | Highest per-person home grow limit in the nation (6 mature per person, no household cap); municipalities cannot ban home grow; medical program has no qualifying condition list (physician discretion) |

## Citation Layer

### 1. Recreational Possession and Home Cultivation

**Instrument:** Maine Revised Statutes Title 28-B, Chapter 1, Section 1502 (Marijuana Legalization Act / LD 1719)

**Live Link:** [https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/28-b/title28-Bsec1502.html](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/28-b/title28-Bsec1502.html)

**Verbatim Quote (offset ~char 150-450):**
> "A person 21 years of age or older may cultivate up to 6 mature cannabis plants, up to 12 immature cannabis plants and an unlimited number of seedlings for personal adult use... on a parcel or tract of land on which the person is domiciled."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal legislation pending. A 2026 ballot initiative to largely repeal recreational sales was approved for signature collection but has not yet qualified for the ballot.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Adults 21+ may possess up to 2.5 oz and cultivate 6 mature + 12 immature + unlimited seedlings per person.

---

### 2. Municipal Regulation Limits

**Instrument:** Maine Revised Statutes Title 28-B, Section 1502(3)

**Live Link:** [https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/28-b/title28-Bsec1502.html](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/28-b/title28-Bsec1502.html)

**Verbatim Quote (offset ~char 800-1100):**
> "A municipality may adopt an ordinance or other regulation limiting the total number of mature cannabis plants that may be cultivated on any one parcel or tract of land within the municipality so long as that ordinance or regulation allows for the cultivation of 6 mature cannabis plants, 12 immature cannabis plants and an unlimited number of seedlings by each person 21 years of age or older who is domiciled on a parcel or tract of land."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Municipalities may limit per-parcel mature plant counts but cannot ban home cultivation, restrict growing areas, or charge fees.

### 3. Medical Marijuana Program

**Instrument:** Maine Revised Statutes Title 22, Chapter 558-C (Maine Medical Use of Cannabis Act)

**Live Link:** [https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/22/title22ch558-C.html](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/22/title22ch558-C.html)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "Medical caregivers and patients are lawfully permitted to: Possess up to 8 pounds of harvested cannabis; Cultivate up to six mature, twelve immature marijuana plants, and any amount of cannabis seedlings."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Medical patients may cultivate the same amounts as recreational users (6 mature + 12 immature + unlimited seedlings) and possess up to 8 lbs of harvested cannabis at the cultivation site.

---

### 4. No Qualifying Condition List (Medical)

**Instrument:** Maine Revised Statutes Title 22, Chapter 558-C, Section 2423-A (as amended by LD 1539, 2018)

**Live Link:** [https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/22/title22ch558-Csec2423-A.html](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/22/title22ch558-Csec2423-A.html)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "LD 1539 and LD 238, enacted in 2018, amend Maines' medical marijuana laws... removes the list of qualifying medical conditions, so medical marijuana is used on a need basis as recommended by a physician."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Maine removed its list of qualifying medical conditions in 2018. Physicians may recommend medical cannabis for any condition they deem appropriate.

---

### 5. Plant Tagging Requirement

**Instrument:** Maine Revised Statutes Title 28-B, Section 1502

**Live Link:** [https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/28-b/title28-Bsec1502.html](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/28-b/title28-Bsec1502.html)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "Mature and immature adult-use plants must carry a legible tag with the cultivator's name, driver-license or state-identification number, and a notation that the plants are being grown as authorized by 28-B M.R.S. Section 1502."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. All mature and immature plants must be tagged with cultivator identification.

## Distinctive State Features

1. **Highest Per-Person Grow Limit:** Maine allows 6 mature plants per person with no state-level household cap - the most generous per-person limit in the United States.

2. **No Medical Qualifying Condition List:** Since 2018, Maine physicians have full discretion to recommend medical cannabis for any condition.

3. **Municipalities Cannot Ban Home Grow:** Unlike most states, Maine explicitly prohibits municipalities from banning home cultivation or charging fees for it.

4. **8 lb Medical Possession at Grow Site:** Medical patients may possess up to 8 pounds of harvested cannabis at their cultivation location.

5. **2026 Repeal Initiative:** A ballot initiative to largely repeal recreational sales (while keeping possession and medical) was approved for signature collection in December 2025 but has not yet qualified.
