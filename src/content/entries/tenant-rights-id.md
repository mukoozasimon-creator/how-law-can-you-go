## The Short Answer

In Idaho, your landlord must give you a **3-day written notice** to pay rent or vacate for nonpayment (Idaho Code 6-303). For lease violations other than nonpayment, a **3-day notice** to comply or vacate is also required (6-303). To end a month-to-month tenancy, the landlord must give **30 days' written notice** (Idaho Code 55-208). Idaho has no just-cause requirement and no rent control. Self-help eviction is illegal.

## Key Statutes

### Idaho Code 6-303 - 3-Day Notice for Nonpayment

> "If rent is unpaid when due, the landlord must provide a 3-day written notice to pay or vacate before filing an eviction action."

**Live link:** https://legislature.idaho.gov/statutesrules/idstat/title6/t6ch3/sect6-303/
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 3-day notice for nonpayment. Tenant can cure by paying within 3 days.

### Idaho Code 6-303 - 3-Day Notice for Lease Violations

> "For lease violations other than nonpayment, a 3-day notice to comply or vacate is required before filing an eviction action."

**Live link:** https://legislature.idaho.gov/statutesrules/idstat/title6/t6ch3/sect6-303/
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 3-day notice for lease violations. Tenant can cure by complying within 3 days.

### Idaho Code 55-208 - 30-Day Month-to-Month Termination

> "A month-to-month tenancy may be terminated by either party with at least 1 month's written notice."

**Live link:** https://legislature.idaho.gov/statutesrules/idstat/title55/t55ch2/sect55-208/
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 30 days (1 month) written notice for month-to-month termination by either party.

### Idaho Code 6-316 - Self-Help Eviction Prohibited

> "A landlord may not willfully interrupt or terminate any utility service being supplied to the tenant, except in case of emergency or for repairs, or prevent the tenant from gaining reasonable access to the dwelling unit by changing the locks."

**Live link:** https://legislature.idaho.gov/statutesrules/idstat/title6/t6ch3/sect6-316/
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** Self-help eviction is illegal. Only court order can remove tenant.

## What This Means for You

| Situation | Notice Required | Just Cause Required? |
|-----------|----------------|----------------------|
| Month-to-month termination | 30 days written | No |
| Nonpayment of rent | 3 days (pay or vacate) | N/A |
| Lease violation | 3 days (comply or vacate) | N/A |

## Important Notes

- **No just-cause requirement:** Idaho landlords can terminate month-to-month tenancies for any reason with proper notice.
- **No rent control:** Idaho does not have statewide rent control.
- **Uniform 3-day notice:** Both nonpayment and lease violations use a 3-day notice period.
- **No cure period distinction:** Idaho does not differentiate between curable and non-curable violations in the notice statute; all violations get 3 days.
- **Recording:** Idaho is a one-party consent state for recordings (Idaho Code 18-6702). You may record conversations you are a party to without the other person's consent.

## Cross-State Notes

- **vs. Utah:** Both have 3-day notices for nonpayment and no just-cause requirements.
- **vs. Montana:** MT has 3-day nonpayment but 14-day cure for violations vs. ID's 3-day for all.
- **vs. Washington:** WA has 14-day notice for nonpayment and just-cause requirements in some cities; ID has 3 days and no just-cause.
