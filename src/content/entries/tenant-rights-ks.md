## The Short Answer

In Kansas, your landlord must give you a **3-day written notice** to pay rent or the lease terminates (KSA 58-2564(b)). For material lease violations, a **14-day notice** to cure is required, with lease termination in **30 days** if uncured (KSA 58-2564(a)). To end a month-to-month tenancy, the landlord must give **30 days' written notice** (KSA 58-2570). Kansas has no just-cause requirement and no rent control. Self-help eviction is illegal.

## Key Statutes

### KSA 58-2564(b) - 3-Day Notice for Nonpayment

> "If rent is unpaid when due, the landlord may terminate after giving written notice of nonpayment and intent to terminate, and the tenant fails to pay within three consecutive 24-hour periods."

**Live link:** http://www.kslegislature.org/li/statute/058_000_0000_chapter/058_025_0000_article/058_025_0064_section/058_025_0064_k/
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 3-day notice for nonpayment. Tenant can cure by paying within 3 days.

### KSA 58-2564(a) - 14/30-Day Notice for Lease Violations

> "If the tenant materially violates the rental agreement or engages in noncompliance materially affecting health and safety, the landlord may give written notice specifying the breach and stating the rental agreement terminates in 30 days unless the breach is remedied within 14 days."

**Live link:** http://www.kslegislature.org/li/statute/058_000_0000_chapter/058_025_0000_article/058_025_0064_section/058_025_0064_k/
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 14 days to cure, 30 days to quit. Second same violation within 6 months = 14-day unconditional notice.

### KSA 58-2564(a) - 14-Day Notice for Repeated Violations

> "If substantially the same violation recurs within six months, the landlord may terminate upon at least 14 days' written notice specifying the breach, and the tenant does not have a right to remedy the recurrence."

**Live link:** http://www.kslegislature.org/li/statute/058_000_0000_chapter/058_025_0000_article/058_025_0064_section/058_025_0064_k/
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** No cure right for repeated violations within 6 months.

### KSA 58-2570 - 30-Day Month-to-Month Termination

> "The landlord or the tenant may terminate a month-to-month tenancy by a written notice given to the other at least 30 days before the periodic rental date specified in the notice."

**Live link:** http://www.kslegislature.org/li/statute/058_000_0000_chapter/058_025_0000_article/058_025_0070_section/058_025_0070_k/
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 30 days written notice for month-to-month termination by either party.

### KSA 58-2550 - Security Deposits

**Key rules:**
- Maximum: **1 month's rent** for unfurnished, **1.5 months' rent** for furnished.
- Pet deposit: Up to **one-half month's rent**.
- Return deadline: **30 days** after tenant vacates with forwarding address.
- Must provide itemized statement if deductions made.

**Live link:** http://www.kslegislature.org/li/statute/058_000_0000_chapter/058_025_0000_article/058_025_0050_section/058_025_0050_k/
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 30-day return. 1 month cap unfurnished, 1.5 months furnished.

### KSA 58-2563 - Self-Help Eviction Prohibited

> "A landlord may not willfully exclude a tenant from possession by changing locks, shutting off utilities, or removing doors. Violations subject landlord to actual damages plus up to one and one-half months' periodic rent and reasonable attorney's fees."

**Live link:** http://www.kslegislature.org/li/statute/058_000_0000_chapter/058_025_0000_article/058_025_0063_section/058_025_0063_k/
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** Self-help eviction is illegal. Only court order can remove tenant.

## What This Means for You

| Situation | Notice Required | Just Cause Required? |
|-----------|----------------|----------------------|
| Month-to-month termination | 30 days written | No |
| Nonpayment of rent | 3 days (pay or quit) | N/A |
| Lease violation | 14 days cure / 30 days quit | N/A |
| Repeated violation (within 6 months) | 14 days (no cure) | N/A |

## Important Notes

- **No just-cause requirement:** Kansas landlords can terminate month-to-month tenancies for any reason with proper notice.
- **No rent control:** Kansas does not have statewide rent control.
- **Two-step notice structure:** Kansas uses a termination notice under the landlord-tenant act, followed by a separate 3-day notice to leave before filing suit (KSA 61-3803).
- **Service:** Personal delivery, substituted service on person of suitable age with mailing, or post-and-mail.
- **Recording:** Kansas is a one-party consent state for recordings (KSA 22-2517). You may record conversations you are a party to without the other person's consent.

## Cross-State Notes

- **vs. Missouri:** Both have similar 3-day/14-day frameworks and no just-cause requirements.
- **vs. Nebraska:** NE requires 7 days for nonpayment vs. KS's 3 days. NE has a 14/30-day structure for violations similar to KS.
- **vs. Oklahoma:** OK has a 5-day notice for nonpayment vs. KS's 3 days.
