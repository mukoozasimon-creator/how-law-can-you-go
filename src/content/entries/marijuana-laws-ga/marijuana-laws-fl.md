# Marijuana Laws - Florida

## One-Sentence Verdict

Florida permits medical marijuana for registered patients with qualifying conditions but recreational marijuana remains illegal; the 2024 Amendment 3 failed to reach the 60% threshold required for passage, and no recreational initiative qualified for the 2026 ballot.

## Medical Program

- **Program name**: Florida Medical Marijuana Program (administered by OMMU - Office of Medical Marijuana Use).
- **Qualifying conditions**: Cancer, epilepsy, glaucoma, HIV/AIDS, PTSD, ALS, Crohn's disease, Parkinson's, MS, comparable conditions, terminal conditions, chronic nonmalignant pain.
- **Product types**: Oils, tinctures, edibles, vaporization, topicals, smokable flower (since 2019).
- **Possession limit**: 70-day supply as determined by physician; up to 4 oz of smokable flower per 70-day period.
- **Registration**: Patient must be a Florida resident and obtain a Medical Marijuana Use Registry ID card.
- **No reciprocity**: Out-of-state medical cards are NOT recognized.
- **No home cultivation**: Patients cannot grow their own medicine.
- **Smoking ban lifted**: Gov. DeSantis signed SB 182 in March 2019, allowing smokable medical marijuana.

---

## Hemp and CBD

- **Hemp**: Legal under federal Farm Bill; Florida adopted hemp program in 2019.
- **Delta-9 THC limit**: 0.3% by dry weight.
- **Delta-8, Delta-10**: In legal gray area; SB 1698 (2024) that would have banned delta-8 was vetoed by Gov. DeSantis.
- **Federal change (Nov 2025)**: Federal spending bill closed Farm Bill loophole, effectively banning most hemp-derived consumer products including CBD, with a one-year grace period.

---

## DUI / Impairment

- **Standard**: It is illegal to drive under the influence of any controlled substance (Fla. Stat. Sec. 316.193).
- **Medical card NOT a defense**: Having a medical marijuana card does not protect against DUI charges.
- **Penalties**: First offense - up to 6 months jail, $500-$1,000 fine, license suspension 6 months-1 year.

---

## Employment Protections

- **No statutory protection**: Florida does not protect off-duty medical or recreational cannabis use.
- **At-will employment**: Employers may terminate for cannabis use.
- **Medical patients**: No employment protections for medical marijuana patients under current law.

---

## Federal Conflict Notes

- **Schedule I federally**: All marijuana remains Schedule I under federal law.
- **Federal property**: Illegal on federal land, including national parks and military bases in Florida.
- **Federal housing**: Section 8 and public housing may prohibit medical marijuana use.

---

## Distinctive State Features

1. **60% ballot threshold**: Florida requires 60% voter approval for constitutional amendments. Amendment 3 (2024) received 56% and failed.
2. **No home cultivation**: Even medical patients cannot grow their own medicine - one of the few medical states with this restriction.
3. **Mandatory minimums for trafficking**: Florida has some of the harshest marijuana trafficking penalties in the nation.
4. **Concentrates = felony**: Any amount of concentrate is a 3rd degree felony.
5. **2026 ballot failure**: The recreational initiative failed to qualify for the 2026 ballot after signature verification issues.

## Recording Consent Rules

Florida is a **two-party consent** state for audio recordings. Fla. Stat. Sec. 934.03 makes it a felony to record a private communication without the consent of all parties.

---

## Practical Notes

- Even small amounts of recreational marijuana can result in arrest and jail time in Florida.
- Vape pens and edibles containing THC are felonies regardless of amount.
- If you have a medical card from another state, it is NOT valid in Florida.
- Medical patients must purchase only from licensed Medical Marijuana Treatment Centers (MMTCs).
- National parks and beaches in Florida are federal property - cannabis is illegal there even with a medical card.
