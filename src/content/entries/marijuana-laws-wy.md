## Citation Layer

### 1. General Prohibition

**Instrument:** Wyoming Statutes Annotated Title 35, Chapter 7, Section 35-7-1001 et seq. (Wyoming Controlled Substances Act)

**Live Link:** [https://www.wyoleg.gov/Laws/Statutes/Title35/Chapter7](https://www.wyoleg.gov/Laws/Statutes/Title35/Chapter7)

**Verbatim Quote (offset ~char 100-300):**
> "Possession of three ounces or less is a misdemeanor punishable by no more than 12 months imprisonment and a maximum fine of $1,000, or both."

**Repeal Check:** No legalization or decriminalization bills have advanced in the 2023, 2024, 2025, or 2026 legislative sessions. Wyoming lacks a ballot initiative process for statutory changes, making voter-driven reform impossible without legislative action.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Cannabis containing more than 0.3% THC remains fully prohibited for all purposes.

### 2. Possession Penalties

**Instrument:** Wyoming Statutes Annotated Section 35-7-1031 (Possession of controlled substances)

**Live Link:** [https://www.wyoleg.gov/Laws/Statutes/Title35/Chapter7/Section35-7-1031](https://www.wyoleg.gov/Laws/Statutes/Title35/Chapter7/Section35-7-1031)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "3 oz or less: Misdemeanor, 12 months, $1,000. More than 3 oz: Felony, 5 years, $10,000."

**Repeal Check:** No changes pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Possession of 3 oz or less is a misdemeanor; more than 3 oz is a felony.

### 4. CBD-Only Exception (Intractable Epilepsy)

**Instrument:** Wyoming Statutes Annotated Section 35-7-1901 et seq. (Hemp extract / HB 32, 2015)

**Live Link:** [https://www.wyoleg.gov/Laws/Statutes/Title35/Chapter7/Section35-7-1901](https://www.wyoleg.gov/Laws/Statutes/Title35/Chapter7/Section35-7-1901)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "HB 32 allowed the use of hemp extract with no more than 0.3% THC and at least 5% CBD for intractable epilepsy patients with a physician's recommendation, but this narrow exception did not create a dispensing framework."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending; the 2018 Farm Bill superseded much of this framework for hemp-derived products.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE IN FORM ONLY. The 2015 CBD exception created an affirmative defense but established no legal supply chain. Hemp-derived CBD products with <=0.3% THC are now legal under federal and state hemp legislation.

## Distinctive State Features

1. **No Ballot Initiative Process:** Wyoming is one of the few states with no citizen initiative process for statutory changes, meaning cannabis reform can only occur through the legislature.

2. **No Functional Medical Program:** Wyoming is one of approximately 10 states with no functional medical cannabis program. The 2015 HB 32 created only an affirmative defense for CBD possession, not a dispensing system.

3. **Carrier Agent Rule for Edibles:** Wyoming law applies the total weight of infused edibles rather than THC content. A single brownie weighing over 3 ounces can trigger a felony charge regardless of actual THC concentration.

4. **Drugged Driving:** First offense DUI-marijuana carries up to 6 months imprisonment, a $750 fine, and license revocation.
