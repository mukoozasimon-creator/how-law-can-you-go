## The Short Answer

In West Virginia, your landlord generally does **NOT** need to give a separate pre-suit notice before filing for eviction for nonpayment or lease violations. Instead, the landlord files a **petition for wrongful occupation** (WV Code 55-3A-1), and the petition served with a notice of hearing serves as the tenant's notice. The court must schedule a hearing **not less than 5 nor more than 10 judicial days** after filing. To end a month-to-month tenancy, the landlord must give **1 full rental period's notice** (WV Code 37-6-5). West Virginia has no just-cause requirement and no rent control. Self-help eviction is illegal.

## Key Statutes

### WV Code 55-3A-1 - Wrongful Occupation Petition (No Pre-Suit Notice)

> "A landlord may file a verified petition for wrongful occupation of residential rental property. The petition itself carries the notice to the tenant. The court must schedule a hearing not less than five nor more than ten judicial days after filing."

**Live link:** https://code.wvlegislature.gov/55-3A-1/
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** No separate pre-suit notice required for nonpayment or lease breaches. The petition and hearing notice serve as the tenant's notice. This is a distinctive feature of West Virginia law.

### WV Code 55-3A-1 - Grounds for Wrongful Occupation

> "Grounds include: nonpayment of rent, breach of a lease provision, deliberate or negligent damage to the property, or holdover after lease expiration."

**Live link:** https://code.wvlegislature.gov/55-3A-1/
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** Enumerated grounds for eviction. Landlord must prove one of these grounds at hearing.

### WV Code 37-6-5 - 1 Full Rental Period for Month-to-Month Termination

> "A periodic tenancy of less than one year may be terminated by notice for one full rental period before the end of any period."

**Live link:** https://code.wvlegislature.gov/37-6-5/
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 1 full rental period (e.g., 1 month for month-to-month) for no-cause termination.

### WV Code 55-3A-3 - Judgment of Possession

> "If the court finds in favor of the landlord, it enters a judgment of possession. The order of possession is enforced through the appropriate officer rather than by self-help."

**Live link:** https://code.wvlegislature.gov/55-3A-3/
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** Only court order can remove tenant. Self-help is prohibited.

## Important Notes

- **No just-cause requirement:** West Virginia landlords can terminate month-to-month tenancies for any reason with proper notice.
- **No rent control:** West Virginia does not have statewide rent control.
- **No pre-suit notice:** Unlike nearly every other state, WV does not require a statutory notice to quit before filing for nonpayment. The petition and hearing notice serve as the tenant's first formal notice.
- **Fast timeline:** Hearing must be scheduled within 5-10 judicial days of filing. This is one of the fastest eviction timelines in the country.
- **Service:** Must be served under Rule 4 of WV Rules of Civil Procedure or by certified mail, return receipt requested.
- **Recording:** West Virginia is a one-party consent state for recordings (WV Code 62-1D-3). You may record conversations you are a party to without the other person's consent.

## Cross-State Notes

- **vs. Virginia:** VA requires 5-day notice for nonpayment; WV requires none. Both have no just-cause requirement.
- **vs. Kentucky:** KY requires 7-day notice for nonpayment; WV requires none.
- **vs. Ohio:** OH requires 3-day notice for nonpayment; WV requires none.
