## Summary

Kentucky does not have a dedicated homeschool statute. Instead, homeschools operate as private schools under KRS 159.030 and 159.040. The requirements are straightforward: file an annual Private School Notice of Attendance with the local board of education within the first two weeks of the school year, provide instruction in required subjects, maintain attendance and scholarship (report card) records, and operate for at least 185 days. Kentucky requires no parent qualifications, no standardized testing, no curriculum approval, and no home visits. The state treats homeschools with the same legal standing as any other private school.

## Legal Framework

### Primary Statute

**KRS 159.030** - Exemptions from compulsory attendance

> "The provisions of KRS 159.010 shall not apply to any child... who is enrolled and in regular attendance in a private, parochial, or church regular day school..."

- **Instrument**: KRS 159.030
- **Live Link**: https://www.lrc.ky.gov/statutes/statute.aspx?id=35688
- **Repeal Check**: Active as of 2026; no repeal legislation pending
- **Verdict**: Private school enrollment exempts children from compulsory public school attendance.

### Private School Definition

**KRS 159.040** - Definition of "private school"

> Defines private schools to include "schools that are privately owned and operated, including home schools."

- **Instrument**: KRS 159.040
- **Live Link**: https://www.lrc.ky.gov/statutes/statute.aspx?id=35689
- **Repeal Check**: Active as of 2026
- **Verdict**: Homeschools are classified as private schools with the same legal autonomy.

### Compulsory Attendance

**KRS 159.010** - Compulsory school attendance

> Establishes that children ages 6-18 must attend school.

- **Instrument**: KRS 159.010
- **Live Link**: https://www.lrc.ky.gov/statutes/statute.aspx?id=35686
- **Repeal Check**: Active as of 2026
- **Verdict**: Establishes compulsory attendance ages and the private school exemption.

## Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| **Notification** | Annual Private School Notice within 2 weeks of school year |
| **Teacher Qualifications** | None required |
| **Required Subjects** | Reading, writing, spelling, grammar, history, math, science, civics |
| **Testing** | None required |
| **Record-Keeping** | Attendance and scholarship (report card) records |
| **Attendance** | 185 days (or 170 days / 1,062 hours) |
| **Compulsory Ages** | 6-18 |

## Notification

**Annual Private School Notice of Attendance** must be submitted to the local board of education within the **first two weeks** of the school year (traditionally by the second week of August). The notice should include:

- Names and ages of children being homeschooled
- Address where instruction occurs
- Parent/guardian information
- Statement of intent to operate a private school

This is an informational filing, not a request for approval. The board of education cannot deny the right to operate a private school.

## Required Subjects

Under Kentucky's private school framework, instruction must include:

1. Reading
2. Writing
3. Spelling
4. English Grammar
5. History
6. Mathematics
7. Science
8. Civics

The state does not approve curriculum or evaluate teaching methods.

## Assessment & Testing

No standardized testing or assessments are required. Kentucky does not mandate any testing for private schools, including homeschools.

## Record-Keeping

Parents must maintain:
- **Attendance records** - documenting days of instruction
- **Scholarship reports** (report cards) - documenting educational progress, updated at intervals similar to local public schools (typically every 6-9 weeks)

Records are kept at home and only submitted if the superintendent requests them.

## Special Provisions

- **KEES Scholarship**: Kentucky Educational Excellence Scholarship may be available to homeschoolers who meet specific criteria; check with KHEAA for current requirements
- **Sports/Activities**: No statutory right to public school extracurricular participation
- **Driver's License**: No specific homeschool provisions
- **Work Permits**: No specific homeschool provisions
- **Transfer Students**: When returning to public school, placement is determined by age and district assessment

## Distinctive Features

- **No dedicated homeschool statute** - operates under private school law
- Same legal standing as any private school in Kentucky
- 185-day attendance requirement (above the 180-day standard)
- "Scholarship reports" (report cards) required at 6-9 week intervals
- No testing, no parent qualifications, no curriculum approval
- Notification is informational, not a request for permission
- Strong legal protections - board cannot deny private school operation
