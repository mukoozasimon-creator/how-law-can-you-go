# Homeschooling Laws - Illinois

## At a Glance

| Feature | Status |
|---------|--------|
| Legal Status | Yes - homeschools recognized as private schools (*People v. Levisen*, 1950) |
| Notification Required | No - zero notification to any government agency |
| Teacher Qualifications | None required |
| Required Subjects | 6: language arts, math, science, social science, fine arts, physical development/health |
| Testing Required | No |
| Instructional Hours | None specified |
| Compulsory Ages | 6-17 |
| Distinctive Feature | No notification, no testing, no approval - one of the easiest states to homeschool; $750 education expense tax credit; *People v. Levisen* (1950) established homeschool = private school |

## Citation Layer

### 1. Legal Framework - Homeschools as Private Schools

**Instrument:** 105 ILCS 5/26-1 (Compulsory Attendance Law); *People v. Levisen*, 404 Ill. 574, 90 N.E.2d 213 (1950)

**Live Link:** [https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/ilcs3.asp?ActID=1005&ChapAct=105%20ILCS%205/&ChapterID=17&ChapterName=SCHOOLS&SectionID=31405&SeqStart=9750000&SeqEnd=10400000](https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/ilcs3.asp?ActID=1005&ChapAct=105%20ILCS%205/&ChapterID=17&ChapterName=SCHOOLS&SectionID=31405&SeqStart=9750000&SeqEnd=10400000)

**Verbatim Quote (offset ~char 100-400):**
> "The object of the law is that all children shall be educated, not that they shall be educated in any particular manner or place."

**Repeal Check:** HB 2827 (introduced Feb. 2025) would require annual notification, documentation, and assessment. **This bill has NOT been enacted.** Current law remains in effect.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. The Illinois Supreme Court held in *Levisen* that a home qualifies as a private school and that compulsory education does not mandate any particular educational setting.

### 2. No Notification Required

**Instrument:** 105 ILCS 5/26-1; 105 ILCS 5/27-1

**Live Link:** [https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/ilcs3.asp?ActID=1005&ChapAct=105%20ILCS%205/&ChapterID=17&ChapterName=SCHOOLS&SectionID=31405&SeqStart=9750000&SeqEnd=10400000](https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/ilcs3.asp?ActID=1005&ChapAct=105%20ILCS%205/&ChapterID=17&ChapterName=SCHOOLS&SectionID=31405&SeqStart=9750000&SeqEnd=10400000)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "Illinois requires no notification to any government agency to homeschool. You do not need to tell the school district, the state, or anyone else."

**Repeal Check:** HB 2827 pending but not enacted.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Illinois is one of the most hands-off states. No notification, registration, approval, or reporting required.

### 3. Required Subjects

**Instrument:** 105 ILCS 5/27-1 (Branches of Education)

**Live Link:** [https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/ilcs3.asp?ActID=1005&ChapAct=105%20ILCS%205/&ChapterID=17&ChapterName=SCHOOLS&SectionID=31406&SeqStart=10400001&SeqEnd=11200000](https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/ilcs3.asp?ActID=1005&ChapAct=105%20ILCS%205/&ChapterID=17&ChapterName=SCHOOLS&SectionID=31406&SeqStart=10400001&SeqEnd=11200000)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "Language arts (reading, writing, grammar, spelling), mathematics, science (biological and physical), social science (social studies, U.S. history, geography, government/civics), fine arts, physical development and health."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Homeschools must teach the branches of education taught in public schools, in English.

### 4. Education Expense Credit

**Instrument:** Illinois Department of Revenue; 35 ILCS 5/201(m)

**Live Link:** [https://www2.illinois.gov/rev/Pages/Tax-Credits.aspx](https://www2.illinois.gov/rev/Pages/Tax-Credits.aspx)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "The Education Expense Credit is worth up to $750 per family for qualifying curriculum and educational material expenses."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Illinois offers one of the only direct tax credits for homeschool educational expenses in the US, up to $750 per family.

## Distinctive State Features

1. **Zero Notification:** Illinois requires absolutely no contact with any government agency to homeschool. Simply begin teaching the required subjects.

2. **$750 Tax Credit:** One of the few states providing direct financial support to homeschoolers through the Education Expense Credit.

3. ***Levisen* Precedent (1950):** One of the earliest and most influential court decisions establishing that homeschools qualify as private schools.

4. **No Testing, No Hours, No Qualifications:** No standardized testing, no minimum instructional hours, no parent qualifications required.

5. **HB 2827 Threat:** A pending bill would dramatically increase regulation. The homeschool community is actively monitoring this legislation.
