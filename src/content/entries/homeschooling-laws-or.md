## Summary

Oregon homeschools operate under ORS 339.035. Parents must file a one-time written notice with the local Education Service District (ESD) within 10 days of withdrawing a child from public school or beginning home education. Oregon has no parent qualification requirements, no mandated subjects, no minimum instructional hours, and no curriculum approval. The primary accountability mechanism is standardized testing at grades 3, 5, 8, and 10, administered by a qualified neutral person. Oregon also guarantees homeschoolers the right to take public school courses and participate in interscholastic activities. An alternative private school pathway (ORS 339.030) avoids testing requirements entirely.

## Legal Framework

### Primary Statute

**ORS 339.035** - Teaching by private teacher, parent or guardian

> "When a child is taught or is withdrawn from a public school to be taught by a parent, legal guardian or private teacher, as provided in ORS 339.030, the parent, legal guardian or private teacher must notify the education service district in writing."

- **Instrument**: ORS 339.035
- **Live Link**: https://www.oregonlegislature.gov/bills_laws/ors/ors339.html
- **Repeal Check**: Active as of 2026; no repeal legislation pending
- **Verdict**: One-time ESD notification is required; testing at grades 3, 5, 8, and 10.

### Private School Alternative

**ORS 339.030(1)(a)** - Private school exemption

> Establishes that children attending private schools are exempt from compulsory public school attendance.

- **Instrument**: ORS 339.030(1)(a)
- **Live Link**: https://www.oregonlegislature.gov/bills_laws/ors/ors339.html
- **Repeal Check**: Active as of 2026
- **Verdict**: Private school enrollment avoids ESD notification and testing requirements.

## Notification

**One-Time Written Notice** must be submitted to the local Education Service District (ESD) within **10 days** of:
- Withdrawing a child from public school to homeschool, OR
- Beginning home education for a child not previously enrolled in public school

If moving to a new ESD, notify the new ESD in writing. The ESD must acknowledge receipt in writing.

**Note**: This is a one-time notification. Annual renewals are not required.

## Required Subjects

No subjects are mandated by Oregon law for homeschoolers. Complete curriculum freedom is granted.

## Assessment & Testing

Students must be examined at grades **3, 5, 8, and 10**:

- The parent selects an examination from the State Board of Education's approved list of comprehensive examinations
- The test must be administered by a **qualified neutral person** (not the parent)
- Approved tests are nationally normed standardized achievement tests (e.g., ITBS, Stanford, CAT, MAT)
- **18-month grace period**: New homeschoolers have 18 months before the first test is required
- If a student scores below the **15th percentile** on any subtest, the ESD may require additional educational services or further evaluation (discretionary)

**Private School Pathway**: Students enrolled in a private school (ORS 339.030(1)(a)) are not subject to ESD notification or testing requirements.

## Record-Keeping

No records are required by law. Recommended documentation:
- ESD notification and acknowledgment
- Test results
- Work samples (especially for high school transcripts)

## Special Provisions

- **Public School Courses**: Under ORS 339.035, homeschool students have a statutory right to take individual courses at the local public school at no cost
- **Sports Access**: Homeschool students have a statutory right to participate in interscholastic activities at the local public school, with the same eligibility rules as full-time students. Must demonstrate academic eligibility by scoring at or above the 23rd percentile on an approved examination.
- **Special Education**: Districts must proactively offer special education services when they learn a homeschooled child has a disability; must hold an IEP meeting
- **No Recordkeeping Mandate**: Oregon has no statutory recordkeeping obligations

## Distinctive Features

- **One-time notification** (no annual renewals)
- No required subjects, no minimum hours, no parent qualifications
- Testing only at 4 grade levels (3, 5, 8, 10) - not annual
- 18-month grace period for new homeschoolers before first test
- **Statutory right to public school courses and sports**
- Private school pathway completely avoids testing
- Test administered by qualified neutral person (not parent)
- 15th percentile trigger for potential ESD intervention
