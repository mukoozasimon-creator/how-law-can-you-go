# DUI / Implied Consent - West Virginia

## Verdict

West Virginia's implied consent law (W.Va. Code 17C-5A-2) requires that any person operating a motor vehicle in the state automatically consents to a preliminary breath test (PBT) and a secondary chemical test (blood, breath, or urine) if lawfully arrested for DUI. A first refusal of the secondary test results in a **1-year license revocation** OR, as an alternative, **45 days of revocation followed by 1 year of mandatory ignition interlock device (IID) participation**. A second refusal triggers **10 years revocation** OR **1 year revocation plus 2 years of IID**. A third refusal results in **lifetime revocation**. Refusal is NOT a criminal offense in West Virginia - penalties are purely administrative. West Virginia uniquely provides that **refusal of a blood test alone does NOT result in license revocation**; the officer must offer a breath or urine alternative.

## Distinctive State Features

1. **Blood Test Refusal Does NOT Revoke License**: West Virginia uniquely provides that if a driver refuses a blood test, the officer must designate either a breath or urine test instead. Refusal of a blood test alone cannot result in license revocation.

2. **IID Alternative Available**: For first and second refusals, drivers can choose an alternative penalty structure: shorter hard suspension (45 days or 1 year) followed by mandatory IID participation (1 or 2 years) instead of the full revocation period.

3. **No Criminal Penalty for Refusal**: Refusing the secondary chemical test is not a criminal offense in West Virginia. There are no jail sentences or fines specifically for refusal.

4. **Written Statement Required**: Officers must give arrested drivers a written statement advising them that refusal will result in license revocation for at least one year and up to life.

5. **Penalties Run Concurrently**: Refusal penalties run concurrently (at the same time) as any underlying DUI suspension, not consecutively (back-to-back).

6. **Lifetime Revocation for Third Refusal**: A third refusal triggers lifetime license revocation, with no IID alternative.

7. **PBT Refusal Not Penalized**: Refusal of the preliminary breath test does not carry separate administrative penalties, though it may support probable cause for arrest.

## Practical Notes

- **Administrative Only**: All refusal penalties in West Virginia are administrative. There is no criminal charge for refusal itself.
- **IID Alternative Is Optional**: Drivers must actively choose the IID alternative program. If they do not enroll, the standard revocation applies.
- **Concurrent Not Consecutive**: Refusal suspension runs at the same time as any DUI conviction suspension, not back-to-back.
- **Out-of-State Drivers**: West Virginia's implied consent law applies to all drivers on West Virginia roads.
- **Under 21 Zero Tolerance**: Drivers under 21 face penalties at .02 BAC or higher.
- **SR-22 Insurance**: Typically required for reinstatement after DUI-related suspension.
