# Marijuana Laws - West Virginia

## Verdict

West Virginia permits medical cannabis only under the West Virginia Medical Cannabis Act (SB 386, signed April 2017; dispensaries operational 2021). Registered patients may purchase up to a 30-day supply of medical cannabis, generally equivalent to up to 4 ounces of dried flower. Home cultivation is prohibited. Edibles are not permitted; approved forms include oils, tinctures, pills, liquids, dermal patches, topicals, and dry leaf for vaporization. Recreational marijuana remains illegal. HB 5259 (2026) would permit qualifying patients to grow up to 10 plants (5 mature), but remains pending.

## Named Instruments

- **W. Va. Code Section 16A-1-1 et seq.** - West Virginia Medical Cannabis Act (SB 386, 2017)
- **W. Va. Code Section 60A-4-401** - Possession penalties
- **HB 5259 (2026)** - Home cultivation for patients (pending)

## Medical Program

- **Status:** Active since 2021 (SB 386 signed 2017)
- **Enabling law:** West Virginia Medical Cannabis Act (SB 386, 2017)
- **Regulator:** West Virginia Office of Medical Cannabis (OMC)
- **Qualifying conditions:** Cancer, HIV/AIDS, ALS, Parkinson's, multiple sclerosis, spinal cord damage, epilepsy, neuropathies, Huntington's disease, Crohn's disease, PTSD, intractable seizures, sickle cell anemia, severe chronic pain, and terminal illness
- **Patient requirements:** West Virginia resident, physician certification from OMC-registered practitioner, state registry card
- **Product forms:** Oils, tinctures, pills, liquids, dermal patches, topicals, dry leaf for vaporization
- **Prohibited forms:** Edibles; smoking; home cultivation
- **Dispensaries:** State-licensed; seed-to-sale tracking
- **Caregivers:** Permitted; max 5 patients per caregiver; background check required

## Home Cultivation

- **Status:** Illegal for all purposes.
- Only licensed growers approved by the state's medical cannabis program may cultivate cannabis.
- **Pending:** HB 5259 (2026) would permit qualifying patients to grow up to 10 plants (no more than 5 mature) for personal use.

## DUI / Impairment

- West Virginia prohibits operating a motor vehicle under the influence of cannabis.
- No per se THC limit is established.
- Medical card is not a defense to DUI.

## Employment Protections

- No comprehensive statutory employment protections for medical cannabis patients.
- Employers may maintain drug-free workplace policies.
- The Medical Cannabis Act does not require employers to accommodate medical cannabis use.

## Federal Conflict

- Cannabis remains Schedule I under federal law.
- Interstate transport is prohibited.
- Federal property in West Virginia prohibits cannabis possession.
