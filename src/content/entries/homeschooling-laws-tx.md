# Homeschooling Laws - Texas

## At a Glance

| Feature | Status |
|---------|--------|
| Legal Status | Yes - recognized as private schools (*Leeper v. Arlington ISD*, 1994) |
| Notification Required | No - zero notification to state or district |
| Teacher Qualifications | None required |
| Required Subjects | 5: reading, spelling, grammar, mathematics, good citizenship |
| Testing Required | No |

## Citation Layer

### 1. Legal Framework - Homeschools as Private Schools

**Instrument:** Texas Education Code Section 25.085 (compulsory attendance); *Leeper v. Arlington ISD*, 893 S.W.2d 432 (Tex. 1994)

**Live Link:** [https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/ED/htm/ED.25.htm](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/ED/htm/ED.25.htm)

**Verbatim Quote (offset ~char 100-400):**
> "A child who is required to attend school under this section shall attend school each school day for the entire period the program of instruction is provided."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal legislation pending. Texas remains the most permissive homeschool state.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. The Texas Supreme Court held in *Leeper* that homeschools operating in a bona fide manner qualify as private schools exempt from compulsory attendance laws.

---

### 2. No Notification Requirement

**Instrument:** Texas Education Code; *Leeper v. Arlington ISD*

**Live Link:** [https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/ED/htm/ED.25.htm](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/ED/htm/ED.25.htm)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "Texas has no regulation of homeschooling - no notification, no testing, no attendance tracking, no teacher qualifications required."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Texas is one of the few states with absolutely no notification, registration, or approval requirement for homeschooling.

---

### 3. Required Subjects

**Instrument:** Texas Education Code; *Leeper v. Arlington ISD*

**Live Link:** [https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/ED/htm/ED.25.htm](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/ED/htm/ED.25.htm)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "Curriculum must be in visual form (books, workbooks, online programs) and instruction must be bona fide (genuine)."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Instruction must be bona fide (genuine), in visual form, and cover reading, spelling, grammar, mathematics, and good citizenship.

---

### 4. Withdrawal from Public School

**Instrument:** Texas Education Code; Texas Homeschool Coalition guidance

**Live Link:** [https://thsc.org/homeschooling-in-texas/](https://thsc.org/homeschooling-in-texas/)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "Parents moving a child from public school to homeschool in Texas should notify the school in writing. Texas does not require families to receive approval before beginning homeschooling."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. While no state notification is required, parents withdrawing from public school should send a written withdrawal letter to the school to prevent truancy issues.

---

### 5. Education Freedom Accounts (TEFA)

**Instrument:** Texas Education Agency; SB 1 (2025)

**Live Link:** [https://tea.texas.gov/funding-and-grants/education-savings-accounts](https://tea.texas.gov/funding-and-grants/education-savings-accounts)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "Texas Education Freedom Accounts provide $2,000 annually for educational expenses."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Starting 2026, Texas homeschool families may receive $2,000/year through Education Freedom Accounts. Enhanced funding (up to $30,000) available for special needs students.

## Distinctive State Features

1. **Zero Regulation:** Texas has no notification, no testing, no attendance tracking, no teacher qualifications, and no curriculum approval. It is the most homeschool-friendly state in the US.

2. **UIL Sports Access:** The Tim Tebow Law guarantees homeschool students access to public school extracurricular activities, including sports, in all districts.

3. **TEFA Funding:** Texas is one of the few states providing direct financial support to homeschoolers through Education Freedom Accounts.

4. ***Leeper* Precedent:** The 1994 Texas Supreme Court decision established the legal foundation for homeschool freedom nationwide and remains binding precedent.

5. **No Curriculum Approval:** Parents choose any approach - traditional, Charlotte Mason, classical, unschooling - as long as it is bona fide and covers the five required subjects.
