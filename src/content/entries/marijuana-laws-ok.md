# Marijuana Laws - Oklahoma

## Verdict

Oklahoma permits medical cannabis only. State Question 788 (June 2018) established one of the nation's least restrictive medical programs. Recreational marijuana remains illegal after voters rejected SQ 820 in March 2023. Patients may cultivate up to 6 mature plants and 6 seedlings. SB 1066 (effective January 1, 2026) requires physician registration with OMMA. Employment amendments (effective November 1, 2026) permit zero-tolerance policies for safety-sensitive positions while maintaining basic patient status protections.

## Named Instruments

- **Okla. Stat. tit. 63, Section 420 et seq.** - Oklahoma Medical Marijuana and Patient Protection Act (SQ 788, 2018)
- **SB 1066 (2025)** - Physician registration requirements (effective January 1, 2026)
- **HB 3974 (2026)** - Employment amendments (effective November 1, 2026)
- **Okla. Stat. tit. 63, Section 2-101 et seq.** - Uniform Controlled Dangerous Substances Act

## Medical Program

- **Status:** Active since August 2018
- **Enabling law:** State Question 788 (2018)
- **Regulator:** Oklahoma Medical Marijuana Authority (OMMA)
- **Qualifying conditions:** NONE SPECIFIED - physicians have broad discretion to recommend
- **Patient requirements:** Oklahoma resident (or 30-day temporary license for visitors), age 18+ (minors with caregiver), physician recommendation
- **Physician requirements:** Must register with OMMA and complete state-mandated cannabis education (SB 1066, effective January 1, 2026)
- **Home grow:** Up to 6 mature plants + 6 seedlings with patient cultivation license
- **Out-of-state:** 30-day temporary patient license available through OMMA

## Home Cultivation

- **Status:** Legal for registered patients with cultivation license
- **Limit:** 6 mature plants + 6 seedlings per patient
- **Requirements:** Plants must be on property owned by patient or with property owner's permission; not visible from public street
- **Caregivers:** May cultivate on behalf of patient

## DUI / Impairment

- Oklahoma prohibits driving under the influence of cannabis.
- No per se THC limit is established.
- Medical card is not a defense to DUI.
- Open container laws apply to cannabis in vehicles.

## Employment Protections

- **Basic protection:** Employers may not refuse to hire, discipline, discharge, or penalize solely based on patient status (Okla. Stat. tit. 63, Section 425).
- **Drug testing:** Employers may not act solely on positive marijuana test unless position is safety-sensitive (as defined by statute).
- **Safety-sensitive amendment (effective Nov 1, 2026):** Employers are required to apply zero-tolerance standards to safety-sensitive positions. Definition of "safety-sensitive" is narrowed to specific statutory categories, removing employer discretion.
- **Note:** Employers may still prohibit on-duty use and impairment.

## Federal Conflict

- Cannabis remains Schedule I under federal law.
- Interstate transport is prohibited even with medical authorization.
- Federal property in Oklahoma prohibits cannabis possession.
