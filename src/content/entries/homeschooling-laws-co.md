## Summary

Colorado homeschools operate as "nonpublic home-based educational programs" under C.R.S. 22-33-104.5. Parents must file annual written notification 14 days before beginning instruction, provide at least 172 days of instruction averaging 4 hours per day, teach 7 required subjects including the U.S. Constitution, and have students evaluated at grades 3, 5, 7, 9, and 11 via nationally normed standardized test or qualified-person evaluation. The instructor must be a parent, guardian, or adult relative. Colorado also offers a " homeschool statute" that is distinct from private school law.

## Legal Framework

### Primary Statute

**C.R.S. 22-33-104.5** - Home-based education

> "A parent who intends to establish a nonpublic home-based educational program shall provide written notification of the establishment of the program... to a school district... not less than fourteen days before beginning the program."

- **Instrument**: C.R.S. 22-33-104.5
- **Live Link**: https://law.justia.com/codes/colorado/title-22/school-districts/article-33/part-1/section-22-33-104-5/
- **Repeal Check**: Active as of 2026; no repeal legislation pending
- **Verdict**: Homeschooling is permitted with annual notification, subject, hour, and assessment requirements.

## Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| **Notification** | Annual written notice 14 days before starting |
| **Teacher Qualifications** | Must be parent, guardian, or adult relative |
| **Required Subjects** | 7 subjects (see below) |
| **Testing/Assessment** | Grades 3, 5, 7, 9, 11 (standardized test or qualified evaluation) |
| **Record-Keeping** | Attendance, test/evaluation results, immunization records |
| **Attendance** | 172 days, averaging 4 instructional hours/day |
| **Compulsory Ages** | 6-16 |

## Notification

**Annual Written Notification** must be submitted to the local school district at least **14 days** before beginning the home-based educational program each school year. The notice must include:

- Child's name, age, and place of residence
- Number of hours of attendance
- Names of persons providing instruction
- Address of the location where instruction will take place

If starting mid-year, submit 14 days before beginning. If continuing from the previous year, submit before the new school year begins.

## Required Subjects (7)

Under C.R.S. 22-33-104.5(3)(d), the program must include, but not be limited to:

1. Reading
2. Writing
3. Speaking (communication skills)
4. Mathematics
5. History
6. Civics
7. Literature
8. Science
9. Regular courses of instruction in the Constitution of the United States

## Assessment & Testing

Students must be evaluated at grades **3, 5, 7, 9, and 11**. Parents must choose one of:

1. **Nationally normed standardized achievement test** - administered by a qualified person (licensed teacher, licensed psychologist, or person with a graduate degree in education)
2. **Qualified person evaluation** - a written evaluation by a Colorado-licensed teacher, licensed psychologist, or person with a graduate degree in education

Test/evaluation results must be submitted to the school district that received the written notice of intent. The parent is responsible for the cost of testing.

## Record-Keeping

Parents must maintain and store:
- Attendance data
- Test and evaluation results
- Immunization records

Records must be provided to the school district upon reasonable request.

## Special Provisions

- **State Assessment Option**: Parents may request that a homeschooled child take a state assessment; results are sent to parents (C.R.S. 22-7-409(III)(1.3)(b))
- **Diplomas**: No state-accredited diploma; parents may issue a diploma. Students may take the GED.
- **Sports/Activities**: No statutory right to public school extracurricular participation
- **Funding**: No state-level financial programs for homeschoolers

## Distinctive Features

- Instructor must be parent, guardian, or adult relative (cannot hire an unrelated tutor as primary instructor)
- 172-day, 4-hour daily average requirement
- Testing at 5 specific grade levels (3, 5, 7, 9, 11)
- U.S. Constitution instruction required
- Test results submitted to district (not just kept at home)
- "Nonpublic home-based educational program" is a distinct statutory category
