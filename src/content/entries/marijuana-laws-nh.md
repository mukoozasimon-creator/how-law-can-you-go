# Marijuana Laws - New Hampshire

## At a Glance

| Feature | Status |
|---------|--------|
| Recreational | No - fully illegal (decriminalized <=3/4 oz since 2017) |
| Medical | Yes (HB 573, 2013; operational since 2016) |
| Home Grow | No - prohibited for both patients and caregivers (only New England state with this prohibition) |
| Age | 21+ for decriminalized possession; 18+ for medical (minors with two provider certifications, one pediatrician) |
| Possession Limit (Rec) | <=3/4 oz: civil violation ($100); >3/4 oz: criminal misdemeanor |
| Possession Limit (Med) | 2 oz usable cannabis at one time; 2 oz per 10-day rolling period |
| Tax | N/A (no legal market) |
| Distinctive Feature | Only New England state prohibiting home cultivation for medical patients; out-of-state patient recognition (can purchase at NH dispensaries); governor vetoed home grow bills multiple times |

## Citation Layer

### 1. Medical Marijuana Program

**Instrument:** New Hampshire Revised Statutes Annotated Title X, Chapter 126-X (Therapeutic Use of Cannabis / HB 573)

**Live Link:** [https://www.gencourt.state.nh.us/rsa/html/x/126-x/126-x-mrg.htm](https://www.gencourt.state.nh.us/rsa/html/x/126-x/126-x-mrg.htm)

**Verbatim Quote (offset ~char 200-500):**
> "A qualifying patient shall not be subject to arrest by state or local law enforcement, prosecution or penalty under state or municipal law, or denied any right or privilege for the therapeutic use of cannabis in accordance with this chapter, if the qualifying patient possesses an amount of cannabis that does not exceed... Two ounces of usable cannabis."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending. The program has been expanded multiple times, most recently in 2024 to add qualifying conditions.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Registered patients may possess up to 2 oz of usable cannabis.

---

### 2. Home Cultivation Prohibition

**Instrument:** RSA Chapter 126-X (no home cultivation provision); multiple vetoed bills (HB 1231, 2024; HB 53, 2025)

**Live Link:** [https://www.citizenscount.org/bills/hb-53-2025](https://www.citizenscount.org/bills/hb-53-2025)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "Allows qualifying medical marijuana patients and caregivers to grow marijuana at home... This bill did not become law. Status: Tabled in the Senate."

**Repeal Check:** Home cultivation remains prohibited. The House has passed home grow bills multiple times, but the Senate has tabled or rejected them. Governor Sununu vetoed a 2019 home grow bill.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE PROHIBITION. New Hampshire is the only New England state that bars medical patients from cultivating cannabis at home.

---

### 3. Out-of-State Patient Recognition

**Instrument:** RSA 126-X:2, V (as amended by HB 1278, effective Oct. 1, 2024)

**Live Link:** [https://www.dhhs.nh.gov/programs-services/population-health/therapeutic-cannabis](https://www.dhhs.nh.gov/programs-services/population-health/therapeutic-cannabis)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "Effective October 1, 2024, HB 1278 removes restrictions on visiting out-of-state patients who access a NH-licensed Alternative Treatment Center more than 3 times per year. Visitors from out of state and from Canada... may now purchase therapeutic cannabis from a NH ATC at the same frequency as NH qualifying patients."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Out-of-state medical cannabis cardholders may purchase at New Hampshire Alternative Treatment Centers without the prior 3-visit annual limit.

---

### 4. Decriminalization

**Instrument:** New Hampshire Revised Statutes Annotated; HB 640 (2017)

**Live Link:** [https://www.gencourt.state.nh.us/rsa/html/nhtoc.htm](https://www.gencourt.state.nh.us/rsa/html/nhtoc.htm)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "Possession of up to three-quarters of an ounce of marijuana is a civil violation punishable by a fine of up to $100 for a first or second offense, and up to $300 for a third offense within three years."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Possession of <=3/4 oz is a civil violation, not a criminal offense.

### 5. Employment and Discrimination Protections

**Instrument:** RSA 126-X:2

**Live Link:** [https://www.gencourt.state.nh.us/rsa/html/x/126-x/126-x-mrg.htm](https://www.gencourt.state.nh.us/rsa/html/x/126-x/126-x-mrg.htm)

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Medical patients receive limited anti-discrimination protections, including in child custody and organ transplant eligibility.

## Distinctive State Features

1. **Only New England State Without Home Grow:** New Hampshire is the sole New England state that prohibits medical patients from cultivating cannabis at home.

2. **Out-of-State Purchase Rights:** New Hampshire allows out-of-state medical cardholders to purchase at its dispensaries, a relatively rare provision.

3. **Governor Veto Pattern:** Multiple home cultivation bills have passed the House and/or Senate only to be vetoed or tabled, reflecting executive resistance to expansion.

4. **Two-Ounce, Ten-Day Purchase Cap:** Medical patients may purchase up to 2 oz in any 10-day rolling period.

5. **Any Debilitating Condition:** As of 2024, providers may certify patients for "any debilitating or terminal medical condition or symptom" in their clinical opinion.
