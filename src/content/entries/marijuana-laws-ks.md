# Marijuana Laws - Kansas

## Verdict

Marijuana is illegal for recreational and medical purposes in Kansas. The state lists cannabis as a Schedule I controlled substance under K.S.A. 65-4105. The only exception is a 2019 law permitting patients with debilitating medical conditions to use CBD treatment preparations containing no more than 5% THC, with a physician's written recommendation serving as an affirmative defense to prosecution. First-time possession of less than 1 ounce is decriminalized to a civil infraction ($300 fine, no jail). Kansas does not allow voter-initiated ballot measures, so any reform must pass through the legislature. HB 2678 (medical) and HB 2679 (adult-use) were introduced in February 2026 but remain in committee.

## Possession Limits

| Product Type | Legal Status | Limit |
|--------------|-------------|-------|
| Marijuana (any amount) | Illegal | 0 |
| CBD treatment preparation | Affirmative defense only | No more than 5% THC; physician recommendation required |
| Hemp-derived CBD (0.3% THC) | Legal under federal farm bill | No limit |

## Penalty Tiers

| Offense | Classification | Penalty |
|---------|---------------|---------|
| First offense, <1 oz | Civil infraction (decriminalized) | Up to $300 fine; no jail |
| Subsequent, <1 oz | Class B misdemeanor | Up to 6 months jail; up to $1,000 fine |
| 1 oz - 450g | Class A misdemeanor | Up to 1 year jail; up to $2,500 fine |
| >450g | Felony | 10 months - 7 years prison; up to $100,000 fine |
| Distribution | Felony | Enhanced penalties based on amount |

## Medical Program

- **Status:** No medical marijuana program exists.
- **CBD affirmative defense:** Patients with debilitating conditions may possess CBD treatment preparations (no more than 5% THC) with a physician's written recommendation. This serves as an affirmative defense to prosecution, not a blanket legalization.
- **No dispensary system:** Patients must obtain products through out-of-state sources or other means (federal legal risk applies).
- **Pending legislation:** HB 2678 (medical) and HB 2679 (adult-use) introduced February 4, 2026, both stalled in House Federal and State Affairs Committee.

## Home Cultivation

- **Status:** Illegal for all purposes.
- Cultivation of any cannabis plant is treated as manufacturing and carries felony penalties.

## DUI / Impairment

- Kansas prohibits operating a motor vehicle under the influence of cannabis.
- No per se THC limit is established.
- DUI penalties mirror alcohol DUI under K.S.A. 8-1567.

## Employment Protections

- No employment protections for cannabis use exist.
- Employers may drug test and terminate for any cannabis use.
- CBD affirmative defense does not create employment protections.

## Federal Conflict

- Kansas law aligns with federal Schedule I classification.
- No state-federal conflict exists because state law does not authorize medical or recreational marijuana beyond the narrow CBD affirmative defense.
