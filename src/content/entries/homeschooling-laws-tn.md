## Summary

Tennessee offers three educational options for families wishing to educate at home: (1) Independent Home School under T.C.A. § 49-6-3050, (2) Church-Related School (CRS) umbrella under T.C.A. § 49-50-801 (Category IV non-public school), and (3) accredited online school (Category III). Approximately 95% of Tennessee homeschoolers choose the CRS umbrella path to avoid state testing and reduce district interaction. The Independent path requires annual notification, attendance tracking, and standardized testing in grades 5, 7, and 9.

## Legal Framework

### Primary Statute - Independent Home School

**T.C.A. § 49-6-3050** - Home schools - Conduct and attendance

> "A home school is a school conducted or directed by a parent or parents or a legal guardian or guardians for their own children."

- **Instrument**: T.C.A. § 49-6-3050
- **Live Link**: https://publications.tnsosfiles.com/acts/2025/pub/pc0912.pdf (Public Chapter 912, 2025 amendments)
- **Repeal Check**: Active as of 2026; amended by Public Chapter 912 (2025)
- **Verdict**: Independent homeschooling is permitted with annual notification, attendance requirements, and testing in specified grades.

### Church-Related School Statute

**T.C.A. § 49-50-801** - Category IV non-public schools (church-related schools)

> Defines church-related schools and permits them to offer umbrella programs for homeschool families.

- **Instrument**: T.C.A. § 49-50-801
- **Live Link**: https://www.tn.gov/education/families/non-public-schools.html
- **Repeal Check**: Active as of 2026
- **Verdict**: CRS umbrella schools handle administrative compliance, freeing families from most state reporting requirements.

## Requirements - Independent Home School Path

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| **Notification** | Annual Notice of Intent to local director of schools |
| **Teacher Qualifications** | High school diploma or GED |
| **Required Subjects** | None mandated |
| **Testing** | Standardized test in grades 5, 7, and 9 |
| **Record-Keeping** | Attendance records submitted annually |
| **Attendance** | 4 hours/day for 180 days |
| **Compulsory Ages** | 6-17 |

## Notification

**Annual Notice of Intent** must be filed with the local director of schools. For first-time homeschoolers, registration may occur during the school year. Continuing families must submit a new intent before the next school year begins.

The NOI must include (for reporting purposes only):
- Names, number, ages, and grade levels of children
- Location of the school
- Proposed curriculum
- Proposed hours of instruction
- Qualifications of the parent-teacher (high school diploma or GED)

## Required Subjects

Tennessee does **not** mandate specific subjects for homeschoolers. Complete curriculum freedom is granted under both the Independent and CRS paths.

## Assessment & Testing

**Independent Path**: Under Public Chapter 912, students in grades 5, 7, and 9 must take either:
- A parent-selected nationally normed standardized test in English/language arts and math, OR
- The same state board-approved standardized assessment required of public school students

**CRS Path**: Testing requirements are set by the individual church-related school. Most do not require state TCAP testing.

**Online School Path**: Testing per the accredited school's policies.

## Record-Keeping

**Independent Path**: Parents must:
- Maintain attendance records and submit them to the Director of Schools at the end of each school year
- Submit proof of vaccination and receipt of any required health services/examinations

**CRS Path**: Record-keeping requirements are set by the umbrella school.

## Special Provisions

- **CRS Umbrella**: Over 500 church-related schools offer umbrella programs statewide. The CRS handles registration and reporting; families do not file a state NOI
- **High School Diploma**: Parent-issued diplomas are recognized for Independent homeschool graduates
- **Dual Enrollment Grant**: Up to 5 free community college courses for high schoolers
- **HOPE Scholarship**: Available for college-bound homeschool graduates
- **IEA Program**: Individualized Education Account for students with qualifying disabilities (autism, hearing impairment, intellectual disability, etc.)
- **ESA Programs**: Tennessee's ESA Pilot Program and Education Freedom Scholarship are **not** available to homeschoolers - only for private school enrollment
- **Sports/Activities**: No statutory right to public school extracurricular participation

## Distinctive Features

- **Three-path system** with dramatically different compliance burdens
- ~95% of homeschoolers use CRS umbrella schools to avoid state testing
- 4-hour daily instruction requirement (specific hourly minimum is unusual)
- Testing only in grades 5, 7, and 9 (not every year)
- Parent must have high school diploma or GED for Independent path
- ESA funding explicitly excludes homeschoolers
