# Marijuana Laws - Hawaii

## Verdict

Hawaii permits medical cannabis only under HRS 329 (the Uniform Controlled Substances Act), enacted in 2000. Registered patients may possess up to 4 ounces of usable marijuana and cultivate up to 7 plants (3 mature, 4 immature) at their primary residence. Primary caregivers were prohibited from cultivating after January 1, 2025, except for parents or legal guardians of minor qualifying patients. Recreational marijuana remains illegal. Multiple adult-use bills (SB 3275, HB 1624, HB 1246) failed to advance in the 2026 legislative session. All cultivation must be indoors in an enclosed, locked facility with security measures.

## Named Instruments

- **HRS Chapter 329** - Uniform Controlled Substances Act (medical cannabis, 2000)
- **HRS Section 329-121** - Definitions (medical use, qualifying patient, primary caregiver)
- **HRS Section 329-122** - Medical use permitted; possession and cultivation limits
- **HRS Section 329-130** - Primary caregiver cultivation prohibition (effective 1/1/2025, except parent/guardian of minor)
- **HRS Section 329-125** - Registration requirements
- **SB 3275 (2026)** - Low-dose adult-use legalization (failed to advance)
- **HB 1624 (2026)** - Adult-use constitutional amendment (failed)

## Medical Program

- **Status:** Active since 2000
- **Enabling law:** HRS Chapter 329 (Hawaii's Uniform Controlled Substances Act)
- **Regulator:** Hawaii Department of Health - Office of Medical Cannabis Control and Regulation
- **Qualifying conditions:** Cancer, glaucoma, HIV/AIDS, cachexia, severe pain, severe nausea, seizures, epilepsy, multiple sclerosis, Crohn's disease, PTSD, lupus, rheumatoid arthritis, ALS, and others
- **Patient requirements:** Hawaii resident, physician certification, state 329 registration card
- **Dispensaries:** State-licensed medical cannabis dispensaries operational
- **Home grow:** Patients may cultivate up to 7 plants (3 mature, 4 immature) at primary residence
- **Caregiver cultivation:** Prohibited after January 1, 2025, except for parents/legal guardians of minor patients
- **Cultivation requirements:** Indoor only; enclosed, locked facility; alarm systems; video monitoring; locks; exterior lighting; plant ID tags with registration number and expiration date

## Home Cultivation

- **Status:** Legal for registered patients only
- **Patient limit:** Up to 7 plants (3 mature, 4 immature) at primary residence
- **Caregiver limit:** Prohibited after 1/1/2025 (except parent/guardian of minor patient)
- **Requirements:** Indoor cultivation only; enclosed and locked; security and surveillance measures required; plant ID tags required

## DUI / Impairment

- Hawaii prohibits operating a motor vehicle under the influence of cannabis.
- No per se THC limit is established.
- Medical card is not a defense to DUI.

## Employment Protections

- No comprehensive statutory employment protections for medical cannabis patients.
- Employers may maintain drug-free workplace policies.
- The medical program does not create anti-discrimination rights in employment.

## Federal Conflict

- Cannabis remains Schedule I under federal law.
- Interstate transport is prohibited.
- Federal property in Hawaii (national parks, military bases) prohibits cannabis possession.
