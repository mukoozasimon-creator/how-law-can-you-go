## Summary

Louisiana offers two legal pathways for home education: (1) a BESE-approved Home Study Program under R.S. 17:236.1, and (2) registration as a Nonpublic School Not Seeking State Approval under R.S. 17:232. Most families choose the Home Study path, which requires an initial application within 15 days of beginning and annual renewal by October 1. Louisiana has no parent qualification requirements, no mandated subjects, no standardized testing, and no curriculum approval. Homeschoolers also have full statutory access to public school sports under Louisiana law.

## Legal Framework

### Primary Statute - Home Study

**La. R.S. § 17:236.1** - Home study programs

> "A home study plan is a program in which an approved curriculum can be implemented under the direction and control of a parent or a tutor. Any child eligible to attend an elementary or secondary school in Louisiana is eligible to participate in a home study plan."

- **Instrument**: La. R.S. § 17:236.1
- **Live Link**: https://www.legis.la.gov/legis/law.aspx?d=110147
- **Repeal Check**: Active as of 2026; no repeal legislation pending
- **Verdict**: Home study programs are approved by BESE with initial and renewal applications.

### Nonpublic School Statute

**La. R.S. § 17:232** - Nonpublic schools

> Establishes the framework for nonpublic schools not seeking state approval, including registration requirements.

- **Instrument**: La. R.S. § 17:232
- **Live Link**: https://www.legis.la.gov/legis/law.aspx?d=110143
- **Repeal Check**: Active as of 2026
- **Verdict**: Nonpublic schools not seeking approval may register annually with the state.

### Compulsory Attendance

**La. R.S. § 17:221** - School attendance

> "Nothing in this Section shall be construed to prohibit a child from enrolling in an approved home study program or a nonpublic school not seeking state approval."

- **Instrument**: La. R.S. § 17:221
- **Live Link**: https://codes.findlaw.com/la/revised-statutes/la-rev-stat-tit-17-sect-221/
- **Repeal Check**: Active as of 2026
- **Verdict**: Home study and nonpublic school enrollment satisfy compulsory attendance requirements.

## Notification

### Home Study Path

**Initial Application**: Submit to the Louisiana Board of Elementary and Secondary Education (BESE) within **15 days** of beginning the home study program. Must include:
- Completed application form
- Certified copy of the child's birth certificate
- Assurance that the program will offer a sustained curriculum of quality at least equal to public schools

**Renewal Application**: Due annually by **October 1** (or within 12 months of initial approval, whichever is later). Must present satisfactory evidence that the program continues to meet requirements, which may include:
- Test results showing the student scored at or above grade level, OR
- Description and substantiating documents detailing the instructional program

### Nonpublic School Path

Submit an annual registration form to the Louisiana Department of Education by the **30th day after the school session begins**. The form must include:
- School year
- Name of school
- Contact information
- Total number of students enrolled

## Required Subjects

No subjects are mandated by Louisiana state law for either homeschool pathway. The "sustained curriculum of a quality at least equal to that offered by public schools" standard is intentionally broad.

## Assessment & Testing

No standardized testing is required for either homeschool pathway. However, test results may be used as evidence of satisfactory progress for Home Study renewal applications.

## Record-Keeping

- **Home Study**: Maintain attendance records and documentation of educational progress
- **Nonpublic**: Maintain basic enrollment and attendance records

## Special Provisions

- **Sports Access**: Louisiana homeschoolers have **full public school sports access** under state law, including LHSAA athletics
- **TOPS Scholarship**: Students who complete a BESE-approved Home Study program may be eligible for the Taylor Opportunity Program for Students (TOPS) if they meet ACT/SAT score requirements and are certified by a parent or court-ordered custodian
- **Special Education**: Homeschooled students with disabilities may be eligible for Louisiana's ESA program for therapies and specialized services. Local districts must evaluate suspected disabilities under Child Find.
- **Returning to Public School**: Students may face placement assessment based on demonstrated proficiency

## Distinctive Features

- **Two-path system**: BESE Home Study vs. Nonpublic School registration
- No parent qualifications, no testing, no curriculum approval
- **Full public school sports access** for homeschoolers
- TOPS scholarship eligibility for Home Study graduates
- 15-day initial application window
- "Sustained curriculum of quality at least equal to public schools" is a flexible standard
- Nonpublic path is simpler but excludes TOPS eligibility
