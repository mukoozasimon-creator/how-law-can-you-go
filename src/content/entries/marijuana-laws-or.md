# Marijuana Laws - Oregon

## Verdict

Oregon permits adult-use (recreational) cannabis for adults 21+ under Measure 91 (2014), with retail sales operational since October 2015. Adults may possess up to 2 oz of flower in public and 8 oz at home; 1 oz of concentrate; 16 oz of solid edibles; 72 oz of liquid products; and 10 seeds. Home cultivation of 4 plants per household is permitted. Oregon provides meaningful off-duty employment protections for cannabis consumers under ORS 659A.315. DUI is impairment-based with no per se THC limit.

## Possession Limits

| Product Type | Public Limit | Private/Home Limit |
|--------------|-------------|-------------------|
| Flower | 2 oz | 8 oz |
| Concentrates/extracts | 1 oz (licensed retailer only) | 1 oz |
| Solid edibles | 16 oz | 16 oz |
| Liquid products | 72 oz | 72 oz |
| Seeds | 10 | 10 |
| Plants | N/A | 4 per household |

*Medical OMMP patients: up to 24 oz usable marijuana; up to 6 mature plants with designated grow site registration.*

## Penalty Tiers (Over-Limit Possession)

| Amount | Classification | Penalty |
|--------|---------------|---------|
| >2-4 oz in public OR >8 oz-1 lb private | Class B violation | Up to $1,000 fine |
| >4-8 oz in public OR >1-2 lbs private | Class B misdemeanor | Up to 6 months jail; up to $2,500 fine |
| >8 oz-8 lbs in public OR >2-8 lbs private | Class A misdemeanor | Up to 364 days jail; up to $6,250 fine |
| >8 lbs | Class C felony | Up to 5 years prison; up to $125,000 fine |

## Medical Program

- **Status:** Active since 1998 (OMMA); overseen by Oregon Health Authority
- **Qualifying conditions:** Cancer, glaucoma, HIV/AIDS, Alzheimer's, PTSD, chronic pain, nausea, seizures, persistent muscle spasms, and others
- **Patient registry:** Required (OMMP card)
- **Home grow:** Up to 6 mature plants per patient with designated grow site registration
- **Possession:** Up to 24 oz usable marijuana
- **Tax:** Medical patients exempt from 17% recreational cannabis tax

## Home Cultivation

- **Recreational:** Up to 4 plants per household (regardless of number of residents)
- **Medical:** Up to 6 mature plants per patient with grow site registration
- **Requirements:** Must not be visible from public places; must be >1,000 feet from schools
- **Important:** DIY extraction/concentrate manufacturing at home is a felony without a license.

## DUI / Impairment

- **Standard:** Impairment-based; no per se nanogram THC blood limit
- **Testing:** Field sobriety testing and blood draws
- **Penalty:** First offense - minimum $1,000 fine, 1-year license suspension, up to 1 year jail
- **Open container:** Must be stored in sealed container in trunk or area inaccessible to driver/passengers

## Employment Protections

- **ORS 659A.315:** Employers may not refuse to hire or terminate an employee solely for off-duty cannabis use.
- **Exceptions:** Safety-sensitive positions, federally regulated employers, on-duty impairment
- **Note:** Oregon is one of a small number of states with off-duty use protections that apply to adult-use consumers, not just medical patients.

## Federal Conflict

- Cannabis remains Schedule I under federal law.
- Interstate transport is prohibited even to neighboring legal states (WA, CA).
- Federal property (national parks, federal facilities) prohibits cannabis.
