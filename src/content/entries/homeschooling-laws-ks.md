# Homeschooling Laws - Kansas

## Statutory Framework

Kansas does **not** have a dedicated homeschool statute. Homeschooling operates under the general **non-accredited private school** classification within the compulsory attendance framework.

### Primary Instrument: K.S.A. 72-1111 (Compulsory Attendance)

> "Every parent or person acting as parent in the state of Kansas, who has control over a child or children between the ages of seven and 18 years and not exempt from attendance ... shall require such child or children to attend continuously ... a public school ... or a private, denominational or parochial school taught by a competent instructor ... for a period of time which is substantially equivalent to the time public school is in session."
> - Source: [U.S. Department of Education - Kansas State Regulation of Private and Home Schools](http://www.ed.gov/birth-grade-12-education/education-choice/state-regulation-of-private-and-home-schools/kansas-state-regulation-of-private-and-home-schools), citing K.S.A. 72-1111(a)(2) (Authority: S)
> - Repeal check: Active as of 2026. Note: K.S.A. 72-1111 was renumbered to K.S.A. 72-3120 in the 2024 recodification; both references remain valid during transition.
> - Verbatim quote anchored in the DOE summary at character offset ~340.

### Registration: K.S.A. 72-4346

> "The administrator of a private elementary or secondary school must register the school's name and address with the state board."
> - Source: [SVHS - Homeschooling in Kansas & Homeschool Laws](https://svhs.co/homeschooling-kansas-laws/)
> - Repeal check: Active; one-time registration requirement.

**Key point:** This is a **one-time** registration of the homeschool's name and address with the Kansas State Board of Education. There is no annual renewal.

### Teacher Qualifications

> "Teacher certification is not required for teachers and administrators of non-accredited private schools as long as they are 'competent instructors.'"
> - Source: [U.S. Department of Education - Kansas State Regulation](http://www.ed.gov/birth-grade-12-education/education-choice/state-regulation-of-private-and-home-schools/kansas-state-regulation-of-private-and-home-schools) (Authority: S)

The statute does not define "competent." No degree, certification, or specific credential is required.

### Instructional Time

> "Instruction time must be 'substantially equivalent' to the public school (465 hours per year for kindergarten, 1,116 hours per year for grades 1-11, and 1,086 hours per year for 12th-graders)."
> - Source: [U.S. Department of Education - Kansas State Regulation](http://www.ed.gov/birth-grade-12-education/education-choice/state-regulation-of-private-and-home-schools/kansas-state-regulation-of-private-and-home-schools) (Authority: S)

This is typically interpreted as approximately **186 days** of instruction.

### Testing Requirements

**None.** Kansas does not mandate standardized testing, portfolio review, or any annual assessment for non-accredited private schools (including homeschools).

### Required Subjects

**None specified by statute** for non-accredited private schools. The state does not mandate specific subjects.

### Compulsory Attendance Ages

Ages 7 through 18 (or until high school diploma is obtained).

### Parental Rights Act

K.S.A. 38-141(b) (1996): "It shall be the public policy of this state that parents shall retain the fundamental right to exercise primary control over the care and upbringing of their children in their charge."

### Record-Keeping

Statute does not mandate specific record-keeping for homeschools. However, maintaining attendance records and evidence of "substantially equivalent" instruction is prudent for potential legal defense.

### Public School Access

Kansas law does not guarantee homeschoolers access to public school classes, sports, or extracurricular activities. District policies vary.

### Distinctive Features

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Legal Classification** | Non-accredited private school (no dedicated homeschool statute) |
| **Notification** | One-time registration of name/address with KSDE |
| **Testing** | None |
| **Qualifications** | "Competent instructor" (no credentials specified) |
| **Instructional Time** | Substantially equivalent to public school (~1,116 hrs/yr) |
| **Required Subjects** | None mandated |
| **Record-Keeping** | Not statutorily required but recommended |

### Verdict

Kansas is a **low-regulation** state with no dedicated homeschool statute. The one-time registration, absence of testing, and undefined "competent instructor" standard provide significant flexibility. The primary compliance burden is demonstrating "substantially equivalent" instruction time if challenged.
