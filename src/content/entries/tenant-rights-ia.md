## The Short Answer

In Iowa, your landlord must give you a **3-day written notice** to pay rent or vacate for nonpayment (Iowa Code 562A.27(2)). For material lease violations, a **7-day notice** to remedy is required, followed by an additional **30-day notice** of termination if uncured (562A.27(1)). For clear and present danger or drug activity, a **3-day unconditional notice** applies (562A.27A). To end a month-to-month tenancy, the landlord must give **30 days' written notice** (562A.34). Iowa has no just-cause requirement and no rent control. Self-help eviction is illegal.

## Key Statutes

### Iowa Code 562A.27(2) - 3-Day Notice for Nonpayment

> "If rent is unpaid when due, the landlord may deliver a written notice to the tenant specifying the amount of rent due and that the rental agreement will terminate if the rent is not paid within three days."

**Live link:** https://www.legis.iowa.gov/law/statutoryCode/sections?code=562A&section=27
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 3-day notice for nonpayment. Tenant can cure by paying within 3 days. Demand must be for past-due rent only; including late fees or other charges is a defect.

### Iowa Code 562A.27(1) - 7/30-Day Notice for Lease Violations

> "If the tenant materially violates the rental agreement, the landlord may deliver a written notice specifying the acts and omissions constituting the breach and that the rental agreement will terminate in 30 days if the breach is not remedied within 7 days."

**Live link:** https://www.legis.iowa.gov/law/statutoryCode/sections?code=562A&section=27
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 7 days to cure, 30 days to quit. Second same violation within 6 months = 7-day unconditional notice.

### Iowa Code 562A.27A - 3-Day Notice for Clear and Present Danger

> "If the tenant or any person on the premises with the tenant's consent creates a clear and present danger to the health or safety of other tenants, the landlord may deliver a written notice that the rental agreement will terminate in three days."

**Live link:** https://www.legis.iowa.gov/law/statutoryCode/sections?code=562A&section=27A
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 3-day unconditional notice for clear and present danger or drug-related activity. No cure period.

### Iowa Code 562A.34 - 30-Day Month-to-Month Termination

> "The landlord or the tenant may terminate a month-to-month tenancy by a written notice given to the other at least 30 days before the periodic rental date specified in the notice."

**Live link:** https://www.legis.iowa.gov/law/statutoryCode/sections?code=562A&section=34
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 30 days written notice for month-to-month termination by either party.

### Iowa Code 562A.12 - Security Deposits

**Key rules:**
- Maximum: **2 months' rent** (counting all refundable deposits combined).
- Return deadline: **30 days** after termination and receipt of forwarding address.
- Must provide itemized statement if deductions made.
- Wrongful withholding: Tenant can recover actual damages.

**Live link:** https://www.legis.iowa.gov/law/statutoryCode/sections?code=562A&section=12
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 30-day return. 2-month cap counting all deposits.

## What This Means for You

| Situation | Notice Required | Just Cause Required? |
|-----------|----------------|----------------------|
| Month-to-month termination | 30 days written | No |
| Nonpayment of rent | 3 days (pay or vacate) | N/A |
| Lease violation | 7 days cure / 30 days quit | N/A |
| Clear and present danger | 3 days (no cure) | N/A |

## Important Notes

- **No just-cause requirement:** Iowa landlords can terminate month-to-month tenancies for any reason with proper notice.
- **No rent control:** Iowa does not have statewide rent control.
- **Strict notice construction:** Iowa courts strictly construe pay-or-quit notices. Defects in amount, dates, or service void the notice.
- **Rent-only demand:** The 3-day notice must demand only past-due rent. Including late fees, utilities, or repair charges is a defect that can void the notice.
- **Partial payment risk:** Accepting partial rent after serving a 3-day notice may waive the notice and require a fresh notice.
- **Recording:** Iowa is a one-party consent state for recordings (Iowa Code 808B.2). You may record conversations you are a party to without the other person's consent.

## Cross-State Notes

- **vs. Nebraska:** NE requires 7 days for nonpayment vs. IA's 3 days. Both have 14/30-day or 7/30-day violation structures.
- **vs. Missouri:** MO has a 10-day notice for nonpayment vs. IA's 3 days.
- **vs. Minnesota:** MN requires 14 days for nonpayment vs. IA's 3 days.
