## The Short Answer

In New Mexico, your landlord must give you a **3-day written notice** to pay rent or the rental agreement terminates (NMSA 47-8-33(D)). For initial material lease violations, a **7-day notice** to cure or quit applies (47-8-33(A)). For substantial violations (criminal activity, violence), a **3-day unconditional notice** applies (47-8-33(I)). To end a month-to-month tenancy, the landlord must give **30 days' written notice** (47-8-37). New Mexico has no just-cause requirement and no rent control. Self-help eviction is illegal.

## Key Statutes

### NMSA 47-8-33(D) - 3-Day Notice for Nonpayment

> "If rent is unpaid when due and the resident fails to pay within three days after written notice of nonpayment and the owner's intention to terminate, the owner may terminate the rental agreement."

**Live link:** https://law.justia.com/codes/new-mexico/chapter-47/article-8/section-47-8-33/
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 3-day notice for nonpayment. Tenant can cure by paying within 3 days. Tender of full amount before expiration bars eviction action.

### NMSA 47-8-33(A) - 7-Day Notice for Lease Violations

> "For an initial material noncompliance with the rental agreement or noncompliance materially affecting health and safety, the owner must deliver written notice specifying the acts and omissions and stating that the rental agreement will terminate on a date at least seven days after receipt if the breach is not remedied."

**Live link:** https://law.justia.com/codes/new-mexico/chapter-47/article-8/section-47-8-33/
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 7-day cure-or-quit for initial violations. Second breach within 6 months = 7-day unconditional notice (47-8-33(B)).

### NMSA 47-8-33(I) - 3-Day Notice for Substantial Violations

> "If the resident knowingly commits or consents to another person committing a substantial violation, the owner shall deliver a written notice specifying the time, place, and nature of the act and that the rental agreement will terminate upon a date not less than three days after receipt."

**Live link:** https://law.justia.com/codes/new-mexico/chapter-47/article-8/section-47-8-33/
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 3-day no-cure notice for substantial violations. Domestic violence victim defense available.

### NMSA 47-8-37 - 30-Day Month-to-Month Termination

> "Either party may terminate a month-to-month residency with at least 30 days' written notice before the periodic rental date specified in the notice."

**Live link:** https://law.justia.com/codes/new-mexico/chapter-47/article-8/section-47-8-37/
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 30 days written notice for month-to-month termination. Week-to-week requires 7 days.

### NMSA 47-8-36 - Self-Help Eviction Prohibited

> "An owner may not knowingly exclude, remove, threaten to remove, or dispossess a resident without a court order by changing locks, blocking access, interfering with utilities, removing belongings, or taking other willful acts that make the unit inaccessible or uninhabitable."

**Live link:** https://law.justia.com/codes/new-mexico/chapter-47/article-8/section-47-8-36/
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** Self-help eviction is illegal. Resident may abate 100% of rent for affected days, seek statutory civil penalties, and recover damages.

## What This Means for You

| Situation | Notice Required | Just Cause Required? |
|-----------|----------------|----------------------|
| Month-to-month termination | 30 days written | No |
| Nonpayment of rent | 3 days (pay or quit) | N/A |
| Lease violation | 7 days (cure or quit) | N/A |
| Substantial violation | 3 days (no cure) | N/A |
| Week-to-week termination | 7 days written | No |

## Important Notes

- **No just-cause requirement:** New Mexico landlords can terminate month-to-month tenancies for any reason with proper notice.
- **No rent control:** New Mexico does not have statewide rent control.
- **Domestic violence defense:** In substantial violation cases, it is a defense that the resident is a victim of domestic violence and has filed for or secured a protective order.
- **Retaliation protection:** NMSA 47-8-39 prohibits retaliation within 90 days of protected tenant activity.
- **Recording:** New Mexico is a one-party consent state for recordings (NMSA 30-12-1). You may record conversations you are a party to without the other person's consent.

## Cross-State Notes

- **vs. Arizona:** Both have similar 3-day/7-day frameworks and no just-cause requirements.
- **vs. Colorado:** CO has a 10-day notice for nonpayment vs. NM's 3 days. CO has just-cause requirements in some cities.
- **vs. Utah:** UT has a 3-day notice for nonpayment similar to NM, but UT has no cure period for lease violations (15-day notice vs. NM's 7-day cure).
