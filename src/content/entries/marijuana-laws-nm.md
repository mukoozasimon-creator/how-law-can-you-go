# Marijuana Laws - New Mexico

## Verdict

New Mexico legalized adult-use cannabis under HB 2 (the Cannabis Regulation Act), signed April 12, 2021. Adults 21+ may possess up to 2 oz of flower, 16 g of concentrate, and 800 mg of edible THC. Home cultivation of up to 6 mature and 6 immature plants per person is permitted, with a maximum of 12 mature plants per household. The state Cannabis Control Division licenses 10 types of cannabis businesses including consumption areas. Medical patients may purchase up to 15 oz per 90-day period (or 230 units / 8 oz). A 12% excise tax applies to recreational sales.

## Named Instruments

- **NMSA 1978, Section 26-2C-1 et seq.** - Cannabis Regulation Act (HB 2, 2021)
- **NMSA 1978, Section 26-2C-5** - Personal use and possession limits
- **NMSA 1978, Section 26-2C-7** - Home cultivation
- **NMSA 1978, Section 7-1-6.18** - Cannabis excise tax (12%)

## Possession Limits

| Product Type | Recreational Limit | Medical Limit |
|--------------|-------------------|---------------|
| Flower | 2 oz | 15 oz per 90 days (or 230 units / 8 oz) |
| Concentrates | 16 g | Equivalent to medical flower limit |
| Edible THC | 800 mg | Equivalent to medical flower limit |
| Plants | 6 mature + 6 immature per person (12 mature max per household) | Same as recreational |

## Penalty Tiers (Over-Limit Possession)

| Amount | Classification | Penalty |
|--------|---------------|---------|
| >2 oz - 8 oz | Misdemeanor | Up to 1 year jail; up to $1,000 fine |
| >8 oz | Felony | 1.5 years prison; up to $5,000 fine |
| >6 mature plants (first offense) | Felony | Up to 9 years prison; up to $10,000 fine |
| >6 mature plants (second offense) | Felony | Up to 18 years prison; up to $15,000 fine |

## Medical Program

- **Status:** Active since 2007; integrated with adult-use framework
- **Enabling law:** Lynn and Erin Compassionate Use Act (2007); expanded by HB 2 (2021)
- **Regulator:** New Mexico Department of Health - Medical Cannabis Program
- **Qualifying conditions:** Cancer, glaucoma, HIV/AIDS, hepatitis C, ALS, Crohn's, PTSD, chronic pain, severe nausea, seizures, multiple sclerosis, painful peripheral neuropathy, intractable nausea/vomiting, severe anorexia/cachexia, hepatitis C, spinal cord damage, and others
- **Patient registry:** Required
- **Home grow:** Same as recreational (6 mature + 6 immature per person, 12 mature max per household)
- **Tax:** Medical patients exempt from 12% recreational excise tax

## Home Cultivation

- **Recreational:** Up to 6 mature + 6 immature plants per person; maximum 12 mature plants per household
- **Medical:** Same limits as recreational
- **Requirements:** Must be in private residence, out of public view, not accessible to children
- **Important:** More than 6 mature plants is a felony; more than 12 mature plants per household carries enhanced penalties

## DUI / Impairment

- New Mexico prohibits driving while intoxicated (DWI) by cannabis.
- No per se THC limit is established; impairment determined by officer observation and testing.
- Penalties follow standard DWI statutes (NMSA 1978, Section 66-8-102).

## Employment Protections

- New Mexico provides employment protections for off-duty cannabis use.
- Employers may not discriminate against employees or applicants for lawful cannabis use outside work, with exceptions for safety-sensitive positions and federal contractors.
- Employers retain right to prohibit on-duty use and impairment.
- Cannabis consumption areas provide designated spaces for those without private residences.

## Federal Conflict

- Cannabis remains Schedule I under federal law.
- Interstate transport is prohibited even to neighboring legal states (CO, AZ).
- Federal property in New Mexico (national parks, military installations) prohibits cannabis.
