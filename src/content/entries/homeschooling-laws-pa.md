# Homeschooling Laws - Pennsylvania

## At a Glance

| Feature | Status |
|---------|--------|
| Legal Status | Yes - 3 pathways (home education program, private tutoring, umbrella/cyber charter) |
| Notification Required | Yes - notarized affidavit to superintendent by August 1 |
| Teacher Qualifications | High school diploma or equivalent required |
| Required Subjects | Mathematics, English, science, social studies, plus fire safety, civics, PA history |
| Testing Required | Yes - standardized tests in grades 3, 5, 8 |
| Instructional Hours | 180 days (900 hrs elementary; 990 hrs secondary) |
| Compulsory Ages | 8-17 |
| Distinctive Feature | Notarized affidavit required; portfolio + third-party evaluator; no parent self-evaluation; cyber charters tuition-free |

## Citation Layer

### 1. Legal Framework - Act 169

**Instrument:** Pennsylvania Public School Code Section 1327.1 (Act 169 of 1988, as amended)

**Live Link:** [https://www.legis.state.pa.us/cfdocs/legis/LI/consCheck.cfm?txtType=HTM&ttl=24&div=0&chpt=13&Sect=1327.1](https://www.legis.state.pa.us/cfdocs/legis/LI/consCheck.cfm?txtType=HTM&ttl=24&div=0&chpt=13&Sect=1327.1)

**Verbatim Quote (offset ~char 100-400):**
> "A home education program shall not be considered a nonpublic school under the provisions of this act."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending. Pennsylvania's homeschool framework has remained stable since Act 169.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Pennsylvania operates under Act 169, creating a structured but navigable homeschool system with clear procedural requirements.

### 2. Notarized Affidavit

**Instrument:** Pennsylvania Public School Code Section 1327.1(b)(1)

**Live Link:** [https://www.legis.state.pa.us/cfdocs/legis/LI/consCheck.cfm?txtType=HTM&ttl=24&div=0&chpt=13&Sect=1327.1](https://www.legis.state.pa.us/cfdocs/legis/LI/consCheck.cfm?txtType=HTM&ttl=24&div=0&chpt=13&Sect=1327.1)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "The notarized affidavit shall include: the name of the parent, guardian or legal custodian... the name and age of each child... a statement that the home education program will comply with the provisions of this section... and a certification that the adult persons residing within the home who are required to be cleared have not been convicted of the offenses specified."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Notarized affidavit due by August 1 annually. Must include: child's name/age, parent's address, educational objectives, immunization records, and criminal background clearance for adults in the home.

### 3. Portfolio and Evaluator

**Instrument:** Pennsylvania Public School Code Section 1327.1(e)

**Live Link:** [https://www.legis.state.pa.us/cfdocs/legis/LI/consCheck.cfm?txtType=HTM&ttl=24&div=0&chpt=13&Sect=1327.1](https://www.legis.state.pa.us/cfdocs/legis/LI/consCheck.cfm?txtType=HTM&ttl=24&div=0&chpt=13&Sect=1327.1)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "The portfolio shall be reviewed by an evaluator... The evaluator shall be a licensed psychologist, or a teacher certified by the Commonwealth with a minimum of two years of teaching experience."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Parents must maintain a portfolio (log of materials, work samples, test results) and submit it annually to a qualified third-party evaluator. Parents cannot self-evaluate.

### 4. Standardized Testing

**Instrument:** Pennsylvania Public School Code Section 1327.1(e)(2)

**Live Link:** [https://www.legis.state.pa.us/cfdocs/legis/LI/consCheck.cfm?txtType=HTM&ttl=24&div=0&chpt=13&Sect=1327.1](https://www.legis.state.pa.us/cfdocs/legis/LI/consCheck.cfm?txtType=HTM&ttl=24&div=0&chpt=13&Sect=1327.1)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "The portfolio shall include the results of nationally normed standardized achievement tests in grades three, five and eight."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Standardized testing required in grades 3, 5, and 8. Must be a nationally normed test or Pennsylvania state test.

### 5. Required Subjects

**Instrument:** Pennsylvania Public School Code Section 1327.1(c)

**Live Link:** [https://www.legis.state.pa.us/cfdocs/legis/LI/consCheck.cfm?txtType=HTM&ttl=24&div=0&chpt=13&Sect=1327.1](https://www.legis.state.pa.us/cfdocs/legis/LI/consCheck.cfm?txtType=HTM&ttl=24&div=0&chpt=13&Sect=1327.1)

**Verbatim Quote:**
> "English, to include language, literature, speech and composition; mathematics; science; geography; history of the United States and Pennsylvania; civics and government; safety education, including regular and continuous instruction in the dangers and prevention of fires."

**Repeal Check:** No repeal pending.

**Verdict:** ACTIVE. Required subjects include English, math, science, geography, US/PA history, civics/government, and fire safety education.

## Distinctive State Features

1. **Notarized Affidavit:** Pennsylvania is one of the few states requiring notarization of the annual homeschool affidavit.

2. **Third-Party Evaluator:** Parents cannot self-evaluate. A licensed psychologist or PA-certified teacher with 2+ years experience must review the portfolio annually.

3. **Criminal Background Clearance:** Adults residing in the home must be cleared of certain criminal offenses - a requirement unique to Pennsylvania.

4. **No Parent Self-Evaluation:** The evaluator must be independent; parents cannot evaluate their own children's progress.

5. **Cyber Charters Tuition-Free:** Pennsylvania offers tuition-free cyber charter schools as an alternative to traditional homeschooling.
