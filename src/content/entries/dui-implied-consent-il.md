<div class="quip">Illinois: where deep-dish pizza will slow you down, but a DUI refusal will stop you for 12 months.</div>

<div class="settled">
<b>The stuff that is actually true in Illinois</b>
<ul>
<li><strong>You already consented to chemical testing.</strong> 625 ILCS 5/11-501.1. By driving in Illinois, you are deemed to have consented to blood, breath, urine, or other bodily substance testing if lawfully arrested for DUI. The officer's agency designates which test.</li>
<li><strong>First refusal = 12-month suspension. Second = 36 months.</strong> 625 ILCS 5/6-208.1. Statutory summary suspension takes effect 46 days after notice. This is administrative, separate from criminal case.</li>
<li><strong>The officer must warn you of the consequences.</strong> 625 ILCS 5/11-501.1(c). Before a refusal is valid, the officer must warn you that refusal will result in suspension AND that a BAC of 0.08+ or drug detection will also result in suspension.</li>
<li><strong>Unconscious = no right to refuse.</strong> 625 ILCS 5/11-501.1(d). If you are unconscious or incapable of refusal, you are deemed not to have withdrawn consent. Blood can be drawn without a warrant.</li>
<li><strong>You can record the stop.</strong> Illinois is a two-party consent state, but recording police in public is generally protected. Do not interfere.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="varies">
<b>The grey area where people get themselves arrested</b>
<ul>
<li><strong>"I want to speak to an attorney first."</strong> You have no right to an attorney before deciding whether to submit to chemical testing. The officer does not have to wait.</li>
<li><strong>"I'll take a blood test instead of breath."</strong> The officer's agency designates the test, not you. You can request a different test, but they are not required to honor it.</li>
<li><strong>"The officer didn't have probable cause."</strong> If the arrest was unlawful, the suspension can be challenged by filing a petition to rescind within 90 days. This is a separate civil proceeding from the criminal case.</li>
<li><strong>"I have a medical marijuana card."</strong> Medical marijuana card holders have given implied consent to field sobriety tests under 625 ILCS 5/11-501.9. Refusal of FSTs = 12-month suspension. Failing FSTs = 6-month suspension.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="wrongbox">
<b>Things TikTok told you that are wrong</b>
<ul>
<li>"If you refuse, they have no case." Wrong. Refusal triggers automatic suspension AND can be used as evidence of guilt. The prosecutor can still convict on officer observations and field sobriety tests.</li>
<li>"You have a right to an independent test before the state's test." Wrong. You are entitled to an independent test AFTER submitting to the state's test, not before.</li>
<li>"The suspension only applies if you're convicted." Wrong. The statutory summary suspension is administrative and happens regardless of the criminal case outcome.</li>
<li>"You can get an MDDP right away." Wrong. You must serve a portion of the suspension before becoming eligible for a Monitoring Device Driving Permit.</li>
</ul>
</div>
