<div class="quip">Wisconsin: where the cheese is curdled and your license is revoked for a year if you refuse the breath test.</div>

<div class="settled">
<b>The stuff that is actually true in Wisconsin</b>
<ul>
<li><strong>You already consented to chemical testing.</strong> Wis. Stat. 343.305(2). By driving in Wisconsin, you are deemed to have consented to breath, blood, or urine testing if lawfully arrested for OWI. The officer chooses which test.</li>
<li><strong>First refusal = 1-year revocation + ignition interlock.</strong> Wis. Stat. 343.305(10). First refusal = 1-year revocation + 1-year ignition interlock requirement. Can apply for occupational license 30 days after revocation begins.</li>
<li><strong>The officer must read the 'Informing the Accused' form.</strong> Wis. Stat. 343.305(4). The officer must inform you of the consequences of taking and refusing the test, your right to an alternative test, and that refusal can be used against you in court.</li>
<li><strong>Unconscious = no right to refuse.</strong> Wis. Stat. 343.305(3)(b). If you are unconscious or incapable of withdrawing consent, you are presumed not to have withdrawn it. Blood can be drawn without a warrant.</li>
<li><strong>Three-hour rule.</strong> Wis. Stat. 885.235(1m). Sample must be taken within 3 hours of the event for admissibility under the per se statute.</li>
<li><strong>You can record the stop.</strong> Wisconsin is a one-party consent state. You may record your own interactions with police without informing them.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="varies">
<b>The grey area where people get themselves arrested</b>
<ul>
<li><strong>"I want to speak to an attorney first."</strong> You have no right to an attorney before deciding whether to submit to chemical testing. The officer does not have to wait.</li>
<li><strong>"I'll take a blood test instead of breath."</strong> The officer chooses the test, not you. You can request blood, but the officer is not required to honor it.</li>
<li><strong>"The officer didn't read me the Informing the Accused form."</strong> If the officer fails to read the form correctly or omits sections, the refusal may be invalid.</li>
<li><strong>"I wasn't driving, I was sleeping in the car."</strong> Wisconsin uses 'operating' standard. If you have the keys and the ability to operate the vehicle, you can be arrested for OWI.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="wrongbox">
<b>Things TikTok told you that are wrong</b>
<ul>
<li>"If you refuse, they have no case." Wrong. Refusal triggers automatic revocation AND can be used as evidence of guilt. The prosecutor can still convict on officer observations and field sobriety tests.</li>
<li>"You have a right to an attorney before deciding." Wrong. You have no right to counsel before the implied consent decision.</li>
<li>"The revocation only applies if you're convicted." Wrong. The revocation is administrative and happens regardless of the criminal case outcome.</li>
<li>"You can get an occupational license right away." Wrong. You must wait 30 days after revocation begins before applying for an occupational license.</li>
</ul>
</div>
