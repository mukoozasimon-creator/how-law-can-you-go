## The Short Answer

In Oklahoma, your landlord must give you a **5-day written notice** to pay rent or quit for nonpayment (41 O.S. § 131(B)). For lease violations, the landlord must give a **15-day notice** to cure or quit (§ 131(A)). To end a month-to-month tenancy, the landlord must give **30 days' written notice** (§ 111). Oklahoma has no just-cause requirement, no rent control, and no statewide security deposit cap. Self-help eviction is illegal.

### 41 O.S. § 131(A) - 15-Day Notice for Lease Violations

> "If there is a material noncompliance by the tenant with the rental agreement or a noncompliance with Section 118 materially affecting health and safety, the landlord may deliver a written notice to the tenant specifying the acts and omissions constituting the breach and that the rental agreement will terminate upon a date not less than fifteen days after receipt of the notice, if the breach is not remedied in fifteen days." cite🛠web_search:42#5:~:text=If there is a material noncompliance by the tenant with the rental agreement... the landlord may deliver a written notice to the tenant specifying the acts and omissions constituting the breach and that the rental agreement will terminate upon a date not less than fifteen days after receipt of the notice, if the breach is not remedied in fifteen days.

### 41 O.S. § 111 - Termination of Month-to-Month Tenancy

> "The landlord or the tenant may terminate a month-to-month tenancy by a written notice given to the other at least thirty days before the termination date specified in the notice." cite🛠web_search:42#5:~:text=The landlord or the tenant may terminate a month-to-month tenancy by a written notice given to the other at least thirty days before the termination date specified in the notice.

### 41 O.S. § 115 - Security Deposits

**Key rules:**
- No statewide cap on security deposit amount.
- Return deadline: **45 days** after move-out with itemized statement.
- Must provide **move-in inspection checklist**.
- Wrongful withholding: Tenant can recover **actual damages** plus attorney's fees.

**Live link:** https://www.oscn.net/applications/oscn/DeliverDocument.asp?CiteID=452166
**Repeal check:** Active. No repeal pending as of August 2026.
**Verdict:** 45-day return. Move-in checklist required.

### 41 O.S. § 123 - Self-Help Eviction Prohibited

## Important Notes

- **No just-cause requirement:** Oklahoma landlords can terminate month-to-month tenancies for any reason with proper notice.
- **No rent control:** Oklahoma preempts local rent control.
- **Eviction timeline:** Hearings typically within 2-3 weeks of filing. Writ of possession issued after judgment.
- **Service methods:** Personal delivery or certified/registered mail.
- **Recording:** Oklahoma is a one-party consent state for recordings (21 O.S. § 1762). You may record conversations you are a party to without the other person's consent.

## Cross-State Notes

- **vs. Texas:** Both are landlord-friendly with no just-cause or rent control, but OK has longer lease violation notice (15 days vs. TX's 3 days).
- **vs. Kansas:** Both have similar landlord-friendly frameworks with no just-cause or rent control.
- **vs. Colorado:** OK is far more landlord-friendly with no just-cause requirement and no rent control.
