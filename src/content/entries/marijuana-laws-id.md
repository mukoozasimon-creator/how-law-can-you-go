# Marijuana Laws - Idaho

## Verdict

Idaho maintains some of the strictest cannabis laws in the United States. Marijuana is fully illegal for recreational and medical purposes. The state does not have a medical marijuana program. Hemp-derived CBD products with no more than 0.3% THC are permitted under federal law. Possession of any amount of marijuana is a criminal offense. Idaho does not recognize out-of-state medical cannabis cards. Legislative efforts to establish even limited medical access have consistently failed. H0771 (2026) establishes new regulations for retail sale of industrial hemp products, allowing only naturally derived CBD in consumable form.

## Medical Program

- **Status:** No medical marijuana program exists.
- Idaho is one of approximately 10 states with no medical cannabis program of any kind.
- The state does not recognize out-of-state medical cannabis cards.
- Legislative efforts to establish medical access have failed repeatedly.

## Home Cultivation

- **Status:** Illegal for all purposes.
- Cultivation of any cannabis plant is treated as manufacturing and carries felony penalties.

## DUI / Impairment

- Idaho prohibits operating a motor vehicle under the influence of cannabis.
- DUI penalties are severe and mirror alcohol DUI under Idaho Code Section 18-8004.
- First offense: Up to 6 months jail, $1,000 fine, license suspension 90-180 days.

## Employment Protections

- No employment protections for cannabis use exist.
- Employers may drug test and terminate for any cannabis use.

## Federal Conflict

- Idaho law aligns with federal Schedule I classification.
- No state-federal conflict exists because state law does not authorize any form of cannabis beyond hemp-derived CBD (0.3% THC).
