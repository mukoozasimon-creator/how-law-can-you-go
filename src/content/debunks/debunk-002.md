<div class="quip">The safety advice that could put you in prison.</div>

<div class="wrongbox">
<b>What people say</b>
<p>"If you're being pulled over and it's dark or isolated, you have the right to continue to a well-lit public place. Call 911 to confirm."</p>
</div>

<div class="settled">
<b>What we found</b>
<p>We read the US Code, the Code of Federal Regulations, and the vehicle codes of all 50 states. <strong>No statute creates a right to continue driving to a lit area.</strong></p>
<p>What <em>does</em> exist: Washington and Virginia give you an <strong>affirmative defence at trial</strong> if you reasonably believed the stop was unsafe. That is not a right at the roadside. It is a defence your lawyer can raise after you have been arrested, handcuffed, and charged.</p>
<p>In Florida, wilful failure to stop is a <strong>third-degree felony</strong> from the first second. The safety advice is a felony exposure that the safety advice does not mention.</p>
</div>
